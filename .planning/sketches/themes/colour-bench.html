Exports every theme, not just the one on screen — one ramp each into the :root ramp block, then one :root.theme-<id> block each. Paste both over the matching sections of app/globals.css. Adding or renaming a theme also needs its entry in THEMES in lib/theme.ts — that list is what the settings menu reads.
