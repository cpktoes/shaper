# Sketch 022: A GUIDE on every page — ROCKER first

## Design Question
The founder's verdict on sketch 021's GUIDE tab (2026-11-08): *"The Guide looks awesome. Love everything about it. I think
every page needs a guide like this. Super useful on what each parameter does to/for the board. Would also be nice to add a
'how to shape' note too."* The todo `2026-11-08-a-guide-tab-on-every-page.md` says what each page's guide would cover and
asks for a sketch first — one page, to settle the shape of a control's card against a cut's — then the pages together.

This is round one: **ROCKER**, in 021's dress. GUIDE joins the page's own tabs — VIEWER · PLANING · DATASHEET on a computer,
VIEWER · TOP · PLANING · DATA on a phone (the live page's strip since quick 261019-gqo; the founder's brief said VIEWER · TOP ·
DATA, which is the phone's without PLANING) — as the last tab. Fourteen cards in the sidebar's own order, each named as the
page labels the control:

- **Inputs** — Center Thickness; Nose Tip · Tail Tip.
- **Blank** — Blank (the PICK A BLANK / CHANGE BLANK button); Placement; Deck Skin; Customize Rocker.
- **Rocker** (read off the blank, hand-set until you pick one) — Nose Rocker; Tail Rocker.
- **Foil** — Pin tips to · Deck and · Bottom (one card per option, like 021's five vees); Nose Thinning Starts; Tail
  Thinning Starts; Nose @ 12" · Tail @ 12" (the Advanced fold's fine-tunes); Fine tune from.

A card is 021's card: the name, the cartoon, a sentence on what the control is and looks like (ink), one on what it does to
the board or the ride (muted), and under a dashed rule a *How to shape* note. Two things are new because a control is not a
cut:

- **The board's own value sits beside the name** — `2 1/4"`, `centered`, `1/8"`, `Natural rocker`, `12" · Automatic`, `No
  tweak`, `Deck` — through the page's own formatters (marks in sixteenths or whole millimetres, a thinning start as a dim in
  centimetres), so the guide also reads the board. A named choice's active option is tagged *On this board* as in 021.
- **The cartoon is the page's own kind of drawing with the one setting pushed.** The board the shaper has open from the side,
  nose left, in its blank with the foam to come off shaded, on the dashed line the rocker is measured up from, the thinning
  marks across it; then, under it, the part of the board the control touches, closer (a dash-dot box on the whole board says
  where — one window for a centre or a tip, a pair for the two tips or the two 12" stations), with the setting pushed by a
  stated amount drawn in muted dashes beside the board as it is, and the number the control sets dimensioned the way the
  VIEWER reads it. The pushes are real: every dashed profile is built by the app's own `designSideProfile` with that one
  field changed — the centre +1/2", both tips +3/8", the board slid to the slider's nose end, a 1/2" deck skin, each Pin tips
  to, a thinning start moved to 24" (or 8" past Automatic), both 12" fine-tunes +1/4", a tail 12" −1/4" taken off the Deck
  and off the Bottom, the blank bent +1" at the nose and at the tail from the centre (Customize Rocker's own maths), and a
  maker's bend `+1/2"N18"`. Nothing is drawn freehand.

Everything else on the page is a picture of today's ROCKER from the same real numbers so the GUIDE can be judged in company:
the sidebar's real labels and values (inert here), the VIEWER with its two rails (cards for the three thicknesses the
shaper sets, readings where the blank derives), TOP on a phone, PLANING and DATASHEET from the one side profile.

**Every sentence and shop note is a first draft from shaping lore, for the founder's pen.** The `GUIDE` and `HOW` tables in
the template are his to correct.

## Variants
None in round one — the question is the shape of a control's card. Round two, after the founder's rulings here, is TEMPLATE,
RAILS, VOLUME and FINS the same way, as pages in the same sketch.

## What to Look For
- **The card's shape.** Whole board, then the part the control touches closer with the pushed setting in dashes — does that
  say what a control does the way 021's shade and section said what a cut is? Is the push the right size to read (an inch of
  rocker, half an inch of centre), and is "dashed · +1" nose rocker" the right way to say it?
- **The value beside the name.** Useful (the guide reads your board) or noise? And *On this board* only for a choice's
  active option.
- **One card or two.** Pin tips to is two cards (one per option, 021's way); Nose Tip · Tail Tip and the two 12" fine-tunes
  are one card each with a pair of windows; Nose and Tail Thinning Starts are two cards. Which way round should pairs go?
- **The groups.** Inputs · Blank · Rocker · Foil follow the sidebar, with Rocker a group of its own though the sidebar has no
  such section once a blank is picked.
- **Five tabs on a phone.** VIEWER · TOP · PLANING · DATA · GUIDE fit across an iPhone 14 with the board's name cut to
  `S…`; is GUIDE worth that, or should it live somewhere else on a phone?
- **The sentences and the shop notes** — right, wrong, too long; the founder asked for one or two sentences.
- Small print: the Longboard here sits in the 9'3"Y the Phase 16 fixture recorded it on (the live preset opens in the 9'4"B);
  the hand-set state (no blank) is not shown — every board here has one — so the Rocker group's cards read the blank's rocker.

## Checked
Headless Chromium at 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844 (iPhone 14, touch), and the phone turned to 844 × 390: no errors on any theme,
units or board. A computer shows VIEWER · PLANING · DATASHEET · GUIDE with the full words and the board lying flat; the VIEWER
draws the blank, the board, two thinning marks and three thickness cards; the sidebar carries the page's real labels with
the board's numbers (`Center Thickness — 2 1/4"`, `Placement — centered`, `Deck Skin — 1/8"`, `Nose Thinning Starts — 12"`);
PLANING has five stations and DATASHEET its nine rows with the three typed thicknesses. GUIDE has fourteen cards under
Inputs · Blank · Rocker · Foil, three across, scrolling inside the folder; every card has the whole board in its blank and a
detail window, a dimension, two sentences and a How to shape note; eleven carry a pushed profile in dashes (all but Blank and
the two Pin tips to); the Shortboard's own values sit beside the names and only Pin tips to · Deck reads On this board; the
Fish redraws every card on its own blank with its own centre; the longer blank's Placement card slides the board 2 3/8" to
the nose. Metric reads marks in whole millimetres, the thinning start in centimetres and the 12" cards as 30.5 cm. The phone
frame on a computer and a real phone width both give the phone stack with VIEWER · TOP · PLANING · DATA · GUIDE on one line,
the board standing nose up, TOP drawing the blank from above, GUIDE one card across with nothing running wider than its card,
and the page never scrolling as a whole; sideways the desktop shell and tabs return.
