<objective>
Stop the rail curve from cutting into the 2in x 2in scale-check square on page 1 of the Full Sized
Template.

Page 1 is the nose page. Its scale square is pinned to the top-outward corner of the page's
alignment box, on the belief written into the code that it is "a corner the curve never reaches".
The previous quick task (260903-fqv) measured that corner properly and found the belief is false on
extreme boards: on the widest longboard the curve runs 5.8mm (Letter) / 11.7mm (A4) into the
square's own footprint, and on a longboard at 100% nose fullness 1.8mm / 7.7mm. On the plain
longboard preset it is only 2.5mm clear at A4.

So: keep the corner whenever the curve genuinely does not touch the square's footprint — which is
every board the app ships — and on the boards where it does, move the square inside the outline on
page 1, stacked 4mm under the how-to instruction box, 4mm off the stringer, with at least 4mm of
daylight to the curve.

Purpose: that square is how a shaper checks the print came out at 100% scale before taping a stack
of pages together and cutting foam to it. A square with a heavy black rail curve slicing its corner
off cannot be measured, and worse, it gets taken off with the offcut. It also has to stay exactly
2in — a scale check that is not 2in tells the shaper nothing.

Output: one new pure placement function in `lib/geometry/template.ts` alongside the how-to box's,
one resolved page-1 furniture bundle in the PDF builder feeding both the drawing and the tests, one
plain-English how-to line that is true wherever the square sits, and derived tests on both sides —
with the existing frozen pins proving nothing else in the tile-grid math moved.
</objective>

<design_decision>
These are locked by the orchestrator's CONTEXT.md. Implement them; do not re-litigate them.

1. **The corner is kept unless the curve actually touches the square (the 0mm rule).** The square
   keeps today's corner spot whenever the outline's MAXIMUM half-width anywhere over the square's
   own footprint stays at or inboard of the footprint's curve-side edge. Clearance of zero or more
   keeps the corner; below zero moves it. This is deliberately a DIFFERENT number from the 4mm
   name-block rule, and gets its own exported constant with a doc comment saying its value is a
   founder decision rather than a formula.

2. **The fallback is inside the outline, on page 1, stacked under the lowest interior furniture.**
   The square's top edge sits `NAME_BOX_CLEARANCE_MM` (4mm) below the bottom edge of the lowest
   piece of interior furniture already on page 1 — the how-to box's bottom when the how-to box is
   interior, otherwise the name block's bottom. Left edge 4mm off the stringer, and at least 4mm of
   daylight to the curve over the footprint's whole height. Same 4mm rule, same
   `clearance + width + clearance` required-half-width shape the how-to box's interior branch
   already uses. The square never leaves page 1.

3. **The square stays exactly 2in x 2in.** In both placements, on both paper sizes, on every board.
   Nothing here shrinks it, and nothing widens its furniture rect to chase the caption.

4. **One how-to wording for every board.** Instruction line 2 becomes exactly:
   `Measure the 2" x 2" square. It should be exactly 2" x 2".` on every board. Lines 1, 3 and 4 are
   unchanged, and so is the square's own caption.

5. **The decision is geometry, the conversion is drawing** (CLAUDE.md Rule 1). The new function
   lives in `lib/geometry/template.ts` right after `howToBoxPlacement`, is pure and exported, and
   answers in the board's own station/half-width frame. The builder converts through the existing
   `stationToY` / `halfWidthToX` and nothing else.

6. **Additive only in `lib/geometry/template.ts`.** `nameBlockPlacement`, `howToBoxPlacement`,
   `templatePageBoxes`, `computeTemplateLayout`, `minHalfWidthOverStationSpan`,
   `minMaxHalfWidthOverStationSpanFine` and every strip function keep their current behaviour
   byte-for-byte. That is why all four frozen characterisation pins stay green WITHOUT being
   edited. **There is no authorised digest recapture in this task.** A red pin means this task broke
   something: find the cause and fix the cause.

7. **The Paper Saver strip is not touched.** Its own `stripFurniture` scale square, and the frozen
   literal pin that guards it, are out of scope entirely.

8. **The how-to box's own outboard candidate is unchanged**, and so is `computeHowToBoxPlacement`'s
   construction of it. It still measures down from the alignment box's top edge past a 2in square
   and its gap even on a board where the square has moved away — which is harmless, because on
   every such board the how-to box is already interior.

Rejected and closed, with reasons, so they are not reopened: the 4mm trigger (the founder chose 0mm
precisely so the plain longboard keeps its corner square at A4, where 4mm would have moved it);
"always inside the outline" (on the shortboard the stack of name block, instructions and square runs
34mm past page 1's floor); the second-column nose page in either corner (everything stays on page
1); sliding the square along page 1's outboard edge (it is already flush with the top, and anything
further inboard is reached sooner); shrinking the square (never).
</design_decision>
