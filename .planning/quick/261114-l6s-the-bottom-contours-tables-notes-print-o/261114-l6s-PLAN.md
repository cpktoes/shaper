# The tables' notes onto the next sheet

## Goal

17-23 made the 9'0" Longboard's printed tables sheet hold all three of DATA's reading notes by setting the rows 2 dots
closer. From 9'7" to 10'0" with channels the tight rows are still 25 to 30 dots short, so two notes dropped — the one
that explains the channel columns among them. The founder's pick: when the notes do not fit under the tables, they print
at the top of the following page instead of dropping.

## Tasks

1. **The fit rule** (`bottom-sheet-fit.ts`): on the tables' own sheet, every note at today's rows; else every note with the
   rows tight (17-23, unchanged for 9'0"–9'6"); else every note on a sheet of its own after the tables
   (`notesOnOwnSheet`), the tables keeping today's rows unless they need the tight ones alone. `sheets` counts it;
   `bottomSheetSpare` reports the notes' sheet's room.
2. **The sheet and the form**: `BottomTablesSheet part="notes"` draws the notes alone; the order form prints it as page 5,
   titled "Bottom Contours · Reading the Tables"; the on-screen sentence already says "three Bottom Contours pages".
3. **Tests**: the fit test widened to the Longboard at every inch from 9'0" to 10'0" on the 96 setups in both systems —
   every note printed once, every sheet roomy, nothing up to 9'6" moving, the rule by its own definition; the 11'6"
   boundary test updated (its notes now print instead of dropping).

## Verify

- `bottom-sheet-fit.test.ts` and the order-form tests, `tsc`, lint green.
- On the dev server: a 9'8" Longboard with channels shows five sheets on SUMMARY, the fifth the three notes.
