- **D-V1 (the founder, 2026-11-02, relayed by the orchestrator after plan 01's litres table): "Switch to the finished
  curve."** Volume integrates the finished (blended) rail instead of the cut, faceted profile. This supersedes R5's "keep
  volume on the cut, faceted profile" (R5 asked the founder first; this is the answer). BRIEF section 4b holds the
  founder's other answers (PC15, PC16, PC21, PC4/PC5 stand).
- From the BRIEF: R2 pictures before the commits land (before FIRST, from the unchanged tree); R3 nothing merges, pushes,

  relations, never a typed litre); Rule 2 (units through `units.ts`).

**Planner's choices (each overrulable):**
- PV1 — Exact, not sampled. Each blend replaces two straight runs A-V and V-B with the parabola from A to B whose
  control point is the corner V; the foam between them is exactly one third of triangle A-V-B (a parabola's own property,
  Archimedes). So one rail's blend area is the sum of those thirds over the finished rail's `quad` steps, and a
  station's finished area is its cut area (unchanged, proven equal to the cut foam — plan 01 M7) minus twice that (both
  rails). Measured (V1): equal to the curve's exact area by Green's theorem to 7.3e-12 mm²; sampling converges to it
  as 1/n², so the module's own 17-point samples would sit about 1.5-3.9 mm² per station (0.002-0.004 L per board) off.
- PV2 — One basis everywhere. The quoted litres come from `computeCrossSectionVolume`: `summarizeDesignWith` (preset
  cards, the Board Rack, summary lines) and the design store (the VOLUME screen's Estimated Volume, the top bar, the
  Summary and the printed order form, which reads the store's quoted litres). VOLUME's working rows (Tail, Center and
  Nose Cross-Section Thickness, Length-Weighted thickness) come from `computeVolume` fed by `deriveRailValues`, which
  both `summarizeDesignWith` and the store call. Both paths take the finished rail here, through one helper, so they
  can never disagree.
- PV3 — The faceted path stays only as the base of the exact sum (`buildRailProfile`, the cut area M7 proves) and in the
  prototype estimator's golden tests (absent blend areas read 0 — the prototype had no finished rail). Nothing quoted
  reads it. The RAILS drawing keeps drawing the cut bands, because those are the cuts a shaper makes.
- PV4 — The left edge volume blends from is each section's own plot edge, -(Domed Deck Band + 2") — what
  `railPlotBounds(result)` gives. Measured (V5): moving it twice as far changes no blend area by more than 1.1e-13 mm² on
  the four presets and the first board, so volume counts the curve RAILS draws.
- PV5 — `VolumeRailValues` gains optional `noseBlendArea`, `centerBlendArea` and `tailBlendArea` (square millimetres,
  one rail); absent reads 0, so the prototype-volume golden tests stay as they are; `deriveRailValues` always fills them,
  and a test says so. `CrossSectionVolumeResult` gains `stationBlendAreas` (both rails, per station) beside
  `stationAreas`, for auditing, as `stationAreas` already is.
- PV6 — No special case for a hard edge or Single Tuck: a hard edge's bottom corner is a straight step (plan 01 PC13), so
  it adds no third; Single Tuck's one tuck is a band like any other.
- PV7 — VOLUME's method line becomes "From the board's own finished cross-sections along its length." so the disclosure
  (D-13) says what is counted. ASK THE FOUNDER only if they want different words.
</decisions>
