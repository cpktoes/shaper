<objective>
Two of the founder's rulings after looking at plan 02 (BRIEF §4c, R17 and R18). Deck Mark 2 and Bottom Tuck 2 lose
their sliders: "Always in the middle is great" — each is now always half its parent mark. And each rail's Advanced menu
gains a Domed Deck Band slider, right under Deck Mark 3, that moves where the deck reaches full thickness up to 2" either
way, but can never land on Deck Mark 3.

What a shaper sees: the Advanced menu holds six nudge sliders instead of seven. On a domed deck, moving the new slider
changes how steeply the deck falls toward the rail, and Band 1's and Band 2's deck ends move with it. On a flat deck the
only things that move are the dome's green dot and the end of its line. When the slider would bring the dome onto Deck
Mark 3, the dome stops 1/16" beyond it. No board changes until a shaper moves the slider: every saved board and preset
draws, reads and counts exactly as before.

Purpose: the founder's words, 2026-11-02: "Deck mark 2 and Bottom Tuck 2 don't need sliders. Always in the middle is
great. I would like to add an advanced slider for the domed deck mark though." Then, after being shown that an inch's
gap would move boards nobody had nudged: "I was afraid of that. Let's keep the ±2" slider, but never let it equal Deck
Mark 3." (BRIEF §4c, R18 as amended).

Output: the rules, storage, presets and their tests; the sidebar and the store; the browser spec amended with a new
dome test; before and after pictures; the SUMMARY. The golden and the preset-figures record are unchanged and stay as
plan 01 and plan 03 left them (M15, M16).
</objective>
