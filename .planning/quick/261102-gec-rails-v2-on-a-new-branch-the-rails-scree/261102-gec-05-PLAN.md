**PC35 — THE R23 RULE: the founder's pick, (d) "one curve over both corners", made continuous.** The founder answered
on 2026-11-02, after the candidate pictures: (d), "with the hand-over made continuous first". Task 1's CHOSEN line reads
`D (continuous)`.

*The rule, for a shaper.* Where Deck Mark 3 and the dome mark sit close together, closer than half of Band 2, the rail
rolls off the deck in ONE smooth curve. It starts on the deck beyond the dome mark and runs over both marks to the
middle of Band 2, with no bend at all where it leaves a flat deck. As the dome mark moves further out, that one curve
slides steadily into the separate blends: on a domed deck, a short blend at the dome mark and one at Deck Mark 3 that
bend alike where they meet; on a flat deck, today's blend. The slide is complete once the run between the marks is as
long as all of Band 2. The line never jumps as the Domed Deck Band slider moves. Everything from Band 2's middle
over the rail to the bottom is as it was.

*The maths* (inches, in the module's inch core). Words used:
- V is Deck Mark 3's corner, D the dome mark, and A Band 2's middle (the midpoint of V and the Deck Mark 2 corner).
- L = |A - V| (half of Band 2), and m = |D - V| (the run, along a flat deck or along the dome band).
- The weight is w = clamp((m - L) / L, 0, 1).
- Shapes are written as Bézier control points. "raise(P0, C, P2)" is the parabola P0-C-P2 written as a cubic:
  [P0, P0 + 2/3·(C - P0), P2 + 2/3·(C - P2), P2]. "lerp" is point-by-point.

The four cases:
- **w = 0 (a short run) — (d):** one cubic Kd = [Fd, D, V, A], where Fd lies on the full-thickness deck beyond D by
  min(L, 2", the drawing's edge). On a flat deck D sits on the deck between V and Fd.
- **w = 1 (a long run):**
  - Domed: (b), the matched blends, exactly as drawn: the dome parabola [Fb, D, Q1], a straight stretch Q1 -> Q2 (zero
    length unless rule 12's 2" cap binds), then the Deck Mark 3 parabola [Q2, V, A].
  - Flat: today's parabola [S, V, A], where S = V + L along the deck.
  - These are emitted as their own `quad` steps, so they equal (b) and today's curve bit for bit.
- **0 < w < 1, flat deck:** ONE cubic, lerp(Kf, raise(S, V, A), w). Kf = [V + (L + min(L, 2"))·deck, V + L·deck, V, A]
  is (d)'s shape frozen where the run equals L, so w = 0 joins Kd exactly.
- **0 < w < 1, domed deck:** split Kd at τ, the one parameter where its tangent runs along the dome band:
  τ = 1 / (1 + sqrt(-c2/c0)), with c0 = (D - Fd) × û and c2 = (A - V) × û (û the dome band's direction). The middle term
  vanishes because V - D runs along û. The split gives [Fd, P01, P012, J] and [J, P123, P23, A]. Then the curve is:
  - cubic lerp([Fd, P01, P012, J], raise(Fb, D, Q1), w);
  - a straight step from lerp(J, Q1, w) to lerp(J, Q2, w);
  - cubic lerp([J, P123, P23, A], raise(Q2, V, A), w).

Why it holds by construction:
- **Inside the cuts:** every control point is a blend of points on the cut outline, so it lies in the (convex) foam,
  and each cubic lies inside its control points.
- **Tangent at the ends:** the first two points of the first piece stay on the deck line, and the last two of the last
  piece on Band 2's line.
- **Smooth at the join:** the join's three points stay on a line along the dome band in both end shapes, so the curve
  is G1 at every w.
- **No jumps:** w and every control point move continuously with the dome. τ, Q1, Q2 and Fb are continuous. At m = L
  the domed and flat forms meet Kd. As a domed deck flattens, (b)'s dome blend shrinks to nothing and the forms meet
  today's.

*The area rule.* The finished area is Green's theorem on the curve, in closed form per step:
- a line: (P0×P1) / 2;
- a parabola: (2·P0×C + 2·C×P2 + P0×P2) / 6;
- a cubic: (6·P0×P1 + 3·P0×P2 + P0×P3 + 3·P1×P2 + 3·P1×P3 + 6·P2×P3) / 20.

`railBlendArea` becomes the cut outline's own area (the chain closed along the drawing's edge, shoelace) less that.
The per-piece cross-check is the polygon through every control point, less a third of each parabola's triangle and
|7·(a×b) + 4·(b×c) - 3·(a×c)| / 20 for each cubic (a, b, c from its first point). It agrees with Green's theorem to
7.3e-12 mm² (M27). For parabolas it is plan 03's third, unchanged.
