# Quick 261106-ni6: the room's colours follow the theme, and the Room icon has three steps

The founder's decisions, 2026-11-06, before go-live 2. They are locked. Users are shapers, not developers.

## What he decided

1. **Theme room by default.** The 3D room's walls and ceiling take the theme's deepened colour. The accent's hue held at
   the depth of today's blue, so white foam and white logos stay as clear as they are now:

   | Theme | Wall | Ceiling |
   |---|---|---|
   | Daylight | #123630 | #081715 |
   | Chalk | #133445 | #08161d |
   | Slate | #0f3446 | #07161e |
   | Phosphor | #0f380f | #061806 |

   The floor, shelves, strips and racks stay as they are. A theme change on the 3D screen recolours the room live.
2. **The Room icon has three steps.** It starts on the theme room. First click: today's bright blue room (wall #0a2f66,
   ceiling #04142e). Second click: no room. Third click: back to the theme room. It is not saved, as today.
3. **Logos.** In both rooms, all four logos show: the circle on both side walls and the line logo on both end walls. With
   no room, only the one line logo on the back wall stays (the wall the first view faces, x = -L/2): "with no room, just
   keep the one linear logo in the back." The circles and the second line logo go with the room.
4. **Walk Around** is off only with no room. Going between the theme room and the blue room moves nothing: not the board,
   not the viewer, not the crop.
5. **The board's shading, the litres and the files do not change.** Measured on previews in a scratch copy: the middle of
   the board differed by 0 levels between the blue room and the deep Daylight room.

This reverses D-43's "the room's colours are the same in every theme". Update the doc comments that say so:
`BAY_PAINT` in `lib/geometry/shaping-bay.ts` and `buildBay` in `components/3d/board-scene.ts`.

## How the code stands (tip 5d47f211 on board-3d)

- `components/3d/board-scene.ts` is the only file that imports three. `buildBay` paints the four walls with `paint(BAY_PAINT.wall, …)`
  (a ShaderMaterial with an `albedo` uniform) and the ceiling with a MeshBasicMaterial. `bay.room` is the Group the Room
  switch hides in `setRoom(on)`. `bay.logos` is a second Group, beside the room, holding every logo today. It must now hold
  only the back wall's line logo; the rest belong in `room`. `logo()` shares one material per picture file across places, so the
  split needs care: two meshes of one material may sit in different groups, or `places` can carry the parent.
- The dev-only hook `__shaper3dBareRoom` starts the screen with no room and hides `bay.logos` in the scene: four spec files
  (three-d-show, three-d-zoom, three-d-wire, three-d-screen) find the board by what differs from the plain ground. Keep that.
- `components/3d/three-d-screen.tsx`: `roomPick` (boolean | null), `roomOn = roomPick ?? !bareRoom`, `dragMode`, the effect near line
  1040 that runs on a change of `roomOn` (settling, free spin) and returns early when nothing changed, `data-bay`, and the
  `MutationObserver` near line 500 that re-reads `readSceneColours()` and calls `setColours` on a theme change.
- `components/3d/three-d-show-buttons.tsx`: the Room button is `pressed={room}`, `onRoom`. Copy is in `components/3d/three-d-copy.ts`
  (`room`, `roomTip`).
- Recommendation, not a ruling: put the two room colours in `app/globals.css` as tokens per theme and read them through
  `readSceneColours`, so a theme change and the `system` theme work with no new code path. globals.css has three layers
  (bare `:root`, the `prefers-color-scheme: dark` block, and `:root.theme-*`), so a token must land in each. Python `str.replace`
  anchors there are indentation-sensitive: count the result per block. Keep `@theme` static.
- A pure next-step function belongs in `lib/geometry/shaping-bay.ts` with a unit test (Rule 1), with the blue room's colours in
  `BAY_PAINT`.

## Tips, in plain words

The tooltip names what the next click does. Suggested, in the file's voice: theme room "Room — click for the bright blue room.",
blue room "Room — click for no room: a plain background and a free spin.", no room "Room — click for the room in this theme's
colours." `aria-pressed` stays true in both rooms and false with no room, and the screen exposes which step it is on for tests, for
example `data-bay-look`, beside `data-bay`, which stays on/off.

reaches the theme room, then two more reach no room). Grep every spec for `THREE_D_COPY.room` and `roomTip`.
