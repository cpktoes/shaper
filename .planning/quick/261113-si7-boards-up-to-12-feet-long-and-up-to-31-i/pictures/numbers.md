# The numbers for the founder's look (quick 261113-si7)

Measured with the app's own functions on the finished code (a board with the 6'0" Longboard preset's rails and foil,
centre 3", tips 7/8", no blank, flat bottom; the three 31" boards have a round nose and a round tail, Nose Angle 90).
Pages are Letter / A4. Blanks are what ROCKER's Pick a Blank lists under FITS THIS BOARD at its default settings
(2" longer than the board, 1/8" deck skin, 1/8" bottom pass, 1" width margin), of 219 pickable blanks.
