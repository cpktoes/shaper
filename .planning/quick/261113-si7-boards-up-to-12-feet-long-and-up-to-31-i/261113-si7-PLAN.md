### 4. My calls (flag these in the SUMMARY)

1. **8'0" itself is a wide board** (the founder's ruling): the top is 31" when the length is at least `inchesToMm(96)`
   less 1e-6 mm of float slack. Metric: 243.8 cm (95.98") is under 8'0" → 25"; 243.9 cm → 31".
2. **A wide board shortened under 8'0" is held on read, in the store**, exactly where the wings are resolved
   (`resolvedOutline`): the drawing, the Width label and the slider read 25" with the thumb at its end; the open board keeps
   its own width and gets it back when lengthened again, until the Width slider itself is moved. **A save stores what the
   screen shows** (25") — the wings precedent (quick 261014-wii, P2) — so every saved board, rack card, file and export reads
   one width without a hold in ~40 readers. Lengthening a board after saving it held does not bring the old width back.
3. **The Offset gets the same hold and its own window**: ±min(12", half the length − 16") on the 1/4" grid. Changes nothing
   from 4'8" up.
4. **The order form keeps its template window** (every existing print unchanged); only a board wider for its length than
   5'0" × 25" draws shorter in it.
5. **ROCKER cards keep today's size** (named 10'0" reference) and shrink — card and type together — only where the drawn
   12" spacing is shorter than today's 82 units, i.e. a board or board-plus-blank longer than 10'0" overall. At 12'0" in the
   12'6" EPS they draw at about 77% (name type ~7.7 units). That also fixes today's overlap for boards in very long blanks.
6. **The saved-board refusal floor stays 30"** (not half of 4'0"); the top becomes twice 12'0", 288".
7. **No fixture regenerates.** `scripts/extract-phase11-foil-golden.ts` reads the length range for its sweep; it gets its own
   literal 60–120" named for the fixture, so a re-run reproduces the committed fixture.
8. VOLUME's quick-estimate Width stays 16–24"; FINS' standalone ranges (already 4'–12') stay.
9. The quad oddity on a 4'0" is the founder's model — reported, not changed.
