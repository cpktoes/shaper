## What the shaper sees

A new **PLANING** tab sits between VIEWER and DATASHEET. It lists your board's five stations from nose to tail — the
board's rocker, the foam that comes off the deck, and the foam that comes off the bottom — and under each foam figure
how many full passes of your planer that is, plus anything left for a last, lighter pass ("3 + 1/16"" is three full
passes and 1/16" more). A figure in the warning colour means the board would poke out of the blank there. With no blank
picked, the tab asks you to pick one first. On PLANING every control in the sidebar still works, so you can slide the
board along its blank or change the Deck Skin and watch the numbers move. On an upright phone the tabs read
VIEWER · TOP · PLANING · DATA, so all four and your board's name fit across the screen; on a computer and on a phone
held sideways they keep their full names.
