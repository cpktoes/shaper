# RAILS' thin bottom line carries on under the tuck band

## Goal

On a soft (rolled) rail with channels, RAILS' thin bottom line carried the channels only out to the last bottom mark and
ended at the channel's floor there, while the tuck band started lower, from the planed bottom — a gap of about 1/16" to
1/8" on the stock Longboard's tail section, on screen and on the printed Rail Band Reference. The founder's ruling: carry
the thin line on under the tuck until it meets the band.

## Tasks

1. **Geometry.** `railBottomLineToBand` in `lib/geometry/rail-bottom.ts`: `railBottomLine`, then — where a channel's floor
   at the last mark stands above the band's foot — on outboard along the drawn bottom (walls and all) to the point where
   it meets the band's straight line, ending there exactly. Without channels, or with no gap, it is `railBottomLine`.
2. **The plot.** `rail-section-plot.tsx` finds the band rising from the last bottom mark (Tuck 1 on a single tuck, Tuck 2
   otherwise; none on a Hard Edge) and draws the bottom connector through the new helper. The printed Rail Band Reference
   draws the same plot, so paper follows.
3. **Prove it.** Geometry tests: the Longboard's case (the gap that was, the carried line ending on the band), the
   no-channels and Hard Edge cases unchanged, and every preset's soft rail at 4, 6 and 8 channels ending on the band or
   plain. Pictures before and after on the Longboard, on screen.

## Verify

- `rail-bottom.test.ts`, `tsc`, lint green; the RAILS browser specs at the next push gate.
- The thin line and the tuck band share an end point on the Longboard's tail with channels; every other board draws as
  it did.
