# Phase 13 Item 4a: The Thinness Rule, a Board's Own Floor, and the Reason Line Summary

A board may now be no thinner than 1/4" anywhere — at a tip, at a 12" station, anywhere along its
length — instead of the old 1/8". Every thickness slider and typed box on ROCKER, and the two tip
boxes in the Fit & Tip Defaults dialog, now start at that same 1/4" instead of offering something
thinner than the fit check will accept. When a blank gets turned down because the board would come
out too thin somewhere, the line under it now says what actually caused it — a thick blank for a
thin centre, a 12" fine-tune taking too much off, the board running past the end of the blank, or a
tip itself set thinner than 1/4" — instead of always blaming "this blank is too thick." Nothing
already saved was touched: an older board or account default with a thinner tip still opens with
exactly that tip, just flagged as not fitting rather than silently changed.

## The four reason-line drafts (verbatim, from the passing tests)

| Cause | Imperial | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| (a) thinCenter — a thin centre in a thick blank | `Less than 1/4" would be left 12" from the nose — this blank is too thick for a 1" center` | `Less than 6 mm would be left 30.5 cm from the nose — this blank is too thick for a 25 mm center` |
| (b) fineTune — a negative 12" fine-tune (typed or carried over) | `Less than 1/4" would be left 12" from the nose — your nose fine-tune takes too much off there` | `Less than 6 mm would be left 30.5 cm from the nose — your nose fine-tune takes too much off there` |
| (c) offBlank — the board runs past the end of the blank | `Less than 1/4" would be left 12" from the tail — your board runs past the end of this blank` | `Less than 6 mm would be left 30.5 cm from the tail — your board runs past the end of this blank` |
| (d) tipSetting — a tip itself set under 1/4" (only an older saved board or stored default) | `Less than 1/4" would be left at the tail tip — your tail tip is set thinner than that` | `Less than 6 mm would be left at the tail tip — your tail tip is set thinner than that` |

"nose"/"tail" in (b) and (d) name the half the station is in. No sentence ends in a full stop (the
flag adds one; the list row shows it bare). A runs-out shortfall with no cause reads exactly as
(a) — the wording every runs-out had before the other three causes existed. All four are pinned,
byte for byte in Imperial and composed from the formatters in Metric, by
`lib/geometry/blank-reasons.test.ts`'s "formatShortfall — the foil runs out, by cause" block and
the end-to-end test in the same file, and every cause is reached through a real `fitAt` call on a
real Marko blank in `lib/geometry/blank-fit.test.ts`'s "the runs-out reason names its cause" block.

## The offered/accepted design (FD-2/FD-3)

`FIT_DEFAULTS_RANGE_IN` is now what the Fit & Tip Defaults dialog's typed fields OFFER: both tips
run 1/4"–1 1/2", the same 1/4" the fit check floors on (`FOIL_THICKNESS_RANGE_IN.min`), so the
dialog can never again produce a value the fit check would refuse. A new
`FIT_DEFAULTS_ACCEPTED_RANGE_IN` is what a STORED or SUBMITTED value may still hold — identical to
the offered range except the two tips still accept down to 1/8" (`LEGACY_TIP_THICKNESS_MIN_IN`),
because a default saved before today, or promoted from a browser's older pick into the account at
sign-in, must read back exactly rather than being silently nulled or raised.

Every stored-default reader goes through `parseFitDefaultValue`, which now reads the accepted
range, so all of them keep a legacy thin tip exactly: `lib/db/queries.ts:113-118`,
`lib/fit-defaults-server.ts:36`, `components/fit-defaults-provider.tsx:93` and
`app/actions/fit-defaults.ts:48`. Only `components/fit-defaults-dialog.tsx:181` and
`components/rocker/board-on-blank.tsx:91` (the Deck Skin slider) read the offered
`FIT_DEFAULTS_RANGE_IN` — confirmed by grep, neither needed an edit. A saved board's own tips
(`lib/models/design-snapshot.ts`) were never bounded at all and stay untouched; a board saved with
a 3/16" nose tip and a 1/8" tail tip reopens with exactly those two tips (with and without a
blank), pinned by the new `design-snapshot.test.ts` describe block.

## For the founder: a pre-existing behaviour, found but not fixed here (Task 3 step 7)

Every typed thickness box on this app commits its number the moment you leave it (blur), and that
commit clamps the number into the box's own allowed range — that's not new, it's how the boxes
have always worked. The side effect: if a board or an account already has an older, thinner tip
(3/16" or 1/8", from before today), clicking into that tip's box and then straight back out again —
even without changing anything — re-enters the number the box is showing and, because the box now
starts at 1/4", lifts it to 1/4" right there. This can happen two places: on ROCKER's datasheet, and
in the Fit & Tip Defaults dialog, where simply tabbing from the first field through to the Done
button passes through both tip boxes on the way. Just opening a board, or opening the dialog,
changes nothing on its own — no slider or box writes until it's actually dragged or tabbed through.
And this is exactly how these boxes have always rounded any off-grid number a shaper types, so
nothing new is being introduced by this plan — the new 1/4" floor just makes it possible to notice
for the first time on an old thin tip. A possible fix, left for the founder to decide: make a box
you click into and leave without typing anything keep its number exactly as it was.
