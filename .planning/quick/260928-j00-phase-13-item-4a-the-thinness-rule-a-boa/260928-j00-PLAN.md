The founder decisions this plan implements (labelled FD-n for traceability; they amend Phase 12 D-18):
- **FD-1 Floor.** "raise the tip floor to 1/4", no boards should have a 1/8" tip anyway". The least foam a
  board may be anywhere, tips included, becomes 1/4" (`MIN_FOIL_THICKNESS_MM`).
- **FD-2 Lowest setting.** No control offers a thickness below 1/4": the tip settings on ROCKER and in the
  Fit & Tip Defaults dialog, and — since the floor is "anywhere" — every other ROCKER board-thickness control
  (Center Thickness, the hand-set 12" stations, the datasheet's typed cells). Decided per use of
  `FOIL_THICKNESS_RANGE_IN` (measured at plan time): rocker-controls.tsx lines 148 and 213 and
  rocker-datasheet.tsx lines 257 and 283 are controls and follow the new minimum; rocker-viewer.tsx line 539

  are untouched and no drawing frame moves; preset-source.ts line 12 is a comment; preset-source.test.ts and
  presets.test.ts read the range and still pass (presets' tips are 7/16" and up).
- **FD-3 Nothing saved is silently changed.** Saved boards' tips stay unbounded `z.number()` in
  lib/models/design-snapshot.ts (untouched). The stored account-default parser keeps ACCEPTING a tip down to
  1/8"; only what the dialog OFFERS rises to 1/4". Design chosen here: `FIT_DEFAULTS_RANGE_IN` becomes the
  offered range (the dialog and the ROCKER Deck Skin slider already read it, so they need no edit) and a new
  `FIT_DEFAULTS_ACCEPTED_RANGE_IN` — the offered range with the two tips' minimum widened back to 1/8" —
  is what `parseFitDefaultValue` reads, so every stored or submitted value (account row, cookie, localStorage,
  the save action's patch) keeps reading back exactly. The save action accepts it too, deliberately: at
  sign-in a browser's older 3/16" pick is promoted to the account in one all-or-nothing patch, and refusing it
  would also drop every other field in that promotion.
- **FD-4 Reason line by cause** ("correct with a proper rewording"). Today every runs-out reads
  `Less than {floor} would be left {where} — this blank is too thick for a {center} center`. The cause is
  classified from values the fit check already holds (is the station over the foam; is that end's tip setting
  under the floor; is that half's 12" fine-tune negative while the board would have had enough foam there
  without it) — no new geometry. Drafts for the founder's review stop, verbatim (Imperial, then Metric):

  | Cause | Imperial draft | Metric form |
  |---|---|---|
  | (a) thin centre in a thick blank (founder's own words, kept) | `Less than 1/4" would be left 12" from the nose — this blank is too thick for a 1" center` | `Less than 6 mm would be left 30.5 cm from the nose — this blank is too thick for a 25 mm center` |
  | (b) a negative 12" fine-tune, or a carried-over negative residual (it shows on the same slider as a tweak) | `Less than 1/4" would be left 12" from the nose — your nose fine-tune takes too much off there` | `Less than 6 mm would be left 30.5 cm from the nose — your nose fine-tune takes too much off there` |

  | (d) a tip setting under 1/4" (only an older saved board or stored default can hold one now) | `Less than 1/4" would be left at the tail tip — your tail tip is set thinner than that` | `Less than 6 mm would be left at the tail tip — your tail tip is set thinner than that` |

  "nose"/"tail" in (b) and (d) is the half the station is in. No sentence ends in a full stop (the flag adds
  one; the list row shows it bare). A runs-out shortfall carrying no cause reads as (a) — the wording every
  runs-out had before causes existed.
