# Quick Task 260830-31h: Vertical ROCKER Rail Titles No Longer Collide With Their Cards Summary

**Nose-up, each ROCKER rail's title ("Thickness", "Rocker") now sits directly above its own Center
card, centred on that card's column, proved by run-width containment math rather than the cap-height
reserve that let the word collide with its card and spill past the drawing's edge.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~45 min
- **Tasks:** 2 of 3 (Task 3 is a blocking human-verify checkpoint, deferred — see below)
- **Files modified:** 3

## Accomplishments
- Fixed the collision: the word `Thickness` was landing 27px inside the deck Center card's right
  edge and spilling 15px past the drawing's own frame edge, nose-up. The layout was only ever
  reserving the word's CAP HEIGHT (like the horizontal view correctly does), never the much wider
  RUN a word turned upright on screen presents across the rail — the same axis-swap the cards
  themselves already account for (`cardBandDepth`).
- Moved each title onto its own rail's live anchor (`deckRailY`/`railY`), directly above the
  Center card, and proved by test that its run fits the card's own column (156.46 units of room
  for a 155.3-unit bound on "Thickness") at every board length and card scale.
- Proved, not assumed, that the title cannot collide with the next station up the board either
  (`Nose @ 12"`): swept across every board length in `LENGTHS_IN`, worst-case clearance is 136.8
  units (~56 screen px) at the shortest board (5'0") — matching the plan's own hand-derivation to
  the tenth of a unit.
- Reshaped `railLabelBandDepth` so its vertical arm is a DERIVED overhang (0 today) rather than a
  hardcoded zero or a copy of the horizontal cap-height formula — a future longer title would grow
  the frame to hold it instead of silently escaping.
- Moved the two title words (`RAIL_LABEL_TEXTS`) into the layout module so the drawing's vocabulary
  and the proof that it fits can never disagree.
- Nose-left is untouched: the horizontal frame, its clearance/centring assertions, and the
  print-path characterisation pin (compact/none `viewBox` literals) all pass unedited.

## Decisions Made
- Corrected the bug report's own stated reasoning (see `<design_decision>` section 0 in the plan)
  before implementing: the fix works because the title's run shares the card COLUMN nose-up, not
  because it becomes "parallel to the rail" — screen-upright text always runs across a vertical
  rail regardless of where along it the anchor sits.
- Kept the frame's own extents reading only CEILING values (never `appliedScale`), exactly as the
  existing frame-invariance suite already protects, while letting the title's own ANCHOR read the
  live card position — these are two different concerns (frame containment vs. visual heading)
  and conflating them would have either broken frame invariance or left the title visibly
  off-centre from its card at non-ceiling scales.
