The founder (2026-11-03), after the Board Rack fix (quick 261103-drw, merged fefaa6ad): a board's litres must count
the tail it is drawn with. Today two tails are wrong. A diamond tail's V is foam the litres leave out. A swallow tail's
notch is air the litres count. Both happen for the same reason: the volume reads the outline's half-width through
`sampleOutline`, which knows nothing of the tail cut. `sampleOutline` reads the board's full half-width straight
across a swallow's notch. Behind a diamond's tail block it reads 0, because its past-the-end fallback returns the
nose's 0.

The plan adds ONE rule to the geometry: the foam actually there at a station, on one side of the stringer. The
litres read it. So does the Template Area, through the drawn outline's true area. The 51 cross-sections are placed
so the cut's corners are stations, which makes a straight-sided cut exact rather than a sampling artefact.
`sampleOutline` and `OutlineGeometry.area` stay exactly as they are: they are the prototype's own curve, pinned by
its golden. TEMPLATE's measuring lines, the fins and the blank fit read them as the drawn curve, and that is right
for them.

## What the tail cut is (outline.ts, as TEMPLATE draws it)

`silhouette()` (screen-tiles.ts) draws three parts: the right half tail-to-nose from `points`, the left half back,
then one closing point `(centreCloseStation, 0)`. Call the outline's first point `first = points[0]` and the closing
station `close = centreCloseStation`. There are three cases:
- **Diamond.** `first` = (effectiveDiamondDepth, half tail block) and close = 0. The closing edges run from the tip
  (0, 0) to `first` on each side. The V is a triangle of area |first.station - close| x first.halfWidth (both sides
  together) that `points`, `area` and `sampleOutline` all leave out.
- **Swallow.** `first` = (0, half tail block) and close = crotchDepth. The closing edges run from each tail point to
  the crotch. The notch is a triangle of the same area formula that `points`, `area` and `sampleOutline` all count as
  foam.
- **Square, pin, round.** `first.station` = close = 0, so there is no cut.
