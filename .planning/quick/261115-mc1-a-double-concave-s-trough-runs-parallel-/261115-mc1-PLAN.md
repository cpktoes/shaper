- **Parallel** — today's rule, unchanged: Trough At off the stringer at every station (D-02).

### 2. A call the planner made against the orchestrator's note (flag it in the SUMMARY for the founder)

The orchestrator suggested keeping each By-hand distance against the cut's half-width **at its own station**. Measured,
that breaks the locked D-05 ("Switching to By hand starts both ends at the current Trough At, so nothing jumps"): a Spiral
Vee ends at the tail, where on the Shortboard and Mid-length the cut is about 1" wide, so its Trough at End would be held
between 3/4" and 7/8" — switching to By hand would swing the whole trough in toward the stringer (at 6" from the tail,
from 3" to about 1 7/16"), and the End slider would barely move. So this plan keeps the two By-hand ends **exactly as
Trough At is kept today** — against the cut's half-width at Deepest At (never read under an inch): at least the flat +
1/4", at most 85% of it — and D-04's "the same clamps apply after" does the rest at every station in the drawing, DATA and
the foam. The cost, the same one Trough At already has: where a cut runs out of room near a narrow tail, the drawing pulls
the trough in there while the slider keeps the figure the shaper set. Switching to By hand changes nothing, on every
board (the unit test proves it), and the End slider is free.

Also measured: on 48 of 240 board × double × wing-rule cases (pin tails) today's keep already pulls a double's opening
Trough At in on the first drag of any slider. So switching to By hand must only seed both ends at Trough At — it must not
run `keepStationsInOrder` — or the switch itself would move those boards.
