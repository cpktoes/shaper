<planner_choices>
Each is the planner's call where the spec left room; the founder can overrule any.
- **200 on a failed email (the spec's question).** Clerk's delivery service (Svix) retries any
  non-2xx for about a day and a half and can switch off an endpoint that keeps failing for days.
  This endpoint also carries user.deleted, so a Resend outage answered with 500s could end with
  Clerk disabling the endpoint and account clean-up silently stopping. And a Resend reply lost to
  the 10-second timeout may still have delivered the email, so a retry could send a duplicate. So
  every verified user.created answers 200, and the body says what happened ('Sign-up email sent' /
  '...skipped: no email key on this server' / '...failed'). That body shows in Clerk's Message log,
  so the founder can see a failed one there and press Clerk's own Resend on that message once
  Resend is back. user.deleted keeps its 500-so-Clerk-retries rule, since a retried delete is
  harmless and needed.
- **The time is Pacific.** The founder works from Oceanside, CA; the line reads e.g. "Saturday,
  October 10, 2026 at 2:15 PM PDT (Pacific time)", PST in winter, assembled from
  Intl.DateTimeFormat formatToParts with timeZone America/Los_Angeles so neither the server's own
  time zone nor the ICU version's joining words can change it. The time is Clerk's created_at for
  the account; when that is missing the time the note was sent.
- **Reply goes to the new shaper.** reply_to is the account's email when it passes the Contact
  form's own CONTACT_EMAIL_PATTERN, so pressing Reply in Zoho writes to the new shaper (say
  hello). Otherwise reply_to is support@ itself (no change to ResendSendBody's shape).
- **How they signed up** is read from the payload in this order: Google (an external account whose
  provider is oauth_google or google, or whose verification strategy is oauth_google; or the primary
  email verified by from_oauth_google or linked to oauth_google — both spellings accepted because
  Clerk's own types say "e.g. google" while its API sends oauth_google); another provider; then the
  primary email's verification strategy (email_code or email_link with a password = "Email and
  password"); then invitation / dashboard / other (Task 2).
- **The note lives in lib/contact/sign-up-notice.ts**, beside the Contact form's sender, so it reuses
  CONTACT_SENDER, CONTACT_RECIPIENT, CONTACT_LIMITS, CONTACT_EMAIL_PATTERN, oneLine,
  resolveContactDelivery and sendWithResend directly. That folder may not import @clerk/ (boundary

  it reads; tsc proves Clerk's UserJSON fits it at the one call in lib/clerk-webhook.ts (measured
  below).
- **Duplicates are accepted.** Clerk delivers at least once; if a 200 is ever lost, or the founder
  presses Resend on a sent message, a second note arrives. No dedupe store (no database change).
