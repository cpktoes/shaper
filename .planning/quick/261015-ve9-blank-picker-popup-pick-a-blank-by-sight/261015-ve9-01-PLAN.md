<objective>

becomes one button. It opens a PICK A BLANK popup where every blank long enough for the board is a catalogue card: its

printed station table, and the shaper's own board dashed on it where the app would place it. A blank too thin at

popup and puts the blank on the board.

the Flat / Upright switch with upright cards, the Show my board switch, the phone filter layouts and the
short-screen compact card.

of from a text list.

- **P-1 — A too-thin blank lands centred** (placement 0) when picked, with no placement search. The brief allows "its

  search per too-thin blank, which is seconds of work when a thick centre makes every blank too thin. The sketch's own
  data did the same.
- **P-2 — The list intro is reworded.** "Shortest first. Each is at least …, with room at the center …" stops being
  true once too-thin blanks are listed and plan 02 adds other sorts. New wording: `Every blank listed is at least {2"}
  longer than your board. A blank fits when it leaves room at the center for a {1/8" deck skin and a 1/8" bottom
  pass} and your board sits inside it from tip to tip. Greyed blanks don't fit — the line under each says why.`
- **P-3 — When blanks are listed but not one is thick enough**, the popup shows the old empty-list E2 / E3 heading and

  instead of replacing the list. E1 ("No blank is long enough") still replaces the list, because nothing is listed.
- **P-4 — The header count.** `{n} of {m} blanks · {k} fit`: n = cards showing, m = blanks long enough for this board

  only when a maker is unticked.
- **P-6 — The upright card's Rocker row and the compact line read the levelled curve's tip rocker** (the same number
