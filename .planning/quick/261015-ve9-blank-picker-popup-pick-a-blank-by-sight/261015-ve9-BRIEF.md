Plus the founder's earlier spec (pasted 2026-10-15), still in force where the above doesn't override it: a button showing
the current blank's name and a small thumbnail opens a scrollable popup listing **every blank that passes the length
filter**; each shows vendor and name, length, center width and thickness, nose/tail rocker, and a drawing of the outline

rocker range; **selecting a row closes the popup and loads the blank**; on a phone a full-screen sheet, one column.

### What to build in THIS task (part 1)

1. **The BLANK section of the ROCKER sidebar becomes one blank button** (both layouts — it lives in `RockerControls`,
   which is the sidebar on a computer and the stacked controls on a phone):
   - With a blank: a small flat thumbnail (outline over rocker), the name (+ volume when printed), the meta line
     (`blankRowMeta`), and "Change blank ›". The fit flag (`BlankFlag`, with its "Switch to This Blank" offer) and
     "Remove This Blank" + its sentence stay as they are today. `BLANK_CATALOG_NOTE` stays at the end.
   - Without a blank: "Pick a Blank" with a line like "196 blanks long enough for this board, 194 fit".
   - Today's inline list (intro, search box, six rows, "Show all N blanks", Change Blank / Keep This Blank) is REPLACED
     by the popup. The intro sentence (`listIntro`) moves into the popup.
2. **The popup** — Base UI dialog (`components/ui/dialog.tsx`; the large `view-full-sized-dialog.tsx` and the scrolling
   `app-settings-dialog.tsx` are precedents). On a computer: nearly the whole window (about 32 px in from each edge, at
   most ~1180 px wide), header "PICK A BLANK", "for your {L × W × T} board · {n} of {m} blanks · {k} fit", ✕. Full screen
   on an upright phone (below the `max-shell` width line, 820) AND on a short screen (`[@media(max-height:500px)]`, a
   phone held sideways) — width and height switches only, never the pointer (CLAUDE.md "Layout"). ✕, Esc, and a click
   outside close it with nothing changed. **Picking a blank closes it and loads the blank** (`pickBlank(record,
   verdict.placement)`, exactly as a row does today).
3. **Filter bar** (all view state; see "Remembered" below):
   - Search (`matchesBlankSearch`, aria-label "Search blanks" kept).
   - **The four makers as chips with counts — these ARE the Settings → Blank Makers setting** (`useBlankMakers()`,
     `setMakerShown`): unticking one here unticks it in Settings, and at least one stays ticked (as Settings' "Keep at
     least one maker ticked").
   - **Length range** and **nose-rocker range** (a chip that opens two sliders, from/to, with "Any length" / "Any nose
     rocker" to clear). Nose rocker = the blank's levelled bottom rocker at the nose tip. Steps: 1" / 1 cm for length,
     1/8" / 1 mm for nose rocker. Labels through `lib/geometry/units.ts` / `measure-display.ts` (Rule 2: length is a dim,
     rocker is a mark).
   - **★ Starred only**.
   - **Sort**: Length (default), Nose rocker, Center thickness, Volume (ascending; ties by length then name).
   - **Flat / Upright** switch (default **Flat on every device**) and **Show my board** on/off (default **On**).
   - Layout: on a computer, line 1 = search + Flat/Upright + Show my board; line 2 = makers, Length, Nose rocker,
     Starred only, Sort. On an upright phone, line 1 = search + Flat/Upright as two icons; line 2 = a sideways-scrolling
     chip row (makers, Show my board, Length, Nose rocker, Starred only, Sort). On a short screen, one sideways row
     (search, the two icons, the chips) and the counts move into the header.
4. **Groups**, in this order: **★ STARRED · n** (starred blanks, any verdict, greyed if they don't fit), **FITS THIS
   BOARD · n**, **WON'T FIT THIS BOARD · n**. Starred always first whatever the sort. Same group-label style as today.
5. **Too-thin blanks are listed, greyed, with why** — today `listBlanks` (`lib/geometry/blank-fit.ts:927`) drops any blank
   failing `floorCheck` (length OR centre). Now only the **length** floor hides a blank; one failing only the centre floor
   is listed in WON'T FIT with a reason sentence quoting the same numbers the list intro and the F4 flag quote (centre +
   Deck Skin + Planer Max Depth — `CenterFloorRules`, `useCenterFloorRules`). Still pickable (it lands at its best
   placement / centre and the flag explains). This is a change to tested geometry — Rule 1: pure, tested, and every
   caller of `listBlanks` / `emptyReason` re-checked (the empty-list messages E1–E5 in `blank-reasons.ts`,
   `catalogueExtremes`, the flag's `nearestFit` which must still offer only blanks that FIT).
6. **Cards** (flat, the default): name (+ volume), maker, the drawing, one line `{length} long · {nose} nose rocker ·
   {tail} tail`, and the printed station table — columns Nose, N 12", Center, T 12", Tail (the catalogue's N0, N12, C,
   T12, T0; metric headers per the app's own station-label convention — check `rocker-datasheet.tsx`), rows Width /
   Thick / Rocker, the printed figures (a missing figure reads "—"). Widths are dims, thickness and rocker are marks
   (Rule 2). Greyed card: name muted, reason in `text-surf-warning-ink` (never opacity). The board's own blank: accent
   border + "On your board now". **Upright cards** (the switch): the drawing standing nose-up — outline and, beside it,
   the rocker (bottom toward the outline) — with Length, Center width × thickness, Rocker nose · tail, 12" widths beside
   it, as the sketch draws them.
7. **The drawing** — true proportion, never stretched, **each blank filling its card** (a fixed-aspect box, about 3.1:1
   flat, the blank fitted inside). Drawn **with the main view's curves**: `prepareBlank(record)` (`rocker.sample`,
   `thickness.sample`, `width.sample` — round noses included), sampled densely near each tip the way
   `buildBlankTopView` does (`TIP_SEGMENT_SAMPLES`). Outline from above (foam shade + ink edge, stringer `16 4 4 4`,
   centre station `5 4`, printed width points as small dots) over the side view (bottom to deck, foam shade + ink edge),
   nose left. **Show my board**: the board's outline (TEMPLATE's own silhouette — `silhouette(outlineGeometry)` from
   `screen-tiles.ts`, as `buildBlankTopView` uses) drawn dashed in board fill on each blank's outline at that blank's
   verdict placement (`blankStationOf`). Strokes stay at screen width as the box scales. Nothing but faint lines inside a
   blank; no text in the SVG needed.
   **Rule 1:** the drawing data (outline samples, side samples, the board placed on a blank) is a pure function in
   `lib/geometry/` (e.g. `blank-card-art.ts`) with unit tests — no React, no DOM. Components only turn its points into
   paths.
8. **Stars, saved in the browser only in this task** (part 2 adds the shaper's account, a nullable `user_preferences`
   column and a production migration — NOT in this task; build part 1 so part 2 only adds the account hand-off, the way
   `components/blank-makers-provider.tsx` + `lib/blank-makers-preference.ts` are built). A star is a blank's identity:
   maker + name (unique together in the `blanks` table). localStorage key `shaper-starred-blanks`, JSON, parsed through
   an untrusted-value allow-list (strings only, each within the catalogue's text limit — `BLANK_TEXT_MAX` 120 in
   `lib/blanks/catalog.ts` — a sane cap on count, duplicates dropped), `useSyncExternalStore` like the makers provider
   (a raw-string snapshot). No cookie (the list renders in the browser; a long star list would outgrow a cookie). A star
   on a blank no longer in the catalogue is simply never shown. Starring never changes a board or marks it unsaved.
   The ☆ sits in each card's top-right corner (44-dot box on a coarse pointer), `aria-pressed`, "Star 6'2"A" /
   "Unstar …"; a filled star in ink, an empty one in muted ink (no new hue, no warning colour).
9. **Remembered**: the Flat/Upright and Show my board choices are remembered in the browser (a per-viewer convenience,
   localStorage, defaults Flat + On). The search, ranges, Starred only and sort are view state for the visit.
10. **Theme and units follow the app** — the popup has no theme or units control of its own; everything reads
    `useUnits()` and the `--surf-*` tokens.

### House rules that apply

- CLAUDE.md: Rule 1 (geometry pure + tested in `lib/geometry/`), Rule 2 (every number through `units.ts` /
  `measure-display.ts`, dims vs marks), the three switches (width = layout, pointer = control SIZE via `coarse:`,
  height = short-screen behaviour — never conflated), phone chrome tightened under `max-shell` and the short-screen rule.
- `coarse:` controls are 44-dot touch boxes (`coarse:min-h-11`, see `blank-picker.tsx`'s TEXT_LINK_CLASS).
- `useControlsLocked()` (`components/design/use-controls-locked.ts`, see `e2e/board-lock*.spec.ts`): when the board is
  locked, browsing and starring may stay open but picking must not change the board — match how today's rows behave
  (`disabled={locked}`, `data-lock-exempt` on browse-only controls).
- Older Safari must work (the founder's iPad, Safari < 18.4): no CSS 3D, no scroll-driven animation; see the
  `old-safari-buttons.spec.ts` / globals.css base-layer fix for `<button>` alignment.
- The sketch-findings skill (`.claude/skills/sketch-findings-shaper/`) — drafting grammar (strokes, dashes, foam/blank
  tokens `--outline-foam-shade`, `--outline-blank-line`), and `references/blank-top-view.md` for the round nose.
- Catalogue text renders as React text only (T-11-32 in the current picker).
- Performance: up to ~220 cards; prepare each blank once per visit (the WeakMap cache in `use-blank-list.ts`), compute
  card art once per prepared blank, and render the cards only while the popup is open.
