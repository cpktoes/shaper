# Quick 261015-ve9 Plan 02: The blank popup's filter bar, stars, upright cards and phone layouts — Summary

The PICK A BLANK popup now has everything a shaper uses to narrow and arrange the catalogue page:
a star on every card (kept in this browser), the four maker chips (which ARE Settings → Blank
Makers), Length and Nose rocker ranges, ★ Starred only, a sort, a Flat / Upright switch and a Show
my board switch — in a computer layout, an upright-phone layout and a sideways-phone layout. And,
on the founder's ruling of 2026-10-16, every nose and tail rocker a card quotes is now the
catalogue's own printed figure.

## What a shaper sees

- **The star.** Every card has a ☆ in its top-right corner. Tapping it fills the star and moves the
  blank into a **★ STARRED · n** group at the top of the popup, above FITS THIS BOARD and WON'T FIT
  THIS BOARD, whatever the sort. A starred blank that won't fit is still greyed there with its
  reason. Stars stay in this browser across reloads. Starring never picks a blank, never changes
  the board and never marks it unsaved. On a locked board the star still works.
- **The filter bar on a computer** is two lines:
  - the search, then **Flat** / **Upright** (with words), then **Show my board: On**;
  - then the four makers with their counts (`US Blanks 95 · Arctic Foam 28 · Marko Foam 20 ·
    Millennium Foam 53` on the default 6'0"), **Length: any ▾**, **Nose rocker: any ▾**,
    **★ Starred only**, and **Sort Length ▾**.
- **The maker chips are Settings.** Unticking Arctic Foam in the popup unticks it in App Default
  Settings, and the reverse. The last maker ticked can't be unticked (it says "Keep at least one
  maker ticked" to a screen reader, as Settings does).
- **The ranges.** Length opens a drawer of two sliders, "Length from" and "to", stepping 1" (1 cm in
  Metric), with the two ends under each and **Any length** to clear. Nose rocker does the same in
  1/8" (1 mm). Once set, the chip reads its two ends, e.g. `Length: 6'4" – 7'0"`; in Metric the
  length ends read in centimetres to one decimal and the nose-rocker ends in whole millimetres.
- **The sort**: Length (the default), Nose rocker, Center thickness or Volume, smallest first; a
  blank with no printed volume sorts after every one with a volume.
- **Nothing matches**: a search alone says `No blanks match "…".` with Clear Search; any other
  filter says `No blanks match these filters.` with Clear Filters.
- **Upright** stands every blank nose-up with its rocker beside it, and beside the drawing: the name
  and litres, the maker, then Length, Center width × thickness, Rocker nose · tail and 12" widths.
- **Show my board: Off** takes the dashed board off every blank. Flat / Upright and Show my board are
  remembered in this browser; the search, ranges, Starred only and sort last for the visit.
- **An upright phone**: the search beside two icon buttons (Flat, Upright), over one sideways-scrolling
  row of chips that also holds Show my board. One card per row.
- **A phone held sideways**: the whole bar is one sideways row, and each flat card trades its table
  for one line, e.g. `21 11/16" × 3 1/16" center · 4 7/8" nose`, which only wraps at its dot.
- **A finger** gets a 44-dot touch box on every chip, switch, star, the Sort box and the ✕.

## Deviations from Plan

### The founder's ruling, applied mid-plan

**P-6 overruled by the founder (2026-10-16): printed figures everywhere.** `blankTipRockers` now
returns the catalogue's printed N0 and T0 rocker — exactly what the station table's Rocker row
prints — and falls back to the levelled curve's tip only where the catalogue printed no rocker at
that tip (a printed 0 stays 0). Everything that reads it follows from that one function: the
upright card's Rocker row, the short-screen line, the nose-rocker filter and its slider ends, and
the nose-rocker sort. The test proves the printed figure wins on every pickable blank, and the
Marko 10'2" M reads its printed nose, 4 9/16", where the levelled curve reads 4 5/16" (checked
against the app's own functions). Commit 084c049a.

### Auto-fixed issues

1. **[Rule 3 – blocking] Two test ledgers named the old files.** `lib/units-isolation.test.ts` now
   lists `blank-filter-bar.tsx` and accepts `blank-browse.ts` as reading through the display boundary,
   with a new test proving `blank-browse.ts` imports `measure-display` and steps its sliders through
   `measureSlider`. `components/ui/input.css.test.ts`'s list of the shared Input's consumers now names
   `blank-filter-bar.tsx`, where the search box moved. Both outside `files_modified`; ledger updates
   only. (Commit 7ed4c893.)
2. **[Rule 1 – bug] The Sort box was 18 dots tall on an iPhone.** WebKit ignores a native select's
   min-height. It now draws without the browser's own look (`appearance-none`) and its own chevron,
   so it is 44 dots tall for a finger and lines up with the search on a sideways phone. (604e79fc.)
3. **[Rule 1 – bug] The sideways one-line card broke inside `4 7/8"`.** It now only wraps at its
   ` · `. (604e79fc.)
4. **[Rule 1 – test] An existing touch-sizing test timed out on WebKit.** It measured every card's
   pick button one round trip at a time (about 190 cards) and passed 30 seconds. It now measures them
   all in one pass in the page — the same assertion. (b42c1648.)
5. **Task 3 became two commits**: the two screen fixes above, then the tests.
6. **TDD order.** Task 1's code was written alongside its tests and both were run together; the
   plan's one-commit-per-task rule meant no separate RED commit.
7. **Short-screen class names are written out in full.** A `${SHORT}:` shorthand would have produced
   no CSS at all (Tailwind only sees class names written out), so every `[@media(max-height:500px)]:`
   is literal.
