`<decisions>`. Read that block first. They stay locked. This plan implements:

- **D-01 (the filter bar's layouts).**
  - Computer: line 1 = search + Flat / Upright (with words) + Show my board. Line 2 = the makers, Length, Nose
    rocker, Starred only, Sort.
  - Upright phone (below `max-shell`): line 1 = search + Flat / Upright as two icons. Line 2 = one sideways-scrolling
    chip row: makers, Show my board, Length, Nose rocker, Starred only, Sort.
  - Short screen (`[@media(max-height:500px)]`): one sideways row (search, the two icons, the chips). The counts
    already sit in the header (plan 01).
  - Width and height switches only; `coarse:` sizes controls and never moves a layout.
- **D-02** Flat by default on every device, with the Flat / Upright switch.
- **D-04** The Show my board switch, On by default.
- **D-06** Stars, saved in the browser only in this task.
  - A star is a blank's identity: maker + name, which are unique together in the `blanks` table.
  - localStorage key `shaper-starred-blanks`, JSON, read through an untrusted-value allow-list: strings only, each
    within `BLANK_TEXT_MAX` (120), a cap on the count, duplicates dropped.
  - Read with `useSyncExternalStore` over a raw-string snapshot, like the makers provider. No cookie.
  - A star on a blank no longer in the catalogue is simply never shown.
  - Starring never changes a board or marks it unsaved.
  - The ☆ sits in the card's top-right corner (a 44-dot box on a coarse pointer), with `aria-pressed`. Starred: a
    filled star in ink. Not starred: an empty star in muted ink. No new hue, no warning colour.
  - The ★ STARRED · n group always comes first, whatever the sort; any verdict; greyed if it doesn't fit.
- **D-07 (filters and sort).**
  - Search.
  - Length range and nose-rocker range: a chip that opens two sliders, from and to, with Any length / Any nose rocker
    to clear. Steps are 1" / 1 cm for length and 1/8" / 1 mm for nose rocker. Length is a length; rocker is a mark.
    Nose rocker is the levelled bottom rocker at the nose tip (`blankTipRockers`, plan 01).
  - ★ Starred only.
  - Sort by Length (the default), Nose rocker, Center thickness or Volume, ascending, ties by length then name.
- **D-10** The upright card: header = name + volume only. Under the maker come `Length`, then Center width ×
  thickness, Rocker nose · tail and 12" widths, beside the drawing (`uprightFacts`, plan 01).
- **D-11** The short-screen compact flat card: no table. Header name … length · volume. One line, `compactLine`
  (plan 01).
- **D-12** The four makers as chips with counts ARE Settings → Blank Makers (`useBlankMakers()`, `setMakerShown`). At
  least one stays ticked, as Settings' "Keep at least one maker ticked".
- **D-13** Remembered in the browser: Flat / Upright and Show my board (localStorage, defaults Flat + On). Search,
  ranges, Starred only and sort are view state for the visit.

**Planner's choices, added here (for the founder to confirm or overrule):**
- **P-7 — A star's accessible name includes the maker**: `Star US Blanks 6'2"A` / `Unstar …`. The brief's example
  was `Star 6'2"A`, but the same name can belong to two makers.
- **P-8 — A maker chip's count** is that maker's blanks long enough for this board (`longEnoughByMaker`), whatever
  the search, ranges or Starred only say, and whether or not the chip is ticked.
- **P-9 — Clear Filters** resets the search, both ranges and Starred only. It leaves the makers (a setting), the sort
  and the two remembered switches alone.
- **P-10 — A starred blank shows only in ★ STARRED**, not repeated in its fit group. Its greying and reason still say
  whether it fits.
- **P-11 — Range ends snap OUTWARD to the step grid** of the system in use, so the shortest and longest blank are
  always reachable. A range set in one system keeps its millimetre ends when the shaper switches systems.
</decisions>
