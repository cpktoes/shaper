# Quick Task 260823-mt5 — Shaper Use Only moves to page 2

`SHAPER USE ONLY` — board name, blank & rocker, board #, price — leaves page 1's header for the foot
of page 2.

## Why it fits there

The muse keeps this box in the header, where the shop fills it in as the order is taken. It belongs
on page 2 here because that whole page is the shaper's: the front is the customer's copy of what they
asked for, and the blank number, the price and the rocker the blank came off are the shop's own record
of the job.

At the **foot** of page 2 rather than the top: the tables above it are what a shaper reads *while*
working the blank, and this is what gets filled in before and after. It also puts page 2's white space
to use — the space left over once both tables went full width.

## The knock-on to handle

Page 1's header band was 17% of the sheet, sized to carry the logo block, four rider fields *and* this
box. Without it the rider fields need about a third of what remains, so the band would sit a third
empty.

- Band drops to **12%**, giving 5% of the sheet back to the drawings row.
- The rider fields go `justify-between` rather than stacking at the top — an order form would rather
  spend spare height on the gaps between ruled lines, since they get written on by hand.
