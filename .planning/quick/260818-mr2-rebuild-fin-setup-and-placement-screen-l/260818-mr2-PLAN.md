<objective>
Rebuild the Claude Design prototype's **Fin Setup & Placement** screen as the third screen of the Next.js app, and port its placement math into `lib/geometry/fins.ts`.

Purpose: fin placement is the other half of the product's Core Value — with the rail bands, it is the set of numbers a shaper routes fin boxes to. The models come from published shaping guidance (McKee's tables, the Shaper Supply half-rate convention) compiled into the prototype, so the port must be provably identical to it, not merely similar. Extract-and-execute golden tests — the technique already proven twice, on the outline and rail-band ports — are what make "provably" real.

Output: `lib/geometry/fins.ts` (pure, metric-internal, golden-tested), `lib/geometry/toe-aim-tables.ts` (the transcribed McKee aim tables), the `/design/fins` screen with VIEWER, DATA and MODEL INFO tabs plus the toe-in aim-table modal, and a FINS entry in the top nav.

**Not a tracer-first plan.** The architecture this work needs — a pure `lib/geometry/*` module + an extract-and-execute golden fixture + a Tailwind/shadcn screen under `app/design/*` — was proven end-to-end by quick tasks 260818-kvp (outline editor) and 260818-lm0 (rail band calculator). A thin vertical slice would add no information, so this decomposes as math-then-screen-then-reference-panels.
</objective>
