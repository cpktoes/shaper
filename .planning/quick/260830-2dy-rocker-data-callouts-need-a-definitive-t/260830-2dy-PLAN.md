<objective>
Give the ROCKER editor's two rails of read-outs a definitive name each, so nothing on the drawing
has to be guessed at: the word **Thickness** centred outside the deck rail, the word **Rocker**
centred outside the bottom rail, in both orientations.

**What the founder sees today.** The rocker drawing carries two rails of figures — thickness on the
deck side, rocker on the bottom side — and the drawing itself never says so. A shaper reading the
sheet has to infer it from which side of the board a number sits on.

**What they asked for.** "A definitive Thickness vs Rocker name. Maybe just some centered text
outside the cards above and below the center mark."

**The vocabulary is already settled and is NOT ours to re-invent.** The sidebar's two collapsible
sections are titled exactly `Rocker` (`rocker-controls.tsx:106`) and `Thickness` (`:304`), and the
DATASHEET's two row groups are labelled exactly `Thickness` (`rocker-datasheet.tsx:98`) and `Rocker`
(`:117`). The drawing uses those same two words, in that same Title Case, byte for byte — that is
what ties a rail on the drawing to the sliders that set it and the table that lists it. No
uppercasing, no abbreviating, no "Deck"/"Bottom", no third word.

**Editor only.** This applies to `callouts === "full"` and nothing else. `"compact"` is the Summary
order form's printed ROCKER box, which already carries this information as a box caption
(`captionRight="Thickness above, rocker below"`, `order-form.tsx:329`) inside a 0.92in-tall
print-calibrated box that quick task 260829-vus proved cannot hold more type at 9pt. `"none"` draws
no rails at all. Both must come out of this task byte-identical, pinned by test.

Purpose: the drawing stops needing an explanation.
Output: two rail titles, owned end to end by the pure layout module, with the dead board-length
label machinery removed rather than left sitting beside them.

## 1. The dead board-length label machinery is DELETED, not repurposed

Quick task `260830-fast2` (commit `1df2cc9`) removed the board-length label from this drawing but
left its layout machinery behind, because unpicking it exceeded that task's remit. What survives is
genuinely dead: `grep -c "labelX\|labelY" components/rocker/rocker-viewer.tsx` returns **0**.

The ruling is **delete**, for a reason stronger than tidiness: the old machinery is not the shape
the new titles need. The length label was ONE anchor, left-aligned, at the NOSE end of the frame's
**long** axis, and its vertical reserve (`minY = PAD_X - maxCardHeight / 2 - LENGTH_LABEL_GAP -
LENGTH_LABEL_SIZE - 4`) buys room on that long axis. The rail titles are TWO anchors, centre-aligned,
at the MIDDLE of the long axis, needing room on the **cross** axis. Repurposing would mean keeping
names (`labelX`, `LENGTH_LABEL_GAP`) that describe a different object, and reserving room on the
wrong axis. So:

- **Removed:** the `labelX` / `labelY` fields and their `if/else` block, the `LENGTH_LABEL_SIZE` and
  `LENGTH_LABEL_GAP` constants, the two `LENGTH_LABEL_*` terms in the vertical `minY`, and the three
  tests that reference them.
- **Removed too — `PAD_TOP` (26).** It is part of the same dead object: `labelY = PAD_TOP - 8` shows
  it was sized to hold the length label's own type band. With the label gone it is an unexplained
  literal; the deck title's own band replaces it as the top reserve, sized from the thing it
  actually holds.

This also collects the follow-up `260830-fast2` logged in STATE.md ("the nose-up frame carries ~22
units of now-unused top margin — reclaim in a follow-up if it reads loose"). Dropping the two
`LENGTH_LABEL_*` terms hands those units back to the drawing nose-up, so the vertical board gets
slightly LARGER rather than paying for its own new title.

## 2. One symmetric band, reserved at the pin ceiling, on both sides in both orientations

Four new constants and one derived band function, all in `rocker-view-frame.ts`:

- `RAIL_LABEL_SIZE = 12` — one unit above the station-name row (10) and one below the value row
  (13): a heading over the rail, not louder than the numbers it heads.
- `RAIL_LABEL_GAP = 8` — clear space between the rail's own outer card edge and the title's nearest
  glyph edge.
- `RAIL_LABEL_EDGE_GUTTER = 4` — clear space between the title and the frame's own edge.
- `RAIL_LABEL_CAP_RATIO = 0.72` — cap height as a share of font size. These are two capitalised
  words with no descenders, so a band only has to clear the cap. (Deliberately its own constant
  rather than a reach into `COMPACT_CAP_RATIO`, which belongs to the order form's digit-only
  readings and must stay free to move without dragging the editor with it.)
- `railLabelBandDepth(orientation) = RAIL_LABEL_GAP + maxCardPinScale(orientation) * RAIL_LABEL_SIZE
  + RAIL_LABEL_EDGE_GUTTER`.

Reserved at `maxCardPinScale`, the **ceiling**, never at the live `appliedScale` — the identical
rule `cardBandDepth` already follows, and the reason the frame -> fit scale -> card size -> frame
loop stays closed (the module's own T-03J-02 note). The existing frame-invariance suite (test at
`rocker-view-frame.test.ts:1069`) will therefore cover the new bands for free.

The band is added on BOTH sides in BOTH orientations, always OUTSIDE the existing card reserve:

- horizontal top: `topPad` becomes `railLabelBandDepth(orientation)` in place of `PAD_TOP`
- horizontal bottom: `viewH` becomes `baselineY + bottomCardBandDepth(orientation) +
  railLabelBandDepth(orientation)` (the `"none"` branch keeps `BARE_PAD` untouched)
- vertical cross axis: `crossFar` and `crossNear` each gain `+ railLabelBandDepth(orientation)`
  after their existing `+ maxCardWidth + CARD_GUTTER`

Symmetry is the point: with the band added outside the same `CARD_GUTTER` on all four edges, a
title sits the same distance from its cards whichever way the board is turned, by construction
rather than by four separate expressions agreeing.

**The cost, named.** Horizontal loses 2 units at the top (24-unit band replacing `PAD_TOP`'s 26) and
gains a ~24-unit band at the bottom, so the horizontal frame gets roughly 20 units (~6%) taller
against an unchanged 900-unit width. In a wide panel that is a few percent off the drawn board.
That is the price of the title and it is worth paying; the alternative — squeezing the deck title
into the existing `PAD_TOP` and only growing the bottom — would put the two titles at visibly
different distances from their rails, which is exactly the kind of asymmetry a shaper reads as a
mistake.

## 3. Anchors: outside the CEILING-sized rail edge, centred on the middle station

Four new `RockerViewLayout` fields, all computed in every mode so none can ever be `NaN`:

- `railLabelSize = RAIL_LABEL_SIZE * appliedScale` — the SAME scalar that scales the card box and
  `cardType`, so a title and the numbers under it move as one unit and hold a constant on-screen
  size in both orientations (that is what `260830-03j` bought, extended to the titles).
- `deckLabelY = deckRailFarY - RAIL_LABEL_GAP`, where `deckRailFarY = deckTickEndY -
  (effectiveHorizontal ? maxCardHeight : maxCardWidth)` — the deck cards' far edge at the ceiling.
- `bottomLabelY = bottomRailFarY + RAIL_LABEL_GAP + railLabelSize * RAIL_LABEL_CAP_RATIO`, where
  `bottomRailFarY = tickEndY + (effectiveHorizontal ? maxCardHeight : maxCardWidth)`. The cap term
  is what puts the GLYPH edge — not the baseline — `RAIL_LABEL_GAP` clear of the cards, so both
  titles sit the same visual distance from their rails.
- `labelStationX = PAD_X + boardSpan / 2 + (effectiveHorizontal ? 0 : railLabelSize *
  RAIL_LABEL_CAP_RATIO / 2)` — the board's own middle station. The vertical half-cap term exists
  because nose-up the glyph box grows along the station axis; without it both titles would sit a
  hair nose-ward of the middle. Zero in horizontal, so horizontal stays byte-exact on the middle
  station.

Anchoring off the ceiling-sized edge (not the live one) makes the deck title's position
frame-invariant: it never walks when the pin changes. When the live scale is below the ceiling the
title simply carries a little more air, which is the harmless direction.

## 4. Casing and colour: exactly what the drawing already does

`Thickness` and `Rocker` in Title Case, byte-identical to the sidebar and DATASHEET strings. Set in
the drawing's existing label treatment — `fontWeight: 700`, `fontFamily: "var(--font-body)"`,
`letterSpacing: "0.08em"`, `fill="var(--outline-callout-label)"` — the same treatment the station
name rows already use, at `railLabelSize` rather than `nameSize`. No new hex, no new token, no
uppercasing invented for this drawing alone. A title is told apart from a station name by being
larger, being outside the rail, and there being exactly one of it per rail.

## 5. What proves the print path is untouched

The compact branch of the frame is not edited at all, and the viewer draws the titles only under
`callouts === "full"`. On top of that, Task 1 adds a characterisation pin: the exact `viewBox`
strings the order form's own call produces (`stationRails: "compact"`, `fitToBoard: true`) and the
`"none"` path's too, captured by running against the UNMODIFIED module and asserted as literals
afterwards. If any of this work reaches the printed sheet, that test goes red.
