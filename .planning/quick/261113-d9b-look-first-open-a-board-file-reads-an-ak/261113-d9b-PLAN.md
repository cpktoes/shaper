Things measured and rejected — keep them out:
- Reading the tips' rocker and thickness at the very tip: AkuShaper's files round the deck (and the nose's bottom) over
  the last inch to almost nothing, and the app's own 3D export rolls the tail's deck over its last inch too (the
  Shortboard reads 15.9 mm at x = 0 against its 23.8 mm tail-tip setting). Reading there gave 6–9 mm thickness gaps at

  Hence the tip rule below: centre and 12" figures read straight off the file; each tip's figure is the one that follows
  the file best over the stretch from 1" to 12" in from that tip; rocker and thickness closeness measured from 1" in.
- A gap measured without the squash tail's straight end drawn on our side (the first 18–25 mm "gaps" at every square

- A plain all-pairs nearest-distance search (0.5–0.8 ms per comparison, 1–4 s per fit). The pruned search below made the
  whole fit 35–160 ms.
- Fitting by mean distance alone (good on average, 12–25 mm at the tail end). Score = largest + typical.
