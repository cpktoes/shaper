# Quick Task 260823-rt8 — the cyan accent comes back

Reverses the colour decision in `b7fe483`, which replaced the original cyan pair with a single
`#006994` sea blue, while keeping every structural change made since.

## Why it is two tokens and not one

`#00e5ff` is **1.54:1 on white**. It can only ever be a fill. That is why the original scheme shipped
as a pair, and restoring it means restoring both:

| Token | Value | Role | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-surf-accent-cyan` | `#00e5ff` | fills **only**, always with `text-surf-black` | 12.28:1 against black |
| `--color-surf-accent-cyan-ink` | `#00767f` | accent text, and strokes in the drawings | 5.39:1 on white |

`b7fe483` inverted the fill rule when it went dark — accent fills took **white** text. Going back to
a bright cyan inverts it again: every fill returns to black text, and the tail-shape and fin-setup
icons, which sit on those fills, return to a dark stroke.

**Confirmed with the user** (they were offered a single AA-safe cyan-ish token instead and chose the
faithful two-token restoration).

## The role map

Taken from the `b7fe483` diff rather than inferred, since that commit is the authority on which role
each call site had:

- **Fills → `accent-cyan` + `text-surf-black`:** slider range and thumb, fin setup/model toggles (3),
  the tail-shape buttons, the toe-aim table highlight, the three sidebar buttons' hover state, the
  nav's active underline.
- **Text → `accent-cyan-ink`:** fin-controls (3), rail-controls, preset-card, continue-board-card,
  the nav wordmark's hover.
- **Strokes → `accent-cyan-ink`:** outline-viewer's construction lines, fin-viewer's marks and legend,
  the rail plot's `band1`.
- **Icons on a fill → dark stroke again:** `tail-shape-icon`, `fin-setup-icon`.

## Built since b7fe483, so not in its diff — mapped by the same rule

- `--outline-board-fill` — a 6% wash, so a fill: mixes from `accent-cyan`.
- `--outline-construction` — lines on white, so ink.
- `--order-form-shade` — a 7% tint behind muted text. Mixing a *lighter* cyan should raise its
  contrast rather than lower it, but that is a prediction and gets measured.
- `.slider-accent` — range and thumb fills take cyan; the `surf-black` hairline added in `260823-qr3`
  stays.
- The summary print button — a fill, so cyan with black text.
