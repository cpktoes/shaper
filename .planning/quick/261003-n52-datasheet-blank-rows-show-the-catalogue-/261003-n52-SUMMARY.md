# Phase quick-261003-n52 Plan 01: Datasheet Blank rows show the catalogue's own numbers Summary

The ROCKER screen's DATASHEET now reads a blank's own catalogue numbers (its N0, N12, C, T12 and T0 stations, exactly as printed) in the BLANK block, whatever the board's length or where it sits on the blank; Your Board, Foam Off and Thinning Starts read exactly as before.

## What changed on the screen

- **The Longboard (a 9'0" board centred in the US Blanks 9'3"Y)** used to show the foam 1 1/2" and 13 1/2" in from the blank's tips in its BLANK rows. It now reads page 60 of the catalogue, nose tip to tail tip:
  - Rocker `4 5/16"  2 5/16"  0"  1 13/16"  3 1/4"` (was `4"  2 1/8"  0"  1 11/16"  3 1/16"`)
  - Thickness `1 3/8"  2 3/8"  3 1/2"  2 3/8"  1 7/16"`
  - Width `4"  20 5/8"  24 15/16"  17 5/8"  9 1/4"`
- **Unchanged (checked in the browser):** Your Board Rocker `4 3/16"  2 1/8"  0"  1 11/16"  3 3/8"`; Foam Off Bottom `9/16"  3/8"  3/8"  3/8"  5/8"`.
- **The BLANK heading** now says which stations the rows are: `BLANK — US BLANKS 9'3"Y (catalog's N0 · N12 · C · T12 · T0)`. The station note is in ordinary case, the blank's name stays in capitals.
- **The intro line** no longer says the blank's numbers sit at the board's stations: "Your blank's own numbers, as its catalog prints them, then your board's at its five stations — take it to the supplier."
- **Footnotes under the table:** the catalogue line now names all five stations (N0 and T0 are the tips, N12 and T12 are 12 inches in, C is the center); a new line says the Foam Off rows are read off the blank under the board's own five stations, so they are not simply Blank less Your Board when the board is shorter than its blank or slid along it; and a dash line ("A dash (—) means the catalog prints no number there.") shows only when a Blank cell is a dash.
- **Metric:** Rocker and Thickness in whole millimetres (`Rocker (mm)`, `Thickness (mm)`), Width in centimetres to one decimal (`Width (cm)`), as CLAUDE.md Rule 2 asks.
- **Nothing stored changes.** Display only: no saved board, database row, catalogue file, print page or the VIEWER moved.

## How it was built

- `lib/geometry/board-profile.ts`: `CATALOG_STATION_LABELS`, `PrintedBlankValues` and `printedBlankStations(record)` — a pure lookup by label on the board's own copy of the blank, no arithmetic. The profile's blank view now carries `printed`, built from `prepared.record`; the old under-the-board field is gone. FOAM OFF still reads the blank's curve under the board's own five stations.
- `components/rocker/rocker-datasheet.tsx`: the three BLANK rows read `blank.printed` (marks through `formatMarkBare`, width through `formatDimBare`), a null cell is an em dash in the same muted ink; data hooks added to the BLANK, Your Board Rocker and Foam Off rows for the browser tests.
