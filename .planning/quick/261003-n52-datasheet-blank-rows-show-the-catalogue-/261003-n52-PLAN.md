<objective>
Make the ROCKER screen's DATASHEET show a blank's own catalogue numbers in its BLANK block.

The founder reported that the US Blanks 9'3"Y's rocker and thicknesses on the DATASHEET disagree with catalogue page
60. The catalogue data is correct everywhere (the CSV, both databases, the Longboard preset's built-in copy all match
page 60 at every station). What is wrong is where the sheet reads the blank: today the BLANK rows are the blank's curve
read under the BOARD's five stations, and the Longboard preset (a 9'0" board centred in the 9'3"Y) sits 1 1/2" in from
each blank tip, so its BLANK rows are the foam 1 1/2" and 13 1/2" in from the blank's tips, not the catalogue's N0,
N12, C, T12 and T0.

After this plan the BLANK rows read the catalogue's own printed numbers at its own five stations, whatever the board's
length or placement; YOUR BOARD and FOAM OFF read exactly as they do today.

Purpose: a shaper holding the sheet beside the catalogue page (or taking it to the supplier) sees the same numbers the
page prints, so the sheet earns trust instead of raising a "this is wrong" report.

Output: one pure, tested helper in `lib/geometry/board-profile.ts`, the DATASHEET reading it, two browser tests, and
this task's SUMMARY. No geometry maths changes, no database change, no saved board changes, no print change, no push.
</objective>
