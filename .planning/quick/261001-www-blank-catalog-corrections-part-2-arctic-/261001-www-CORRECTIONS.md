# Blank catalog corrections, part 2 — the source list

Quick task 261001-www. Part 1 (quick 261001-v1q) fixed the US Blanks numbers that came from a
mistyped centimetre line. This round applies the three decisions the founder made on 2026-10-01
from the findings sheet (sections 3 and 4), by question card:

1. **Arctic Foam's four 2022 pages — "The inch figures".**
2. **Six US Blanks numbers the catalog prints wrong in both units — "The sister-blank figure, with a note".**
3. **The 7'9"HX and 8'4"SPX litres — "Keep the page's litres"** (so nothing changes for those two).

How the check was made, and where the catalog pages live, is in part 1's
`.planning/quick/261001-v1q-blank-catalog-corrections-part-1-us-blan/261001-v1q-CORRECTIONS.md`.

## A. Arctic Foam — 29 numbers on four pages (10'2" LB, 9'4" G, 9'9" G, 10'6" G)

On these four pages (all dated June 2022) the page prints an inch figure and a centimetre figure
that disagree. When the catalogue file was first built, the centimetre figure was taken and the row
was noted "used catalog cm …; printed inch … is stale from an older page". This round reverses
exactly those notes: **every cell that carried that note now uses the page's inch figure**, and the
note now reads, in the Arctic file's own wording, "catalog cm (… cm = … in) disagrees with inch
(…); used inch".

Why the inch figures are the right ones: with the centimetre figures, each blank's own printed
litres come out about 10% more than its stations can hold (catalogue litres ÷ the width × thickness
of its stations: 1.11–1.13, the four highest in the whole Arctic catalog); with the inch figures
the same ratio is 1.02–1.04, in line with every other Arctic longboard and gun (1.02–1.03). An
older published spec for the 9'9" gun also gives the rocker 12" from the nose as 3 5/16" — next to
the page's inch figure (3 7/8"), far from the centimetre one (4 13/16").

The founder's card named "the 12-inch stations": 24 of the 29 cells. The other five are the same
pages' remaining cells with the same note, changed with them so that no curve is drawn through a
mix of the two sets of figures — the 10'2" LB's width 3" back from the nose (shown on the findings
sheet), the 9'4" G's two tip rockers (6 3/4" and 3 1/8"; the app had 7 1/8" and 3 5/16"), and the
9'9" G's center and nose-tip thickness (4 1/4" and 1 3/4"; the app had 4 3/16" and 1 11/16").

## The machine-readable copy

`261001-www-corrections.json` holds exactly the changes above (for each row: the cells, the old
note and the new note). `261001-www-apply-corrections.py`, run from the repository root with the
JSON's path, applies them to both catalogue files and refuses to write either unless every old
value and every old note is found as listed. Dry run on a copy: 13 lines change in the Arctic file,
6 in the US Blanks file, CRLF kept; the four presets' blanks do not change; the whole unit suite
(3,614 tests) passes on the corrected copy with no test edited.
