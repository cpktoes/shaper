# Quick Task 260823-gc4 — plain station lines on the order form

The station lines added by `260823-ffv` came across with the outline editor's own widepoint
treatment: a rose line (`--outline-widepoint-line`) and two rose knots at the rails
(`--outline-widepoint-knot`). On an otherwise monochrome drafting sheet that reads as an editor
affordance rather than a template marking. Both go, **on this page only** — the outline editor keeps
its colour, where it earns its place next to the drag handles.

## Approach

CSS custom property overrides on the order form's outline panel, alongside the
`--outline-board-fill: transparent` override already there. No new prop on `OutlineViewer`: these
are theming tokens, that component already has five display gates, and the tokens exist precisely
so a consumer can retheme the drawing.

| Token | Order form value | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `--outline-widepoint-line` | `var(--outline-station-line)` | Widepoint station takes the same faint grey as the stringer, centreline and 12" stations. |
| `--outline-widepoint-knot` | `transparent` | The two rail knots disappear. They carry no stroke, so a transparent fill removes them completely. |

**`--outline-widepoint-dash` is deliberately left alone** (`2 3`, against the centreline's
`16 4 4 4`). The viewer's own comment notes that on a board whose widepoint sits near centre the two
lines land within a few pixels of each other, and that colour was what told them apart. With the
colour now matched, the dash is the only thing left doing that job — changing it too would merge the
two lines into one indistinguishable pair.

Extend the existing `style` prop on the `FormBox` wrapping the Deck/Bottom drawings, and update the
