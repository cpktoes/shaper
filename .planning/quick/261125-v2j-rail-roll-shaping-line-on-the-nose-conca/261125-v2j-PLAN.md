## READ THIS FIRST: what the planner measured (2026-11-25, `npx tsx` against main 687259d4, the repo untouched)

**Why 'fish every8' ran 3.5 dots short: neither the letters-per-line estimate nor the spare.** The arithmetic is right. The
Teardrop's note on paper is its head plus its words, 292 letters, 3 lines at 116 letters a line; with the line it is 415
letters, 4 lines (3.58 by the count; a real line would need 139 letters for it to stay at 3, and the estimate is designed to be
close and never short). One more line is 13.8 dots on a phone's sheet (15 on a computer's). The stock Fish with a cut in every
section and 8 channels had 10.26 dots left on its page once its tables had already gone to their own sheet (the Fish draws the
tallest picture of the four stock boards: 161 dots of board room). 10.26 - 13.8 = -3.54. The Blended (169 to 292 letters) and the
Slight (176 to 299) each gain one line too.

**The real gap: the rule has no rung for the page's own parts.** `bottomSheetLayout` only ever moves the tables (then their notes,
then splits them). It assumes the picture, the sections and the How to shape notes always fit once the tables have left. When
they do not, the browser quietly shrinks the picture (`min-h-0` on `[data-bottom-sheet-part="top"]`), labels and all. No
existing check sees it: the unit tests check only the four stock boards, and the e2e fit sweep only its five boards.

**It already happens today, without any roll.** Over the "split's reach" sweep (4 shapes x 20 lengths 4'0" to 12'0" x three
widths x the 11 test bottoms x 2 systems, 5,280 layouts with a cut), 94 layouts leave less than the 4-dot hairline
(`PAGE.spare`), worst -20.74 dots. Every one is `every8` (a cut in every section and 8 channels) on a board wide for its length,
drawn at or near the 190-dot cap: for example the Fish at 4'6" x 25", the Shortboard at 6'0" x 25", the Longboard at 5'6" x 22.5".
Every other bottom fits on every board. The four stock boards as they open all have at least 10.26 dots spare, so none moves.

**The fix the planner prototyped (the picture's room, by whole dots):** the picture is the page's one part made to give way, so the
rule gives it. On the stock Fish every8 with the line, the board's room goes 161 to 153 dots (95%, 5.02 spare); on the 190-dot
boards with the line, 190 to 153 (80.5%); with no roll, those boards go 190 to 166 (87.4%). Only the board's own room shrinks;
the gutter names, the labels and the rows under it stay full size, and `topViewFrame` already fits a board to a smaller room (it
is how the 190-dot cap draws a short wide board). Moving the How to shape notes to another sheet instead was rejected: it splits
a cut's shaping note from its drawing and adds a sheet for a few dots.

**Which tests already read these surfaces.** `e2e/bottom-guide.spec.ts` reads every card's `how` straight from
`BOTTOM_GUIDE_WORDS`, so the GUIDE's line is checked with no edit. `e2e/summary-bottom-page.spec.ts` checks the notes of a
Deep Single Concave and Channels only (no nose concave), so it stays green. `e2e/summary-planing.spec.ts`'s "BOTTOM CONTOURS page
fits its sheet" sweep measures real printed sheets and asserts the picture is never squeezed. **No baseline screenshot covers
either surface**: `e2e/desktop-baseline.spec.ts` shoots TEMPLATE, ROCKER, RAILS, VOLUME, FINS and 3D only.
