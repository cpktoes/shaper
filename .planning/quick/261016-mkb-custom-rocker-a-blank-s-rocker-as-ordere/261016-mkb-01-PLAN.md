- **D-01 — One notation, app-wide: US Blanks'.** `-1/4"N18" +1/8"T36" +1/8"T18"` — nose changes first, then tail;
  `+` adds rocker at that tip, `-` takes it away; a figure after N or T is where the bend starts, whole inches in
  from that tip; no figure = from the centre; changes stack. The note is always in inches, in Metric too (it is the
  maker's order code, like the blank's name). Every drawn, tabled or slid figure follows Imperial/Metric (Rule 2).
- **D-02 — Every blank is named the same way:** the name, then its rocker on its own line, `Natural` written out
  (`9'4"B` / `Natural`, or `9'4"B` / `-1/4"N18" +1/8"T36"`). One-line form `9'4"B Natural`; with the maker for the
  order form (`US Blanks 9'4"B Natural`). This plan builds the helpers; no screen uses them yet.
- **D-03 — Tables: Natural Rocker over Custom Rocker** (plan 02/03 draw them). This plan supplies
  `customPrintedStations`: the five catalogue stations as ordered, printed + bend, exact, a printed dash a dash.
- **D-05 — The change rows** (plan 02 draws them; this plan supplies the rules): each change is Rocker (1/16",
  1 mm in Metric, −2" to +2") beside Bend starts (whole inches, 3" in to the centre). At most two changes per end.
  A second change on an end must start nearer the tip than the first (its slider capped one inch nearer; moving the
  first pushes the second ahead of it). Two changes stack: the first in full and the second on top of it, so
  `+1/2"N +1/2"N18"` lifts the nose tip exactly 1" above natural.
- **D-06 — No rocker value goes negative:** "A negative rocker where the nose or tail would be below the centerpoint
  is not a realistic board." Each Rocker slider stops at the floor that keeps every part of the bent blank at or
  above the blank's low point; +2" stays the top.
- **D-09 — Every custom rocker in use is a saved blank of the shaper's own**, starred the moment it is saved
  (plan 02 saves and stars; this plan supplies the storage). Signed in, the account's list is the list; signed out,
  the browser keeps it. Joining a browser's list into the account at sign-in is NOT in this task (ve9 part 2).
- **D-10 — Switching to a different blank clears the custom rocker and starts from natural.** Remove This Blank
  keeps the board's figures (today's rule; a tip below 0 clamps to 0 per `ROCKER_LIFT_RANGE_IN`, as today).
- **D-12 — Pictures before every merge, go-live on the founder's word.** Nothing migrates production or deploys
  in this plan.

**Planner's choices (measured at plan time; the SUMMARY carries each to the founder to confirm or overrule).** Where
each lives across the task: P-1, P-2, P-5 … P-10 here; P-3 (RockerViewer or its own drawing) and P-11 … P-18 in plan
02; P-4 (the screen tile on an upright phone) and P-19 … P-23 in plan 03.
- **P-1 — Metric amounts.** In Metric the Rocker slider steps 1 mm and the note rounds the amount to the nearest
  1/16" while the drawing and tables show the stored millimetres. Measured: every whole-millimetre amount from 1 mm
  up rounds to at least 1/16" (1 mm = 0.63 of a sixteenth), so the note never reads `0"`; the note and the stored
  figure differ by at most half a sixteenth (0.79 mm); at a Metric floor the note can read up to 1/32" past the floor
  (a −44 mm floor writes `-1 3/4"`, −44.45 mm). The alternative — snapping the stored value to the sixteenth — makes
  a Metric slider jump 1.6 mm a step.
- **P-2 — Bend starts in Metric moves in whole inches** (the bend point is part of the maker's order code) and its
  label reads centimetres to one decimal, like a Thinning Start (`45.7 cm in`), or `At the centre`.
- **P-5 — Caps: 100 saved blanks per account, and 100 in a browser.** Measured: the largest saved blank (the
  biggest catalogue copy with four changes) is 2,636 bytes of JSON, so 100 is about 257 KB — about 5% of a browser's
  ~5 MB of local storage. A 101st save is refused with: `You have 100 saved blanks — remove one in Pick a Blank to
  save another.`
- **P-6 — The centre is half the blank's length** (`L/2`), the app's centre everywhere else (`blankStationOf`,
  `boardOnBlank`), not the printed `C` station. Measured: the `C` station sits off `L/2` on 33 of 219 pickable
  blanks, by at most 0.005"; on the sketch's two blanks (9'4"B, 6'3"RP) they are equal, so the golden agrees.
- **P-7 — Floors on the real catalogue.** From the centre, −2" is reachable at the nose on 184 of 219 pickable blanks
  and at the tail on 155. Five US Blanks blanks (8'9"Y, 8'9"YX, 8'8" EPS, 8'8"X EPS, 9'6" EPS) cannot be lowered at
  all from the centre — their natural rocker is printed 0 at more than one station, so any lowering from the centre
  dips below the low point; their slider stops at 0 with the Floor hint, and from 12" or 24" in they can be lowered.
  The 10'4"A SUP EPS tail stops at −1/8"; Marko's Wakesurf Regular and 5'3" Foil at −1/4" nose, −1/16" tail.
- **P-8 — The low point.** Measured: the levelled centre is 0 on all 219 pickable blanks (the app's square-root rule
  anchors every blank's low at its lowest printed station, which is `C` on every one), and within the floors no
  change ever moves the low point (a +2" change from the centre at either end moved it on no blank by more than
  0.001 mm). The bent curve is still re-levelled on its true low point by `levelCurve`, as every blank is — never
  clamped — so a hand-made saved spec beyond a floor still draws honestly (probed: `-2"T` on the 6'3"RP, past its
  −1 3/4" floor, re-levels with its tail tip on the flat).
- **P-9 — The second change's room.** While an end has a second change, its first change's Bend starts runs 4" to the
  centre, so the second always has 3" to sit at; adding a second change to an end whose first sits at 3" moves the
  first to 4". A new second change starts at +1/8" from 18" in (or the nearest allowed point), the sketch's default.
- **P-10 — The reader is strict.** `parseRockerNote` reads a note only when every non-space character is consumed by
  changes and no end has more than two; measured, this agrees with the sketch's own looser reader on all 282 listing
  lines (the only line the two rules judge differently, `-1/8"N12" -1/8"N6" -1/4"N3"`, is refused by both for its
  three nose changes). The bare words `Natural` and `NAT`, and an empty note, read as the natural rocker.
