<objective>
Give shapers an Off-Rail fine tuner on FINS, as the founder decided on 2026-10-18 (D-01 to D-03, with D-02b for the quad rears). What changes for a shaper:

1. **Side and front fins.** In Advanced, under Aft/Forward position, a new Off-Rail slider nudges the pair in toward the stringer or out toward the rail from where the chosen template puts them. Its label always reads where the fins end up, e.g. `Off-Rail — 1 3/16"` (Metric `30 mm`). This works on the Thruster (all four models), the Twin (all three templates), the 2+1 side bites and the quad fronts.
2. **Quad rear fins placed from the stringer** (Basic - Spread, McKee SB/Gun, McKee Longboard). A second Off-Rail slider in Rear Fins moves both rears together. Moving them in from the rail brings them closer together by the same amount. The drawing and DATA keep showing the spread, now moved, and a McKee rear toe aimed by formula re-aims to the new spread. Basic - Off-Rail keeps its own slider exactly as it is.
3. **Limits.** The front and side fins are held between 7/8" and 2" from the rail (22 mm to 51 mm). The quad rears placed from the stringer move up to 1/2" either way from where the template puts them, never closer than 7/8" to the rail. A template that already sits outside its window keeps its own figure as the limit on that side, so the slider's resting point never moves a board.
4. **Nothing moves until a shaper moves it.** Every saved board, preset and print is exactly as today until a slider is touched. Reset Advanced and a change of Fin Setup put the fins back on the template.
5. **Wording.** Both Aft/Forward position labels lose their bracketed note that the off-rail stays the same.

Purpose: the founder: "fins need an off rail fine tuner." A shaper trusts these numbers enough to cut foam to them, so the tuned figure is calculated in `lib/geometry/fins.ts` and shown everywhere from that one place.
Output: three commits of code and tests in the executor's worktree, plus a plain-English SUMMARY (not committed). No database change, no migration, no deploy, no push.

<founder_decisions>
Locked on 2026-10-18. Implement them exactly as written.
- **D-01 NUDGE, not set.** The slider is built like Aft/Forward position. It stores a signed offset (Mm) in FinAdvancedSpec, defaulting to 0, so every saved board (which has no value for it) opens exactly where it is today. The label shows the distance the fins END UP from the rail (`Off-Rail — 1 3/16"`; whole millimetres in Metric). It resets with Reset Advanced and with a change of fin setup, exactly as the position offsets do.
- **D-02 RANGE (front and side fins).** The fins' resulting distance from the rail is held between 7/8" and 2". Today's `clampOffRail` (1" to 2") and the Basic - Off-Rail slider stay exactly as they are. If a template's own figure is outside 7/8"–2", the window must still contain it, so a zero nudge is always reachable and never moves a board.
- **D-02b RANGE (stringer-placed quad rears), the founder's amendment of 2026-10-18 for D-03(b) only.** The rears' resulting distance from the rail is held within ±1/2" of the template's own figure t, and never closer than 7/8" to the rail. The window is [max(7/8", t − 1/2"), t + 1/2"]. When t itself is under 7/8", the window still contains t: lo = min(t, max(7/8", t − 1/2")). So a zero nudge is always reachable and never moves a board. The slider ends use the same outward rounding to 1/16" / whole mm as the front, so zero stays on a step. The front and side fins keep exactly D-02.

<planner_decisions>
These are the questions the founder left to the planner, now settled. Name them in the SUMMARY.
- **Rear toe follows the nudged spread.** The McKee SB/Gun and Longboard rear toe is `aimToe(baseLenRear, spread, L − 12 / L − 18)`: the fin's line aimed at a point near the nose. Moving the rears closer together needs less toe to keep aiming at that same point, so the formula's own behaviour is right. The nudged spread feeds it, and a toe the shaper has set by hand (`rearToeOverride`) is never changed. No front toe reads the off-rail or spread: every front toe is a fixed figure or `mckeeFrontToe(L)`, which depends only on board length. So nothing else re-aims.
- **Aft/Forward wording: drop the bracket.** Both labels become `Aft/Forward position — <distance>`, the same as the center fin's. Three reasons. (1) The new Off-Rail row sits directly under the slider and shows the figure live, so the words add nothing on the front pair. (2) On the quad rears placed from the stringer the words were never true: those rears keep their spread as they slide, so their distance from the rail changes with the board's width. The new rear Off-Rail row would visibly contradict them. (3) Once off-rail can move, "unchanged" reads as "can't be changed".
- **Slider shape.** The slider holds the nudge, as Aft/Forward position does. Its two ends are the window, not a fixed ±range, so there is no dead travel where the thumb moves and the label doesn't. The ends are rounded OUTWARD to the slider's own step: 1/16" in Imperial, a whole millimetre in Metric. That keeps zero exactly on a step (Base UI snaps from the minimum) and makes each window's ends reachable in both systems (front: 7/8"/2", 22 mm/51 mm). The engine's hold absorbs the last part-step. Because the ends are the window, the thumb's resting position shows where the template sits within it.
- **Hints:** left `Closer to rail`, right `Closer to stringer`. These are plain directions; no claim about how the board rides.
- **Modified:** the DATA tab's `· Modified` shows when a tuner actually moves the fins (the held figure differs from the template), not merely when a stored value is non-zero.
- **Model notes stay as written.** The MODEL INFO and order-form notes ("Off-rail 1.125"", "off-rail 1¼"") describe the published template, as they already do when Aft/Forward is moved.
</planner_decisions>
