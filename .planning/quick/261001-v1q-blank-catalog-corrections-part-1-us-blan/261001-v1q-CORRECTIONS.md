# Blank catalog corrections, part 1 — the source list

Quick task 261001-v1q. This is the list the founder's todo asked for ("the founder lists each wrong
blank with the right numbers and where they come from"): every change below names the catalog page
it comes from and what that page prints.

## How the list was made (2026-10-01)

Every number the app holds for the 162 blanks was compared with the makers' own catalog pages — the
three PDFs the catalogue files were first built from (US Blanks Catalog Letter Size June 2025 v2,
Arctic Foam 2024 Catalog, Marko Foam 2026 catalog; they live in the founder's Downloads folder, not
in this repository).

- **Marko Foam (28 blanks):** every number matches its page (read by eye, all 30 pages).
- **Arctic Foam (33 blanks):** read straight from the PDF's text. 29 match; four June-2022 pages
  (10'2" LB, 9'4" G, 9'9" G, 10'6" G) are a separate decision — NOT in this task.
- **US Blanks (101 blanks):** each drawing prints every measurement twice, inches with centimetres
  underneath. Both lines were read by machine twice (whole page, then one enlarged crop per number)
  and compared with the app's number: 3,631 of 3,855 agreed all three ways, the remaining 224 were
  read by eye from the page.

**The cause.** The catalogue file's US Blanks numbers were taken from the centimetre line. On the
rows below the catalog's centimetre figure is a slip — often a copy of the neighbouring row's — and
the inch figure is right. Each was checked two more ways before being listed: against the curve
through the blank's own neighbouring stations, and against the sister blank (the same blank made
thicker or thinner by a fixed amount) or the page's own headline where there is one.

## Left alone on purpose (not in this task)

- **Arctic Foam's four 2022 pages** — the founder's call (the page's inch and cm figures disagree at
  the 12-inch stations; the blanks' own litres favour the inch figures).
- **Six US Blanks numbers printed wrong in both units** (6'2"A, 10'6"AX, 6'4"EAX, 8'4"SPX,
  7'2"X EPS thickness; 8'0"H rocker) and the two copied litres figures (7'9"HX, 8'4"SPX) — the
  founder's call whether the app follows the page or the sister blank.
- **Twelve rows where the page's inch label is the slip and the app is already right** (5'9"P N6,
  6'4"MB N18, 6'9"EAX N0 thickness — found after the plan was written, by reading every "13/16
  versus 1 3/16"-style label by eye — 7'11"A N6, 8'2"A, 8'2"AX, 8'9"Y N48, 9'2"A C, 9'9"B,
  9'8"X EPS, 11'8"BG T36, 10'4"B SUP EPS) — no number changes; a note on those rows is a possible
  follow-up.
- The 10'0"T's name (it is the 10'10"T — the page's title has the slip), the two catalog addresses
  that repeat a neighbour's (6'9"EAX, 11'8"BG), lengths and deck lengths where the page's inch and
  cm differ by a quarter inch or less (6'7"P, 6'10"RH, 11'2"C; the app does not use deck length),
  the rocker the catalog does not print at the 48-inch stations of five long blanks (stored as 0),
  and the Marko stations placed on the 12-inch grid.
