### 1. What to port from the trial diff, and what not

Port (renamed to railRoll, and every `process.env` switch removed):

  SPIKE_GATE_MM=1 SPIKE_ONE=1`, which is ONE start (the Blended Nose Concave from 1/16" with a 1/8" roll), never the

Not built: `acrossShape` / `spikeSec` (the power curve; out of scope), `SPIKE_ROLL=grid` (the roll in the refine's grid: it
flipped testLB's reading order, spike step 5), the smoothness freedoms, the `SPIKE_READ` readings and `spikeRailRise` /

  keeps the polish's own promise ("names are never changed") and its existing tests untouched.
