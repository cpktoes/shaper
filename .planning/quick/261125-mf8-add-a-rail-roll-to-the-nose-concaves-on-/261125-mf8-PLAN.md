### 1. The roll's maths, as measured (port of `trial-code.diff`, the `lib/geometry/bottom.ts` hunks)

Port: the stored key and the read key; `EDGE_ROLL_SMOOTHNESS` (0.6, the Rolled Vee's own Smoothness 60) as
`RAIL_ROLL_SMOOTHNESS`; `edgeRollShare`, split into its two factors (along the board, across it) so the import's fast scorer
can call the very same two functions in Task 2; and the two edited lines of `cutFoamOffParts`. Do NOT port: `acrossShape`,
`spikeSec`, or any environment switch.

- **Across** (at station `s`, `y` out from the stringer): `W` is `board.railAt(s)`; the edge `C` is the reference rail less
  Off Rail, never below 0 (`refAt(board, rule, s) - offRail`), and never the Teardrop's closing width. Nothing unless `W` is
  wider than `C` and `y` is outside `C`; outside it, `rise` of the share of the way from `C` to `W` (capped at 1) at
  `RAIL_ROLL_SMOOTHNESS`.
- **Along**: nothing behind the concave's End; the whole of it at or ahead of Deepest At (so ahead of the concave's Start
  too, to the nose tip), and also when Deepest At and End are within `FADE_GUARD`; between End and Deepest At,
  `smoothstep` of the share of the way from End to Deepest At.
- **The lift**: the roll times (along times across), added to a nose concave's `lift`. Where the concave's own fade is 0
  (ahead of its Start) the cut still returns that lift, with no concave work. A cut whose Depth is 0, or a station with no
  rail, takes nothing at all, roll included (today's two early returns stay first).
- **Which cut**: a Nose cut whose kind is a single concave. That is exactly the three names of D-01, so one rule
  (`section` is nose and `cutKind` is single) decides it everywhere: the maths, the row on BOTTOM, the board reader.
