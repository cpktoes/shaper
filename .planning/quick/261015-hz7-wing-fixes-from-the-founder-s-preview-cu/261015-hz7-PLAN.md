  - `WingLock` becomes `"front-trailing" | "rear-trailing" | null`. Wing 1 (index 0) may hold only
    `"front-trailing"`; wing 2 (index 1) only `"rear-trailing"`. A lock follows that fin group's trailing edge
    (`FinMark.offTail`) plus the wing's offset.
  - A quad's rear fins are the marks with `role === "rear"` (side ±1); no other setup has one. A single has no front

  - A stored lock the fin setup cannot honour (a rear lock after switching the quad to a thruster) is inert: the wing
    keeps its stored station and the screen treats it as placed by hand, exactly as a front lock on a single does
    today. Switching back to a quad brings it back. That is a placement fact, so the resolver handles it, not the
    schema (the brief's saved-board section).
- **D2 — Wing 2 behind wing 1 (item 6).** "Wing 2 has to be closer to the tail than wing 1, and can't get any closer
  than 1/2" or 2x the run along the stringer from wing 1 (whichever is larger)." The orchestrator's reading:
  - wing 2's position (its apex) ≤ wing 1's position − gap, gap = max(1/2", 2 × wing 1's run). Twice the run keeps a
    flat of at least one run between wing 1's inner corner and wing 2's apex.
  - Pure geometry in `lib/geometry`, one function the resolver, `placedWings`, the sliders and the grab points share,
    applied AFTER the locks resolve: wing 2's station = clamp(its own target, 2", wing 1's resolved station − gap).
    The 2" floor wins when the two disagree (planner's choice: a wing never leaves the board's 2" minimum).
  - Wing 1 placed by hand with a wing 2 present: its slider minimum is 2" + gap (never above its top).
  - If wing 1 (locked, dragged, or slid) moves tail-ward or its run grows, wing 2 is pushed toward the tail, never past
    2". A pushed wing 2 placed by hand stays where it was pushed once anything writes the wings list (the store's P2
    rule: the stored station is the one last shown); a locked wing 2 springs back to its own target when there is room.
  - "+ Add wing 2" is disabled with a one-line note when wing 1 − gap < 2".
  - A wing 2 locked to the rear fins whose target breaks the rule is held at the limit, with a one-line note under its
    offset. Its offset slider keeps its full ±3" (planner's choice: the slider and the grab point set the offset, the
    resolver holds the station, so the two always agree).
  - The orchestrator's note to keep: this reverses the old default (wing 2 was added 4 1/2" nose-ward). Wing 1 is now
    the forward wing (the front fins, or a stinger), wing 2 the aft one (a quad's rear fins, or by hand).
