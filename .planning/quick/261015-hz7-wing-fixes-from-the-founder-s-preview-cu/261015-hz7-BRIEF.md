   to the front fin, wing 2 a stinger at 30 9/16"):

2. **"Only wing 1 gets lock to front fins option."**
3. **"Wing 2 can lock to rear [fins] if it's a quad setup, otherwise no lock."** In the fin marks, a quad's rear

6. **"Wing 2 has to be closer to the tail than wing 1, and can't get any closer than 1/2" or 2x the run along
   the stringer from wing 1 (whichever is larger)."** Orchestrator's reading: wing 2's apex (its position)
   ≤ wing 1's apex − max(1/2", 2 × wing 1's run). Wing 1's run is the step leaving wing 1 toward the tail, so
   twice it keeps a flat of at least one run between wing 1's inner corner and wing 2's apex. This reverses
   today's default (wing 2 is added 4 1/2" NOSE-ward of wing 1) — wing 1 is now the forward wing (front fin or
   a stinger), wing 2 the aft one (a quad's rear fins, or by hand).
   - Enforce it as pure geometry in lib/geometry (a function the resolver and the sliders share), applied
     AFTER the locks resolve: wing 2's resolved station = clamp(its own target, 2", wing 1's resolved station −
     gap). Wing 1 hand-placed with a wing 2 present: its slider minimum is 2" + gap. If wing 1 (locked, or
     dragged by its grab point) moves tail-ward or its run grows, wing 2 is pushed toward the tail, never past
     2". When wing 1 sits too close to the tail for a wing 2 to fit (wing 1 − gap < 2"), "+ Add wing 2" is
     disabled with a one-line note. A wing 2 locked to the rear fins whose target breaks the rule is held at the
     limit, with a one-line note under its offset.
   - The grab points obey the same limits (plan 02's `solveWingDrag` in `lib/geometry/outline-drag.ts`).
   - `nextWing` (lib/geometry/wings.ts): wing 2 is added BEHIND wing 1 — on a quad locked to the rear fins'
     trailing edge (offset 0, then the rule clamps it), otherwise by hand at wing 1 − max(gap, 3"), clamped to
     ≥ 2". Its default depth is 3/16" (item 7).
7. **Fin-box depth warning — REPLACED by the founder's scaling rule (2026-10-15, second message):** "Apply
   Scaling Rule: If Wing Depth is > 3/16", recommend a distance using the formula Distance Behind Trailing Edge =
   (2 × Wing Depth) − 0.25", but that they should check their fin box specs also." And: "drop the default wing to
   3/16"."
   - Recommended distance `D(depth) = 2 × depth − 1/4"` (3/8" deep → 1/2"; 1/2" → 3/4"; 1" → 1 3/4"). A pure
     function in lib/geometry with tests, inch constants through `inchesToMm`.
   - The warning shows, per wing, when depth > 3/16" AND its apex sits behind a SIDE fin group's trailing edge
     (role front or rear, side ±1 — the centre fin is on the stringer, nowhere near the rail) by less than D:
     `0 ≤ TE − apex < D`. A wing ahead of the trailing edge, or far behind it (a stinger), shows nothing; at
     exactly D behind, nothing. Boundary tests: depth exactly 3/16" → none; apex exactly at TE → warning; exactly
     D behind → none; just under D → warning. CONFIRMED by the founder (2026-10-15): "at or behind the fin mark
     only is fine for the warning, since the fin position jumps in based on the new narrower tail curve when the
     apex is above the trailing edge at all." — a wing ahead of the trailing edge already moves the fin inboard
     onto the stepped rail (the fins-listen-to-the-physical-rail rule), so it needs no warning.
   - Wording, in the sidebar right under that wing's Depth row, in the warning ink (only ever for warnings):
     `Modern fin boxes: for a wing this deep, set its apex at least 1/2" behind the fin's trailing-edge mark
     (twice the depth less 1/4"). Check your fin box's spec too.` — the distance computed and formatted as a MARK
     through units.ts (Metric: whole mm, e.g. `13 mm`). Decide whether the close-up also marks it; the sidebar line
     is required.
   - **Defaults:** the first wing is added **3/16" deep** (the founder's ruling); the second wing too, so neither
     opens with a warning.

8. **Position limit — the founder (2026-10-15, second message):** "the wing's position off tail should be limited
   by center or the widepoint, whichever is closer to the tail." The position range becomes 2" to
   min(length / 2, the widepoint station) — `wingPositionRangeIn` and every clamp that uses the centre today
   (`lockedWingStation`, the resolver, `nextWing`, the grab-point solve, the sliders). On a board whose widepoint
   sits behind centre (negative WP offset, e.g. the default board's −1/2"), the top is the widepoint station.
   Saved wings beyond it are clamped at resolve time, never rewritten on open.

## Saved boards (rule 7 of lib/models/design-snapshot.ts)
`WingLock` today is `"front-trailing" | "front-leading" | null`. New values: wing 1 `"front-trailing" | null`,
wing 2 `"rear-trailing" | null`. The schema accepts all three strings; on read, `"front-leading"` reads as
`"front-trailing"`, a lock the wing's slot may not hold (a front lock on wing 2, a rear lock on wing 1, a rear
lock on a non-quad — the last is a placement fact, so handle it in the resolver, not the schema) reads as
`null` keeping the stored station, and wings stored out of order are handled by item 6's clamp at resolve time.
Opening a board never rewrites a stored wing (rule 7). Saved-board tests must cover each normalisation.
