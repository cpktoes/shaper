<objective>
The .brd file leaves a board's channels out. On Export .brd File, a board with channels is written from the same
bottom with the channels switched off — every concave, vee and belly kept — so AkuShaper and Shape3D open the board as
it stands before the channels are cut. The 3D page says so in one line under the export button, only on a board with
channels. The drawing on screen keeps its channels.

Purpose: the founder's ruling (2026-11-12): "It doesn't appear that either handle channels well, esp in the free teir.
what if we just exclude channels from the .brd output?" Five rounds of sample files showed both programs blend a few
smooth cross-sections, so a straight, sharp-walled channel does not travel well in a .brd.
Output: two pure functions in lib/export/brd.ts with tests, a changed export tap, one line of copy, browser checks.
</objective>
