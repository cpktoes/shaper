<objective>
Take the rotate icon off the viewer on every touch device, at any screen width — not just on a
narrow one.

Purpose: the founder, testing on his phone, wrote it plainly: "In phone horizontal, the view
vertical button is visible but does nothing. Lets hide/remove it from view." He is right on both
counts. On TEMPLATE and ROCKER the little rotate icon is hidden only when the window is narrower
than 820px. A phone held sideways is wider than that — 844px on an iPhone 14, 863px on a Pixel 7 —
so the icon comes back. But on any touch device the board's direction follows the device's own
direction: hold the phone upright and the board stands up, lay it sideways and the board lies down.
The button's own setting is never read in that case, so tapping it genuinely does nothing. A
control that cannot do anything is worse than no control — a shaper taps it, nothing moves, and now
they are wondering what else on the screen is lying to them.

The same dead button appears on a touchscreen laptop with a wide screen. Phase 9 knew about this
and wrote it down as a known, unaddressed edge case (09-02-SUMMARY.md, "the residual edge case (a
touchscreen laptop with a landscape screen) is rare and not addressed"). The founder has now hit it
on the commonest device there is, so it stops being residual.

Output: one extra hide rule on one button in each of the two editors, the comments around those
buttons made true again, one honest clause added to each of the two places that describe the
width/pointer rule, and one browser test that holds a phone sideways and proves the icon is gone.
Two commits. No change to any component under components/ui/, nothing under lib/geometry/ touched,
no baseline screenshot regenerated, and — on a mouse-driven desktop — no rendered pixel different.
</objective>

edited here.
