## What was checked, and the exact results

- **Layout pin (pure arithmetic, 5,856 layouts: the four presets with 11 bottoms plus flat, and four shapes from 4'0" to 12'0" at the narrowest, stock and widest width, Imperial and Metric):** 5,856 before, 5,856 after; identical 5,208; different 648. The 648 are exactly the 36 boards at 11'6 1/16", 11'8" and 12'0" (138 1/16", 140" and 144" at three widths on four shapes) with a layout-mark column, nine bottoms each in both systems — each changed only by "rows tight" true to false, sheets 2 to 3, and the old "spare −26" to "no single shared sheet". Panel / belly / flat bottoms at those lengths and everything up to 11'6" are identical.
- **Print spread, 160 real PDFs from the app (20 boards x Imperial/Metric x Rail Band page off/on x Letter/A4):**
  - the 16 PDFs of the two marked 12'0" boards changed as intended (one page more: 4 to 5, or 5 to 6 with the Rail Band page);
  - the 12'0" board with no layout marks (`lb-144x31-panel`) is identical page for page;
  - of the other 144 PDFs, 135 are byte-identical to today's in at least one of up to five prints, and the other 9 (8 on A4) differ only in the size of the **axis numbers on the plan drawings** (page 1 and the Rail Band page, 9 pt versus 11 pt) — the same difference shows on boards with no bottom at all (flat boards), repeats differently between two prints of the same board, and no BOTTOM CONTOURS page is among the differences. That is not this change (see the SUMMARY).
- **Page counts of the marked 12'0" boards (Rail Band page unticked / ticked):** 5 / 6 pages, on Letter and A4, Imperial and Metric. Before: 4 / 5. The 10'0" boards (`lb-120x22.5`), the 11'6" boards (`lb-138x...`), the 4'0" board and the 12'0" board with no layout marks: unchanged (4 / 5).

## What I chose (for your call)

1. **One table a sheet, rather than both tables broken at a row ("continued").** Each table reads straight down from Tail to Nose tip, no "continued" words, rows at today's height, each with its own reading notes. If you would rather keep each station's depths and marks on one sheet, breaking both at a row also fits.
2. **The split starts exactly where today's last squeeze fails:** a board over 11'6" with at least one layout-mark column. Every board up to 11'6", and any board with no layout marks, prints as it does today.
3. **Page titles:** "Bottom Contours · Depths" and "Bottom Contours · Layout Marks"; the line under the paper says "plus three Bottom Contours pages".
4. **Not changed, your call:** boards from 11'0" to 11'6" print no reading notes on their tables sheet (10'0": one or three of three; 10'6": one of three) because the sheet drops notes before it shrinks rows. After this fix a 12'0" board prints every note and an 11'6" board none.
5. **Flagged, not ours:** on page 3 of a short, wide board with a cut in every section and 8 channels (5'0"–6'0" x 25", and the new 4'0" boards and 8'0" x 31"), the fit rule's page count is 3.6 to 20.7 dots short, so the picture from above is the part that gives way. No row or note is involved.
