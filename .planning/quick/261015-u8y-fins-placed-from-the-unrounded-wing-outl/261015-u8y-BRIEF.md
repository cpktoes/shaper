So:
1. **Today's corner rounding is unchanged** — the drawn board, the WINGS close-up, the prints, the rack,

3. A board with no wings, or whose wings all have radius 0, places its fins exactly as today (byte-identical).

## The diagnosis (done by an investigation agent this session — confirm, don't redo)

- Today's rounding fits a circle of radius R into each corner: tangent length `t = R·tan(θ/2)`
  (`lib/geometry/wings.ts:319`); the outer arc starts at `S + t`, nose-ward of the wing's position S
  (`:332`), the inner arc lands at `S − run − t` (`:335`); `shareFromShape` (`:376-389`) is 0 only
  nose-ward of `S + t`, so at S the rail is already pulled in by `R(1 − √(1 − tan²(θ/2)))`.
- A locked wing's S sits exactly at its fin group's trailing edge (`lib/geometry/wing-lock.ts:83-87`),
  so the fin sits inside the rounded zone. Fins measured in from the rail beside them move in with it:
  `lib/geometry/fins.ts:704` (front fins), `:713-715` (Basic off-rail rears), `:845` (twin), `:894`
  (side bites); McKee quad rears (`:677`, spread from the 12" width) keep their position but their gap
  to the rail shrinks. Measured, 3/16"-deep wings, radius only changed: rear fins −0.0021 / −0.0042 /
  −0.0083 / −0.0167 / −0.0240" at radius 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1, 1 7/16"; front fins (1/4" run) −0.0072 /
  −0.0144 / −0.0253"; the Mid-length quad with a 3/8" × 1/2" wing at radius 15/16": −0.054". An
  UNLOCKED wing at the same station moves them identically — it's where the fin sits relative to the
  rounding, not the lock. Off-tail and toe never changed.
- A second, smaller path: the fade's anchor is the inner tangent point (`wings.ts:435`, `:446-447`), so a
  bigger radius also narrows the rail everywhere behind the wing (centre fin −0.0056"), and it feeds the
  12" width when a step or fade covers the 12" station (the only loop: fins → wing → 12" width → fins;
  settles in two passes). With radius 0 for the fins, both paths read the sharp step — so radius has
  NO effect on any fin number.
- Investigation scripts (the app's own pipeline, reusable for the proof):
  `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-kontoes-Code-shaper/a943832e-bb83-4a90-a834-e556695848ff/scratchpad/wing-radius/copy/scratch-q1-fins.ts`
  and `scratch-q1-extra.ts` (`resolveWingStations` → `buildOutline` → `deriveEffectiveFins` →
  `computeFinPlacement`), outputs in `../q1-extra.txt`. Picture: `../pictures/7-why-the-fins-move.png`.
