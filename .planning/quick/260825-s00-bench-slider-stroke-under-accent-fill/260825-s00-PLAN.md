<already_diagnosed>
Do not re-derive any of this. It was measured in the browser against the running app.

**The bench**, three rules at lines ~171-173 of `colour-bench.html`:

- `.s-track` — 6px tall, `1px solid var(--surf-ink)` border, `border-radius:99px`,
  `background:var(--surf-well)`, `position:relative`
- `.s-range` — absolutely positioned, `width:62%`, accent background, full pill radius, and
  an `inset` whose top/bottom/left values are negative
- `.s-thumb` — absolutely positioned at `left:62%`, 13px square with `margin:-7px 0 0 -7px`,
  ink border, accent background

The bug is `.s-range`'s negative inset: it pulls the fill out over the track's own border on
three sides and paints over it.

**The live app** — measured via `getComputedStyle` on the real `data-slot` elements at
`/design/outline`. The styling comes from `.slider-accent` in `app/globals.css` (~line 648),
which deliberately overrides the shadcn primitive:

- track: `--surf-well` background, `1px solid --surf-ink` on all four sides, 6px, full radius
- range: `--surf-accent` background, **`border-width: 0`, `border-radius: 0px`** — it sits
  INSIDE the track's border, so the track's stroke reads around the whole bar
- thumb: `--surf-accent` background, `1px solid --surf-ink`, **12px x 12px**, full radius

**Why the live technique cannot be copied verbatim.** Live, the thumb is a *sibling* of the
track, so the track can use `overflow:hidden` to clip a square-cornered range. In the bench
the thumb is a *child* of `.s-track` at both call sites (lines ~303 and ~357), so clipping
would also clip the thumb, which overhangs the 6px track. The fix below reproduces the same
result without `overflow:hidden` and without touching the markup.

**Box model.** The bench sets `*{box-sizing:border-box}` at line 37. So `.s-track`'s 6px is
the border box: 1px stroke, 4px interior, 1px stroke. An absolutely positioned child resolves
its inset percentages and offsets against the containing block's **padding box** — i.e.
inside the border. That is what makes a zero inset land exactly on the 4px interior, which is
the same 4px interior the live range fills. (`globals.css` says as much in its own comment:
6px "rather than the primitive's 4px" because 1px either side of a 4px bar leaves only two of
interior.)
</already_diagnosed>

    Edit the `.s-range` rule only. Two declarations change; leave `position`, `width:62%` and
    `background` exactly as they are, and do not touch `.s-track`, `.s-thumb`, or any HTML.

    1. Replace the rule's `inset` value with `inset:0 auto 0 0`. This seats the fill on the
       track's padding box — flush against the inside of the 1px `--surf-ink` border on the
       top, bottom and left, instead of outset over it. The stroke then survives across the
       filled range, which is the reported bug.
    2. Replace the rule's `border-radius` value with `border-radius:99px 0 0 99px`. Left-only
       rounding: the fill follows the track's inner curve at the left end, and stays square on
       the right where the thumb meets it — visually identical to the live app's
       `overflow:hidden` clip of a square-cornered range. Do not try to compute an inner
       radius by hand; `99px` self-clamps to half the 4px interior, which *is* the inner
       curve.

    Keep the rule on a single line, matching this stylesheet's existing one-rule-per-line
    style — the verification greps read that line.

    Note on alignment, so it is not mistaken for a regression: after this change `width:62%`
    resolves against the padding box, and the thumb's `left:62%` already did. The fill's right
    edge and the thumb's centre now share one reference and line up exactly; before, the fill
    started outside the box and ended a pixel short of the thumb centre.

    If you want to leave a comment, put it on its own line *above* the rule, not inline.
  </action>
