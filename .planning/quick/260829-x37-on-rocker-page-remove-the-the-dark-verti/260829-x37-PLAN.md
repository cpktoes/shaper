<objective>
On the ROCKER screen, when the shaper switches construction lines on, the drawing currently
gains five dark vertical lines standing at the measured stations and running the full height
of the window. They are not part of steering the curve and the shaper wants them gone. This
plan removes them and leaves the rest of the construction overlay exactly as it is.

Purpose: the construction toggle should show the curve's own steering — the lines and points
that actually move the rocker — and nothing else. The five verticals compete with the curve
for attention and mark stations that already have their own read-outs and tick lines.
Output: one edited drawing file, `components/rocker/rocker-viewer.tsx`.
</objective>
