# Phase quick-261007-c3h Plan 01: The round nose on ROCKER's top view and the fit check Summary

**A foam blank whose catalogue prints its nose 0 wide now draws a round nose (a parabola from the tip, the founder's pick of sketch 012 variant C) instead of a point, and the fit check reads the same curve.**

## What changes for a shaper

On ROCKER, the blank seen from above (the mini display on a computer, the TOP VIEW tab on a phone) used to come to a point at the nose whenever the catalogue printed that nose 0 wide. That was the Mid-length's US Blanks 7'4"SP and the Longboard's 9'4"B among 43 catalogue blanks. Now the foam's outline leaves the very tip straight across the stringer and meets the last printed width exactly at the printed number, with no kink. From that last printed station back to the tail, nothing is different, and every printed width still reads its catalogue number exactly. A blank printed wider than 0 at its nose (the Shortboard's and the Fish's) draws exactly as before.

The fit check reads the same round curve. A round nose has more foam than a point inside the last printed stretch, so a board that was refused as "too wide" right near the nose can now fit. Across the app's whole 1,635-board stress set, no board that fitted before is refused, and none that were refused are newly freed (0 freed there; a board with an outline that bulges exactly into the extra foam is freed, which the tracer test proves on both preset blanks).

Nothing else moved: thickness, rocker, the side view, a hand-set board's foil, the DATASHEET, the order form, the preset cards and every saved board's stored data are unchanged. Saved boards simply redraw. No control, no wording and no database changed.
