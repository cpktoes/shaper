# Summary — Revert the colour palette to the currently published version

`main` was hard-reset from `7665f23` to `origin/main` (`12b6023`). The working
tree is now byte-identical to what is deployed at
https://shaper-coral.vercel.app; `git diff origin/main` is empty.

## Palette now in force

Seven tokens, not nine — `--color-surf-chrome`, `--color-surf-sand` and
`--color-surf-accent-blue` do not exist on published `main` and are gone again.

| token | value | role |
|-------|-------|------|
| `--color-surf-base` | `#ffffff` | the single white ground (no paper/chrome/mount split) |
| `--color-surf-black` | `#111111` | matte black text and major elements |
| `--color-surf-accent-cyan` | `#00e5ff` | selection fills, always with black text |
| `--color-surf-accent-cyan-ink` | `#00767f` | accent text and construction strokes |
| `--color-surf-accent-orange` | `#ff5722` | warning fills |
| `--color-surf-accent-orange-ink` | `#c93f10` | warning text |
| `--color-surf-muted` | `#6b6b6b` | hairline borders and secondary text |

`--outline-construction` points back at `--color-surf-accent-cyan-ink`, not the
blue. The widepoint knot `#a8425f` and the categorical rail-band hexes were never
part of the brand palette and are unaffected.

## Removed

`lib/design/contrast.ts` and `lib/design/palette.test.ts` — the contrast helpers
and the 27-assertion AA contract. Both were created by the palette work and do
not exist on published `main`. Removing them was an explicit founder decision,
not a side effect: they were offered as keepable (the published palette clears
the AA bar and a retargeted test would have passed) and declined.

**Consequence worth knowing:** the AA bar is no longer machine-checkable. Test
count went from 8 files to 7, 638 tests. Any future palette change is back to
being verified by eye unless the test is reinstated.
