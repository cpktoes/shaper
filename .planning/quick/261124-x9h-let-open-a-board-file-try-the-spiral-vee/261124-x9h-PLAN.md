### 1. The one addition, as measured

In `fitBottomToBrd`, after `pool` is built and before `chooseBrdBottom(pool, best)`:

- Look at the closest candidates only: the first `1 + FULL_NEIGHBOURS` entries of `pool` (the refined seed and the three
  neighbours refined in full).
- A candidate matches when its tail is the Spiral Vee and its centre is a double (`cutKind` of the centre name).
- For each match, add two entries to the end of the pool:
  1. that candidate's own three cuts with the centre replaced by the flat centre at its starting numbers, through
     `scorer.refine` with its defaults (all three sections, three rounds);
  2. the Spiral Vee afresh: the candidate's nose, the flat centre, and a tail Spiral Vee at the name's starting numbers
     (`specOfPick` for the tail, with the Spiral Vee as the tail it is built for) at whichever of the Depth steps 1 to
     `DEPTH_STEPS` scores closest by `scorer.valueOfSpecs` (the first of equals), then through `scorer.refine` with its defaults.
- `chooseBrdBottom(pool, best)` is called exactly as today. It is not edited, and neither is any constant.
