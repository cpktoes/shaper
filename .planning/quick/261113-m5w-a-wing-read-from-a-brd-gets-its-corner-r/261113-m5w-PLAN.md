## What the planner measured (2026-11-13; scratch scripts, never in the repo)

**Headline.** A stable method exists, for the corners a file clearly rounds. On an outline drawn exactly, the way a shaping
program's own knots draw it, the radius comes back to within thousandths of an inch. Through this app's own `.brd` writer,
which fits the outline with curves to within 0.5 mm, a radius that moves the corner by less than about a millimetre is
blurred and cannot honestly be told from sharp. The rule below rounds a corner only when the file's corner sits clearly

### 1. Why hyy's attempt wobbled, and what fixes it

- **The wobble.** Station, run and radius trade against each other. A short step with big rounds and a long step with small
  rounds draw nearly the same S, so fitting the radius as a fourth number beside station and run slides along that valley.
- **The fix: describe a wing by its footprint.** Four numbers:
  - `stepStart`: where the step leaves the curve.
  - `inner`: where it reaches full depth.
  - the depth.
  - `share`: the radius as a share (0 to 1) of the most that fits.
  The footprint and the depth are what the file shows plainly. Only `share` is subtle.
- **Exact outlines.** With the board's own curve held and the outline given exactly, this brings every radius back to within
  0.001". Examples: 0.125 for 1/8", 0.251 for 1/4", 0.438 for 7/16", 1.688 for 1 11/16". Station and run come back exact too.
- **Through our writer.** The same search against the written file is poor for small radii, because of how the writer draws:
  - A sharp corner that turns more than 30° is kept as a corner knot. It reads back sharp to within 0.005".
  - Everything gentler is smoothed by curves within 0.5 mm, so the file itself no longer holds a small radius.
  - The rounded truth often fits the written file worse than some other wing does (RMS 0.31 against 0.23 mm for a 3/16" × 1/4"
    wing rounded 1/8").

### 2. The evidence that says "this corner is clearly rounded"

- **The corner inset.** How far the best rounded wing's corner sits inside the sharp corner it rounds:
  R·(1/cos(θ/2) − 1), with θ = atan2(depth, run).
  - On exact outlines it measures the truth closely: 0.17 for a true 0.17 mm, 0.34 for 0.34, 0.51 for 0.52, 1.55 for 1.70,
    3.08 for 3.53.
  - Through our writer, sharp corners read at most 0.43 mm. Clearly rounded corners read 1.35 mm and up.
- **The improvement at the step.** The largest gap over the step and 1/4" either side, best sharp wing minus best rounded wing.
  - Sharp corners: at most 0.03 mm.
  - Every wing that comes back rounded: 0.12 mm and up.
- **Measures tried and dropped.** The window's largest-gap improvement (it tracks the curve's own misfit, not the corner), its
  ratio, and RMS. None separated sharp from rounded as cleanly.

### 4. The rule, its margins, and the refit

A wing's corners read as **rounded** when both of these hold for the best rounded wing (the curve held):
- its corner inset is **at least 1.1 mm** (`BRD_WING_ROUND_INSET_MIN_MM`), and
- it fits the step **at least 0.1 mm** closer than the best sharp wing (`BRD_WING_ROUND_BETTER_MIN_MM`).

Our wing then becomes that rounded wing. Otherwise everything stays as today.

Margins on the inset:

| | Inset measured | Distance from the 1.1 mm line |
|---|---|---|
| Sharp corners: the Fryer | 0.02 mm | |
| Sharp corners: our writer's sharp steps (the most is the gentle 3/16"×1/2" wing 2, whose 21° corners the writer smooths) | 0.00–0.43 mm | 0.67 mm under, 2.6 times |
| Sharp corners: exact outlines | at most 0.07 mm | |
| Rounded, but too slightly to tell: through the writer (the most is a 1/2" step rounded 1/4", true 0.52 inflated to 0.92–1.05; and the same step rounded as far as it fits, true 1.70 deflated to 0.90–1.05) | 0.29–1.05 mm | 0.05 mm under at the closest |
| Rounded, but too slightly to tell: exact outlines | at most 0.70 mm | |
| Clearly rounded: through the writer | 1.35–3.46 mm | |
| Clearly rounded: exact outlines | 1.16–3.08 mm | 0.06 mm over at the closest (a 1/4" square step rounded 1/8" on the fish) |

Margins on the improvement: sharp corners 0.03 mm at most, wings that came back rounded 0.12 mm and up.

The sharp side's margin is wide. The band between "rounded too slightly to tell" and "clearly rounded" is narrow, about 1.05
to 1.16 mm, and that is the honest limit of a half-millimetre drawing. A corner in that band may come back either way between
boards, but it is a rounded corner either way. A sharp corner never reaches it.

The refit after rounding is needed.
- **The problem.** A rounded wing reaches full depth further back than the sharp fit's wing did (9.29" against 9.66" on the
  deep wing). Its fade to the tail then moves the outline behind it by up to 0.75 mm. Rounding with the curve held made the
  two-wing shortboard worse (1.07 to 1.31 mm).
- **First try, dropped.** A per-wing guard ("refuse a rounding that makes the whole tail worse") refused the fish's clearly
  rounded deep wing, because the wing behind had not caught up yet.
- **The fix.** Today's round step (b): refit the curve to what the rounded wings leave, then keep whichever of today's, held
  or refitted scores lowest by the fit's own largest + typical. It lowered that score in every rounded case and never once
  fell back to today's. The widest gap fell or held on every one-wing case; on the two-wing midlength it rose 0.02 mm while
  the typical gap fell 0.03 mm.

## The method — write it exactly like this

The scratch reference is `S/m5w/fitx.ts`, with `S` = `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-kontoes-Code-shaper/4bd02881-795f-4931-8d36-b0c262541886/scratchpad`:

**A. `lib/geometry/brd-wings.ts` gains, pure, still without importing `brd-fit.ts`.** Imports added from `./wings`:
`maxWingRadius`, `effectiveWingRadius`, `type Wing`. Each constant gets a one-line why taken from the measured findings.

Constants:
- `BRD_WING_CORNER_WINDOW_MM = inchesToMm(1.5)`: the file's stretch a wing's corners are fitted on, from the file wing's inner

- `BRD_WING_CORNER_PAD_MM = inchesToMm(1 / 4)`: how far past the step the evidence is read.
- `BRD_WING_ROUND_INSET_MIN_MM = 1.1`.
- `BRD_WING_ROUND_BETTER_MIN_MM = 0.1`.

Functions and type:
- **`wingCornerInset(w)`** (w a `Pick<Wing, "depth" | "run" | "radius">`) returns `Mm`:
  - R = `effectiveWingRadius(w)` and θ = atan2(depth, max(run, 0)).
  - The answer is R·(1/cos(θ/2) − 1): how far a rounded corner sits inside the sharp corner it rounds.
  - It is 0 for a sharp wing. For a square step it is R·(√2 − 1).
- **`wingFromFootprint(stepStart, inner, depth, share, base)`** returns `Wing`. It is the inverse of `wingFootprint`, the
  algebra in `S/m5w/foot.ts` and `wingFromFootprintX`:
  - F = max(0, stepStart − inner), and k = 1 − share².
  - The run is 0 when F ≤ 0. When k < 1e-9 it is (F² − d²)/(2F). Otherwise it is (F − share·√(F² + k·d²))/k.
  - The run is then clamped into `WING_RUN_RANGE_IN` (through `inchesToMm`).
  - radius = share · `maxWingRadius({ depth, run })`, and t = share · hypot(run, depth)/2.
  - It returns `{ ...base, station: stepStart − t, depth, run, radius }`.
  - With share 0 it is the sharp wing from stepStart to inner. With share 1 it is the step rounded as far as it fits.
- **`interface BrdWingCorner`**, a file wing's corners as the fit measured them:
  - `rounded: boolean`: the rule in the measured findings, section 4.
  - `measured: { station: Mm; depth: Mm; run: Mm; radius: Mm }`: the rounded wing that fits the file's step best with our
    curve held, in the WINGS tab's terms.
  - `inset: Mm`: `wingCornerInset` of `measured`.
  - `better: Mm`: the improvement at the step, sharp less rounded.

The header comment gains a paragraph in plain words: the footprint, the inset, the rule, the margins and why a gentle
step's radius can't be read.

**B. `lib/geometry/brd-fit.ts`.**

1. **A private `windowGap(file, ours, lo, hi)`** returning `{ largest, typical }`. This is `windowGapX`:
   - Pack each line's points with x in [lo − 1", hi + 1"].
   - For each of the file's points with x in [lo, hi], take its nearest distance to ours. Sum these for `typical` and track
     the most for `largest`.
   - For each of our points with x in [lo, hi], take its nearest distance to the file. These count towards `largest` only.
   - `typical` is the mean over the counted file points, 0 when there are none.
   - Reuse `packLine` and `nearestDistance`.

2. **A private `measureWingCorners(c, held, start, geometry)`** returning `{ wings: Wing[]; corners: BrdWingCorner[] }`.
   `c` is the `TailAroundWings` already passed, `held` is `c.tailSpec(answer.xs, squash)`, `start` is `wingsOf(answer.u)`.
   Keep a `wings` list, starting as `start`, and go through `c.fileWings` in order (nose-most first). For file wing `fw` at
   index k:
   - (a) **Windows.** lo = fw.inner − WINDOW and hi = fw.station + WINDOW. `filePts` = `c.file`'s points with x in
     [lo − 1", hi + 1"].
   - (b) **The seed.** `base` = wings[k]. A0 = base.station, B0 = base.station − base.run, d0 = base.depth.
   - (c) **A candidate** from unit numbers x and a flag `rounded`:
     - d = clamp(d0·(0.5 + x[2]), `WING_DEPTH_RANGE_IN` through `inchesToMm`).
     - w = `wingFromFootprint`(A0 + (x[0] − 0.5)·2", B0 + (x[1] − 0.5)·2", d, rounded ? x[3] : 0, base).
     - The candidate list is `[...clampWingStations(wings with index k replaced by w, geometry)]`.
   - (d) **The score of a list** is `windowGap(filePts, drawnOutline({ ...held, wings: list }), lo, hi)`: its largest plus its
     typical.
   - (e) **The sharp fit.** `sharp` = `nelderMead`(x → score(candidate(x, false)), [0.5, 0.5, 0.5]).
   - (f) **The rounded fit.** Run `nelderMead`(x → score(candidate(x, true)), [...sharp.x, s0]) for s0 = 0.15, then 0.5, then
     0.9. Keep the lowest value; on a tie the earlier start wins.
   - (g) **The evidence.**
     - sharpList = candidate(sharp.x, false) and roundList = candidate(round.x, true).
     - fpS = `wingFootprint(sharpList[k])` and fpR = `wingFootprint(roundList[k])`.
     - The narrow window: nlo = min(fpS.inner, fpR.inner) − PAD and nhi = max(fpS.stepStart, fpR.stepStart) + PAD.
     - better = windowGap(filePts, drawn sharpList, nlo, nhi).largest − windowGap(filePts, drawn roundList, nlo, nhi).largest.
     - inset = `wingCornerInset(roundList[k])`.
   - (h) **The decision.** rounded = inset ≥ `BRD_WING_ROUND_INSET_MIN_MM` AND better ≥ `BRD_WING_ROUND_BETTER_MIN_MM`.
     Push `{ rounded, measured: roundList[k]'s station/depth/run/radius, inset, better }`. When rounded, wings = roundList.
     A wing that is not rounded keeps its value in `wings` as it was, untouched.
   - Every number uses `NELDER_MEAD_ITERATIONS` as it is.

   - **When no corner reads rounded,** build the spec exactly as today's code does (`c.tailSpec(answer.xs, squash)` with
