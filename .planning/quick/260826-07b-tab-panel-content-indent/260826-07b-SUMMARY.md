# One 12px inset, from one place, instead of three

## What changed

`TabbedPanel`'s inner content card (the `rounded-lg border border-surf-line-faint
bg-surf-panel` element) now carries a default `p-3` inset, composed through `cn` from
`@/lib/utils` instead of a template literal — so a caller's own `panelClassName` padding
deterministically wins over the default via tailwind-merge, rather than depending on
Tailwind's stylesheet order. The doc comment above `panelClassName` and the block comment
above the card itself both now say the inset lives here on purpose.

Volume's `panelClassName="p-3 overflow-y-auto"` dropped its `p-3` — the padding is now the
default, so passing it again would be the duplicate this task removes. Template's viewer
content div lost its own `p-3` (kept `relative`, its load-bearing positioning-context role
per D-06). Rails and Fins gained the inset without either file touching `panelClassName` —
their hand-rolled `pt-1` top nudges (a stand-in for the inset they never had) were retired in
Task 2, since a real 12px on all four sides made the 4px top-only nudge asymmetric for no
reason anyone could name later.

## Why: one treatment, arrived at three different ways

Before this task, the same 12px inset existed at three different call sites, arrived at
three different ways: Volume passed it explicitly via `panelClassName`, Template built it
itself on a child div, and Rails/Fins never had it at all. That is exactly the drift
`TabbedPanel` was extracted (260825-pkq) to prevent — its own docstring says copying the
treatment a fifth and sixth time is how it drifts. Making the inset a `TabbedPanel` default
collapses all three treatments into one, so Rails and Fins get it for free and so does the
next screen anyone adds.

## The absolute-positioning trap

Template's rotate button is `absolute` against the div that used to carry the padding. An
absolute child offsets from its containing block's *padding* box, not its border box —
moving the padding off that div onto the card would have pushed the button's old
`top-3 right-3` offsets an extra 12px down and right. Rebased to `top-0 right-0 z-10` so the
button lands on the (now unpadded) div's corner, which is exactly where it renders today,
12px in from the card's border — with a comment on the offsets explaining why they are zero,
so a later reader doesn't "correct" them back.

## The trade

Rails and Fins content now insets 12px on all four sides where it previously sat flush
against the card's border, shrinking their section plots and the fin diagram by roughly 24px
of width and 20px of height. This lands two quick tasks after 260825-vot and 260825-w8d,
which were specifically about maximising drawn board size — so this is a deliberate exchange
of a little drawing area for a consistent inset across the app, made at the founder's
request, not a regression. On Template the change is net-neutral: the inset simply moved
from the child div onto the card, so the drawing's box is unchanged.

One more expected, called-out-but-not-fixed consequence: Rails DATA, Fins DATA and Fins
MODEL INFO keep their own deliberate 40px top gap on top of the new 12px inset, so their
content now begins 12px lower than before. That gap was left alone per the plan's scope
boundary — it's a considered typographic gap above a panel heading, not a substitute for the
missing inset.
