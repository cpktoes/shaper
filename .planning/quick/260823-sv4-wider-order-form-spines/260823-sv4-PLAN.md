# Quick Task 260823-sv4 — wider spines

The vertical band labels — `RIDER INFO`, `SURFBOARD SHAPE AND DESIGN`, `GLASSING`, and page 2's
`SHAPING DATA` — are 18.4px wide holding 12px type, so barely 3px of air either side of the letters.
They go to 24px, which leaves 6px.

One value moves: `--order-form-spine`, `1.15rem` → `1.5rem`.

## Why that is the whole change

`--order-form-spine` is one of the three properties the sheet's column geometry derives from
(`260823-nv2`). The logo column is written as
`calc(spine + gap + (100% - spine - gap) * left)` precisely so that it tracks the spine — so widening
it should keep the logo/Rail Sections/Laminating right edges aligned **by construction**, and narrow
every band's content column by the same amount.

That is the claim worth testing rather than assuming: the geometry was built to survive exactly this
kind of edit, and this is the first edit to test it.

## Tasks

### Task 1 — Widen and re-verify the geometry

**Files:** `app/design/summary/order-form.css`
