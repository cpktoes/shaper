# Quick Task 260910-0b1: One Set of Rail-Line Ticks, Shared by RAILS and the Printed Sheet — Summary

**The nine line-ticks on the RAILS screen's INSTRUCTIONS tab now control what the printed Rail Band Instructions sheet draws, and the Summary got its own copy of the same nine ticks under the print buttons — one shared, session-only set, reachable from either screen.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~15 min
- **Tasks:** 2/2 completed
- **Files modified:** 9 (3 created, 6 modified)

## Accomplishments

- A shaper who unticks a line on RAILS → INSTRUCTIONS (say, Bottom Tuck 1) now sees that
  line vanish from the printed Rail Band Instructions sheet too — previously the sheet
  always drew all nine lines no matter what was ticked on screen.
- The Summary screen now carries its own copy of the same nine ticks, right under the
  print buttons, visible only when Include Rail Band Instructions in Print is checked.
  Ticking one there changes the RAILS tab's own figure too — there is only ever one set
  of ticks, not two that could disagree.
- The choice is forgotten the moment the page reloads: every line starts ticked again,
  nothing is written to the shaper's account, a cookie, or a saved board, and no board is
  ever marked as changed by touching these ticks.
- The example rail printed at the top of the sheet is still always the Flat one — this
  task never added a Flat/Domed switch to the Summary.

  the D-01/D-02 rules structurally (state shape, seed source, mount location, no storage).

  `toggleGroup`; now reads `useRailLegend()` and renders `<RailLegendTicks>` in place of
  the old hand-rolled label block.

  `ALL_RAIL_REFERENCE_GROUPS` export (and its now-false doc comment) and the unused

  to attribute the all-on seed to the shared provider instead of the RAILS tab's own state.
- `components/summary/rail-instructions-sheet.tsx` — now reads `useRailLegend()` and
  passes `visibleGroups` to `RailPlanSideFigure`, instead of the hard-coded "every group"

## Decisions Made

`rail-instructions.tsx`), the Summary's `w-full` nesting to protect the phone-safe wrapping
row, and deleting `ALL_RAIL_REFERENCE_GROUPS` outright rather than leaving it alongside the
new shared set.
