# Quick 260930-03d: Visitor analytics and a privacy page Summary

**A public /privacy page with five plain-English sections, Vercel Web Analytics counting page-address-only visits app-wide, and Privacy linked from both menus, the Contact form's note and Clerk's sign-in card**

## Performance

## Accomplishments

- A shaper can open `/privacy` — signed in or out, on any device — and read five short sections in
  plain English: what we keep and why, cookies and your browser, who handles it for us, deleting
  your data, and questions. Every claim is backed by real code or Vercel's own documentation, per
  the plan's fact table. On a phone it has the compact top bar and the six-tab bottom bar, same as
  the setup and Contact pages.
- Every screen in the app now counts its own visit through Vercel Web Analytics — cookie-free, and
  it never records anything beyond the page's own address. A sign-in step's hash, a returning
  visit's token, and a social-media click id are all stripped before the visit is sent.
  `@vercel/analytics` is pinned to exactly `2.0.1`, matching the registry's own published
  integrity hash.
- Clerk's sign-in and sign-up card now carries a "Privacy" link in its footer (via
  `appearance.options.privacyPageUrl`), opening `/privacy` in a new tab.
- Privacy sits directly under Contact in both the desktop gear menu and the phone's one Menu
  button — two taps from the setup screen, all six design tabs and the Contact page. It hides on
  `/privacy` itself, the way Contact already hides on `/contact`.
- The Contact form's founder-approved privacy note is untouched, word for word, and now ends with
  a "Privacy" link to the new page.
