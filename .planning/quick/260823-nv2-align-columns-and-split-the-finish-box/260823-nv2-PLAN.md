# Quick Task 260823-nv2 — one left column, and a split finish box

Four changes to page 1.

## 1. The logo box, Rail Sections and Laminating share a right edge

Today all three are "about a third", arrived at three different ways, so none of them line up:

| Panel | Today | Sits inside |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | `w-[34%]` | the header band, **before** the spine label |
| Rail Sections | `flex-[1]` against a `flex-[2]` | the drawings row, **after** the spine |
| Laminating | `flex-1` of three equal boxes | the glassing band, **after** the spine |

Two problems. The three bands do not share a left inset — the logo starts at the sheet's edge while
the other two start after a spine label — and the glassing boxes are direct children of their band
while the drawings row sits inside a content column, so the same percentage resolves against
different widths.

**The fix is to make one geometry and derive all three from it**, rather than hand-tuning three
numbers until they look close:

```
--order-form-spine   fixed width for every RailLabel, so all bands inset identically
--order-form-gap     the flex gap, declared once instead of implied by `gap-1`
--order-form-left    the left column's share of a band's content width
```

- The glassing boxes get wrapped in a content column, so its inner width matches the drawings row's.
  A percentage `flex-basis` resolves against the container's inner width with gaps *not* deducted, so
  once the two containers are the same width the same percentage is the same number of pixels —
  regardless of the two rows having different numbers of gaps.
- Rail Sections and Laminating both become `flex: 0 0 calc(100% * var(--order-form-left))`.
- The logo spans what the spine, the gap and the left column occupy together:
  `calc(spine + gap + (100% - spine - gap) * left)` — resolving against the header's own inner width.

`RailLabel` gains the fixed width, which also lines up page 2's spine with page 1's.

**Verification is a measurement, not a look:** the three right edges must be equal to the pixel, at
print width.

## 2. Core dims row slightly taller, better spacing

6.2% → 7.4%, with the caption/value pair given room to breathe rather than sitting jammed together.

## 3. Finish splits into Leash and Finish

- **Leash** — Leash Cup, Drill Hole
- **Finish** — Sanded, Gloss & Polish

Ticks stack rather than pair up: at a third of the remaining width there is not room for two across.

## 4. Fin System, Leash and Finish take three equal portions

Of what remains after Laminating's left column — `flex: 1 1 0` each.
