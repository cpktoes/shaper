Planner decisions (each measured at plan time; alternatives recorded for the founder's picture review):

- **P-1 — The two rules and their values.** Upright (`max-shell:`, width under 820) and sideways
  (`[@media(max-height:500px)]:`, 500 dots tall or less) carry the same values: the drawing column's margin 2 dots on
  top, left and bottom; the right edge `max(2px, env(safe-area-inset-right))` (P-6); the folder card a 2-dot rim; the
  inner card no border; the tabs 22 dots. Two values differ on purpose and are the only ones that do: the drawing
  column's bottom on a short screen (61, P-5) and nothing else. The pointer appears only in the tabs' touch boxes (P-3),
  which are control size, the one question the pointer answers.
- **P-2 — Slim tabs everywhere.** Every tab and label in the strip draws 22 dots on a phone (`py-0.5` in place of
  `py-1.5`, the same 12-px capitals), so all five screens wear the same tab. On TEMPLATE and VOLUME, whose single
  label is not a button, the strip then costs 25 dots above the drawing (2 + 21 + 2). Alternative for the founder:
  keep today's 30-dot tab (8 dots more on TEMPLATE and VOLUME only; the other three are governed by P-3 either way).
  Removing the label strip altogether on TEMPLATE and VOLUME was not chosen: it is a design element, not a gutter.
- **P-3 — A 44-dot touch box on every tappable tab, and the honest cost.** On a touch screen (`coarse:`) each tappable
  tab gets an invisible 44-dot box centred on it (a `::after`, absolute, `h-11`, `z-10`, the same idea as the slider
  thumb's ring). Centred on a 22-dot tab it reaches 11 dots above and 11 below, so on a phone the strip carries 9 dots
  of top padding (with the column's 2, that is 11) and the card's frame under the tab is 11 (1 border overlapping the
  tab, 2 of rim, 9 of inner top padding). The box therefore covers only empty frame: never the top bar, never the
  drawing, never the viewer's own buttons. Consequence: a row of tappable tabs costs exactly 44 dots of height on a
  phone, which is the floor (today 63 upright, 67 sideways). On a tall touch screen (an iPad) the tab is still 30 and
  the box reaches 7 dots into today's 12-dot margin and 25-dot frame, so its look does not change. The tab and the
  folder card are both made `relative` on a touch screen so the folder card still paints its 1-dot line under the
  active tab exactly as today (measured: the seam's pixel row is identical).
- **P-4 — Drawing tabs touch the rim; text tabs keep a reading margin.** With no inner padding, the prototype put the
  DATA tables, MODEL INFO and ESTIMATE text 3 dots from the frame line. So the prop has two values: `drawing` (no
  inner padding) for each screen's VIEWER, and `text` (8 dots, `p-2`) for DATASHEET, DATA, INSTRUCTIONS, MODEL INFO and
  ESTIMATE. The strip and the rim are identical in both, so switching tabs never moves the frame. Alternative for the
  founder: a 12-dot reading margin.
- **P-5 — The drawing stops above the Undo/Redo pair when the phone is sideways.** Sideways the phone gets the desktop
  shell, where 9c's pair floats 16 dots in from the window's bottom-right corner, 44 dots tall on a touch screen, over
  the drawing column. With a 5-dot bottom band the prototype put the pair over TEMPLATE's TAIL BLOCK chip and ROCKER's
  tail readings on a Pixel 7 sideways. The ruling says the pair stays clear, so on a short screen the column's bottom
  padding is 61 (61 + 2 rim + 1 border = 64 = 16 + 44 + 4 of air). Upright the pair sits over the controls, above the
  bottom tab bar, so the upright bottom band is 5. Alternative A for the founder: a 5-dot bottom band sideways, the pair
  floating over the drawing's bottom-right corner once there is an edit to undo. On the iPhone sideways the board is
  the same size either way (it is limited by width); on the Pixel 7 sideways A draws TEMPLATE's board 385 long instead
  of 347 and FINS's tail 200 tall instead of 169 (table above). Note: on a narrow phone held sideways (under 820 wide,
  an iPhone SE or mini) both rules apply and Tailwind writes the `[@media(max-height:500px)]:` rules after the
  `max-shell:` rules (checked in the compiled CSS), so the 61 wins there too, costing those phones about 60 dots under
  the drawing where the pair is not over it. Recorded, not fixed; neither founder phone is that narrow.
- **P-6 — The notch side.** Held sideways, an iPhone's notch or Dynamic Island sits at one side of the screen, over the
  page (the app asks for edge-to-edge, `viewportFit: "cover"`). Today's 38-dot right gutter happened to clear it; a
  5-dot one would not. So the drawing column's right padding is `max(2px, env(safe-area-inset-right))` under both
  rules: 2 dots in the test browser and in portrait (where the inset is 0), about 47 on a real iPhone sideways, so the
  real-phone gain there is 416 -> about 437 dots wide rather than 482. The left edge sideways is beside the controls,
  not the screen edge, so it stays 2. Residual, recorded: a narrow phone held sideways (an iPhone mini, under 820 wide,
  stacked layout) has the screen edge on its left too and is not inset there.
- **P-7 — Top bar 48.** The bar's Save button and menu button are 44 on a touch screen; a 48-dot bar leaves 47 inside
  its 1-dot line, so both keep their full 44 with 1.5 dots of air: honest for the floor. 45 would press the Save pill
  against the line, so 48 is the tightest clean value. It is one token (`--phone-top-bar-h` in `app/globals.css`,
  read by the bar and by the setup screen's card-size cap), so the home screen's preset cards draw about 6 dots taller
  on a phone; `phone-home` and `phone-setup-landscape` read the token and passed. Alternative for the founder: 9d's 56.
- **P-8 — Bottom tab bar stays 56.** 48 would also hold the floor (47-dot tabs), but 9c's pair is placed with
  `calc(3.5rem + ...)`, 3.5rem being this bar's 56, and `e2e/undo-redo.spec.ts` asserts the 12-dot gap; that offset is
  not ours to touch. Upright, the tab bar sits under the controls, not the drawing, so 8 dots there would go to the
  controls, not the board. Recorded for the founder as a follow-up to do together with the pair's offset.
- **P-9 — RAILS on an upright phone.** Its NOSE / CENTER / TAIL row copies TabbedPanel's tab look and gets the same
  slim tab and touch box. Two rows of tappable tabs stacked need their own 11 dots each, so that row carries 11 dots of
  top padding (its box then begins exactly where the VIEWER row's box ends) and the plot box under it 11 dots of top
  padding (2 on its sides and bottom, was 4). The colour key's 16-dot gap above it becomes 4 on a phone.
- **P-10 — FINS.** The gap between the tail drawing and its Base Length key, 16 upright, becomes 8 on a phone, the
  same 8 the sideways rule already uses (so both rules carry the same value). Sideways stays 8: at 4 the prototype put
  the "1 3/16"" callout against the key's text.
- **P-11 — Opt-in, so nothing else moves.** The new `compactOnPhone` prop is opt-in and only the five drawing screens
  pass it. RAILS's View Full Sized dialog also uses TabbedPanel and prints full-size rails; it does not pass the prop,

- **P-12 — Not touched, with reasons.** The viewer toolbar's 34-dot buttons (a separate, known follow-up). The toolbar

  The controls column's width (the founder's shaping surface). The pinned drawing heights (66/55/50%, D-09). The
