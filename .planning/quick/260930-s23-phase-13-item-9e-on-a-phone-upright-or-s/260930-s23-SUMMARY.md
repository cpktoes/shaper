# Phase quick-260930-s23: Phone chrome gets slimmer, upright and sideways Summary

**TabbedPanel's new opt-in `compactOnPhone` prop and a matching `design-screen-shell.tsx` rim
slim every phone frame to a 2-dot rim and 22-dot tabs with a 44-dot touch box, the top bar drops
from 56 to 48, and a computer stays pixel-for-pixel unchanged — proven by 1,268 new lines in
`e2e/phone-chrome.spec.ts` across four phone sizes and five screens.**

## What changes for a shaper, in plain English

**On a phone, upright or sideways,** the drawing — the board, the rail plot, the fin tail — now
gets noticeably more of the screen. The strip of tabs above it (VIEWER, DATASHEET, DATA, and so
on) draws thinner, and the padded frame around the drawing shrinks to almost nothing. Every tab a
shaper taps still answers a full-thumb area even though it LOOKS smaller — tapping anywhere near
it still works, because the tappable area reaches into the frame that used to be wasted space. The
top bar at the very top of the screen (the wordmark, Save, the menu button) is also a little
thinner. Nothing about how the app WORKS changed — only how much of the phone's screen the chrome
around the drawing eats up.

**On a computer, nothing changes at all.** Every frame, padding and tab stays exactly the size it
has always been, proven by the five reference pictures the test suite compares pixel-for-pixel.

**On a tall touch screen (an iPad, say)**, the look is also unchanged — only the invisible tap
area around each tab grows a little, which a shaper would never notice without measuring it.

## Accomplishments

- Added an opt-in `compactOnPhone` prop (`"drawing" | "text"`) to `TabbedPanel`, slimming its tab
  strip to 22 dots and dropping both card layers' padding to a 2-dot rim on a phone, with an
  8-dot reading margin kept for text pages — unset, its output is byte-identical to before.
- Added a 44-dot invisible touch box to every tappable tab on a touch screen, reaching into the
  frame the slimming above freed up, never into the top bar or the drawing itself.
- Slimmed `design-screen-shell.tsx`'s drawing-column margins the same way upright (by width) and
  sideways (by height), with the sideways bottom specifically sized to keep the floating Undo/Redo
  pair clear of the drawing.
- Wired all five design screens (TEMPLATE, ROCKER, RAILS, VOLUME, FINS) onto the new prop, and
  gave RAILS's own phone-only NOSE/CENTER/TAIL switch the same slim-tab-plus-touch-box treatment,
  with 11 dots of clearance so it never overlaps the outer VIEWER/DATA/INSTRUCTIONS row above it.
- Dropped the phone top bar from 56 to 48 dots (`--phone-top-bar-h`), keeping Save and the menu
  button full 44-dot touch targets, and gave the bar's own side padding a notch-safe inset.
- Wrote `e2e/phone-chrome.spec.ts` (1,268 lines): per-screen frame/touch-box proofs across all
  four phone sizes, an iPad-like tall-touch-screen check and a mouse-only computer check, all
  extended to the full five-screen set.
- Captured 44 before/after/Alternative-A pictures for the founder across both phone sizes, upright
  and sideways, all five screens — copied out of the worktree before it is removed.

## Touch box measurements

Every tappable tab's `::after` pseudo-element measures exactly 44 CSS px tall on every touch
device (iphone/android projects), centred on the tab, reaching 10 dots above and below it into
previously-empty frame — `document.elementFromPoint` confirms a tap there lands on the tab, while
a point 1 dot above the top bar's own bottom edge or 1 dot inside the drawing area's own top never
does. On the tall touch screen (1180x820, iPad-like), the same box reaches a 5-dot probe (the
theoretical reach there is 7, since the tab is still 30 dots there, not 22) with no visible change
to the frame. On a mouse (desktop project), the same tab's `::after` computes `content: none` —
no touch box exists at all. RAILS's own second (nested) NOSE/CENTER/TAIL row passes the same
44px/10-dot proof, and the two stacked rows never overlap: a point 1 dot above NOSE's own touch
box is not NOSE, and a point 1 dot below VIEWER's own touch box is not VIEWER, on both phone
sizes upright.

The phone top bar's Save Board and Menu buttons both measure at least 44x44 on all four phone
sizes, with the bar itself measuring 48 dots (±1) and matching the `--phone-top-bar-h` token
exactly.
