<objective>
On RAILS, a board with a Rolled Vee or a belly and channels shows the tail's cross-section with the channels cut in —
but the thick Finished Rail line was being drawn as 24 straight pieces about a quarter inch long, so it cut across every
channel wall on a slant, and the thin bottom line ramped over an eighth of an inch at each wall. That is what the founder
saw on Dex's 5'8" Twin Mod Fish. This plan draws the channels the way they are cut: last, after the rail is shaped,
straight up at each wall's own place, into both lines. It also stops RAILS from treating a channel that runs out
through the rail as if it made the rail thinner — only a vee or a belly lifts the rail RAILS bands.

Purpose: closes the RAILS half of G-17-6 (17-UAT.md test 6) under the founder's ruling of 2026-11-10: "channels are the
last cut, made after the rail is shaped". Requirements R5 (the channels), R11 (RAILS works from what the bottom leaves),
R15. Decisions D-05, D-10, D-22, D-35, D-40, D-41, D-43.
Output: the sections' part of the one number in `bottom.ts`; the channel cut in RAILS' frame in `rail-bottom.ts`; RAILS'
rail lift from the vee or belly alone in `design.ts`; geometry tests with a vertical wall on both lines; a browser test;
before-and-after pictures. The 3D board, the CNC files and the litres are 17-20's (17-19 pins their litres first).
</objective>
