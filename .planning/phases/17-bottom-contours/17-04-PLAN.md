<objective>
RAILS works from what the bottom leaves, and every screen reads one bottom held in the design store — with every board
still flat, so nothing a shaper sees moves yet.

A vee or a belly lifts the rails; RAILS bands the rail that is left (D-22). The research found the honest way: RAILS'
three anchors read the planed thickness less the rail lift there, in a step that only RAILS' own figures take, so the
rack card's headline thickness and VOLUME's estimator rows never move (D-35). This plan adds that step, then puts the
bottom into the design store beside the rails and fins and wires every reader — VOLUME's litres, the 3D board and its
export, RAILS, the rack cards — so the BOTTOM screen (17-08) has only to set it.
