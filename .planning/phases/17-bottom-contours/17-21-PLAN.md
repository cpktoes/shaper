<objective>
At his look at 17-18 the founder answered its two questions, and this plan builds both answers.

**"Show channels too."** Since 17-18, RAILS draws each channel straight up at its wall on a board with a vee or a
belly. But on a board whose tail is flat, RAILS still drew no channels at all — the tail's cross-section showed a
plain flat bottom. Now it shows them there too, the same way: the rail laid on the flat bottom as RAILS has always
drawn it, then each channel cut last, straight up at each wall, into both the thin bottom line and the thick Finished
Rail line. The rail RAILS bands does not change — its thickness and its band marks are exactly what they are with no
channels, because a channel is cut after the rail is shaped.

**"Match RAILS."** BOTTOM's DATA tab has a Rail lift column. On a board whose outer channel runs out through the rail
at the tail, it counted that channel as rail lift — the Fish with a flat tail and eight channels read about 5/16" at
the tail. It now reads the vee's or belly's lift alone, the same figure RAILS thins the rail by, so a flat tail reads a
dash there. Every other DATA figure stays as it is, and no litres figure moves anywhere.

Purpose: closes the founder's two answers on G-17-6 (17-UAT.md, Founder Rulings, 2026-11-11). Requirements R5 (the
channels), R8 (DATA), R11 (RAILS works from what the bottom leaves), R15 (shown before it ships). Decisions D-05, D-10,
D-22, D-25, D-34, D-35, D-40, D-41, D-43.
Output: RAILS' flat-tail sections carry the channels (`design.ts`, the store); geometry tests for both lines on a flat
tail; a browser test on RAILS; DATA's Rail lift from the vee or belly alone (`bottom.ts`) with its tests and a browser
test; before-and-after pictures of both.
</objective>
