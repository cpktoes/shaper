<objective>
The cut counted in the litres, drawn by the 3D board and carried by the CNC files — through one seam, with a flat
board's every figure unchanged to the last digit.

`boardSectionsWith` already builds every station's section once, and the litres, the 3D surface, the STL, the DXF and
the XYZ all read those sections (Phase 16 D-05). This plan lifts each finished section's bottom by the foam off at that
station and distance from the stringer, holding the deck exactly where it was planed and never leaving less than 1/4"
of foam at any column (D-21, D-33), and corrects the section's area by the change. The sampler is built in `design.ts`
from the bottom and the board facts, so `volume.ts` still knows nothing about outlines, wings, fins or the bottom.
`summarizeDesign` learns the bottom too, so a rack card can never disagree with VOLUME (D-20). The store and the
screens start reading it in 17-04.

Purpose: R3 (the cut takes foam away), R8 (DATA's first line adds up), R11 (one number everywhere), R14 (no preset card
moves), R15. Decisions D-05, D-06, D-20, D-21, D-33, D-34, D-38.
Output: the seam, the sampler, the summary fields, the pin three ways, the litres/mesh/CNC relations, the source test.
