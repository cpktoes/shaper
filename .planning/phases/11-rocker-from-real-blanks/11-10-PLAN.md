<objective>
Decide how a new board starts: presets sit in real blanks, and an untouched board follows the
shaper's own tip defaults.

1. **Presets in blanks (D-03).** Each of the four presets — Shortboard, Fish, Mid-length, Longboard —
   now names a blank and a placement instead of a hand-drawn rocker. The first picks are provisional:
   a script chooses, for each preset's own length, centre and tips, the fitting blank closest in length,
   and writes that blank's catalogue rows into a generated file the setup screen can use without a
   database. The founder replaces them later through the same "Copy preset values" loop used for
   outlines and rockers. Opening a preset now lands in its blank on ROCKER.
2. **Live tip defaults (D-19).** A brand-new board nobody has touched shows the gear menu's current tip
   defaults; the moment it is edited, picked into a blank, saved or started from a preset, it keeps its
   own tips.
3. **Every summary agrees.** The rack card, the preset card and the VOLUME screen compute the same
   litres from the same side profile — built from the board's own copy of its blank.

Purpose: D-03, D-19, D-01 across screens; R5, R13, R14.
Output: the generator and its generated module, presets without rocker blocks, the store's preset and
tip wiring, blank-aware summaries, one browser spec.
</objective>
