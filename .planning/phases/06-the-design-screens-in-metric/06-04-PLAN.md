<objective>
Make the RAILS screen read in the shaper's chosen system — the sidebar, the rail data table and the
cross-section drawing's grid.

In plain English: rail band marks are the numbers a shaper actually measures off the foam with a
rule, so on Metric they read in whole millimetres everywhere — on the sliders, in the table, and on
the cross-section plot, whose grid squares become ten millimetres instead of an inch so the squares
you count match the marks you read. The table says `(mm)` once at the top of each column and leaves
the numbers themselves bare, the way a shaping sheet does.

Purpose: this is the one place in the phase where Metric is not a formatter swap. The plot's grid
is drawn by a loop that counts in whole inches, and counting in ten-millimetre steps is different
arithmetic. It also gets its first test, in either system.

Output: `rail-controls.tsx`, `rail-data-table.tsx` and `rail-section-plot.tsx` converted, plus a
new `rail-section-plot.test.ts`.
</objective>
