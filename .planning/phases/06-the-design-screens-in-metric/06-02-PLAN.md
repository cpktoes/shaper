<objective>
Give a shaper on Metric a box to type a board length into, on all three screens that ask for one.

In plain English: today a shaper picks a board length from two dropdowns — feet, then inches. That
is an imperial way to say a length and has no metric equivalent, so on Metric those two dropdowns
become a single box you type `188` into, and the slider under it moves a whole centimetre at a
time instead of a whole inch. Type something the app cannot read and it puts your last good number
straight back and tells you, in plain words, what it was expecting — exactly the way the rocker
datasheet's typed cells already behave.

Purpose: this is the one genuinely new control in the phase, and it is shared. Building it once
and wiring all three Board Length controls in one plan means a shaper reviews "typing a board
length" as one change rather than three near-identical ones.

Output: `components/design/measure-field.tsx` and its source-contract test, the commit pipeline's
behaviour tests in `measure-display.test.ts`, and the TEMPLATE, FINS and VOLUME Board Length
controls converted.
</objective>
