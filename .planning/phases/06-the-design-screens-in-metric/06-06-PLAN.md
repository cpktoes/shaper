<objective>
Make the Fins sidebar and the fin drawing read in the shaper's chosen system.

In plain English: a shaper on Metric sets fin base length the way fin makers actually quote it — a
hundred and fourteen millimetres, straight off the packet — sets toe-in and the distance in from
the rail in whole millimetres, and reads each fin's distance up from the tail in centimetres. The
drawing's callouts follow, each number carrying its own unit so a millimetre mark and a centimetre
mark can share a picture without being misread.

Purpose: this is the last screen of measurements before the volume card, and the fins sidebar has
more of them than any other file in the app.

Output: `fin-controls.tsx` and `fin-viewer.tsx` converted; the FINS screen complete.
</objective>
