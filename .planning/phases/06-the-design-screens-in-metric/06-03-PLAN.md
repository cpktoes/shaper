<objective>
Make the ROCKER screen read in the shaper's chosen system — the sidebar, the datasheet and the
side-profile drawing.

In plain English: a shaper on Metric adjusts nose and tail rocker and the five foil thicknesses in
whole millimetres, the way a metric rule reads them off a blank; the datasheet's columns are
labelled by where the measurement is actually taken — thirty and a half centimetres from each end,
because that is honestly where the mark is — and its rows say which unit they are in so the numbers
themselves can stay bare. Typing into a datasheet cell works the same as it always has.

Purpose: this is the screen with the most numbers per square inch and the only one with typed
cells, so it is also where the typed field earns its keep and where `ImperialField` finally
retires.

Output: `rocker-controls.tsx`, `rocker-datasheet.tsx` and `rocker-viewer.tsx` converted;
`components/rocker/imperial-field.tsx` deleted.
</objective>
