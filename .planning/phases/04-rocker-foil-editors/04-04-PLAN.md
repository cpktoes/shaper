<objective>
Give a designed board a litres figure a shaper can check against a published blank: real
cross-sections taken along the board's own length — the foil's thickness, the drawn outline's width
and the rail bands' actual shape — added up with Simpson's rule, and shown everywhere the app quotes
volume. Keep the quick estimator alive as the Volume screen's standalone mode for anyone who just
wants a fast number.

Purpose: `lib/geometry/volume.ts` has carried a written IOU since Phase 3 — its deviation 3 says the
approved design's cross-section integration needs the foil editor. The foil editor now exists, so
this plan pays it off. It also closes deviation 2, the two-litre-constant divergence that was
explicitly parked until this moment.

Output: `computeCrossSectionVolume` and `simpsonIntegrate` in `lib/geometry/volume.ts`, validated
against the Arctic Foam 7'3" SBF datasheet; `deriveQuotedVolumeLitres` in `lib/geometry/design.ts`;
and every volume consumer in the app reading one figure.
</objective>
