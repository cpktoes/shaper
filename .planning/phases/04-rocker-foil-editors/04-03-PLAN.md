<objective>
Make thickness one number instead of two. The thickness a shaper sets on the ROCKER screen becomes
the thickness the rail band calculator works from, so the bands move the moment the foil does — and
add the switch that lets someone who only wants rail-band numbers keep using RAILS on its own.

Purpose: the design flows forward. Rocker precedes rails the same way the outline already precedes
fins. Without this the app would carry two thickness values that could silently disagree — and the
rail band dimensions are numbers a shaper cuts foam to, so disagreeing is the one thing they must
never do.

Output: `deriveEffectiveRails` in `lib/geometry/design.ts`; `railsImportFoilThickness` threaded
through the store and the save snapshot; the link checkbox and disabled-while-linked treatment on
the RAILS sidebar.
</objective>
