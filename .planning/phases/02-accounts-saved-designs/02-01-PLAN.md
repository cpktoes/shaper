<objective>
Sign in, save this board, and find it again — the whole spine of Phase 2 proven on one path,
end to end.

This is the phase's tracer. A shaper who has never had an account can sign up in a dialog over
the design screen, press Save, name the board, and after a full reload see that board on the
home screen and reopen it exactly as they left it. One save, one list, one reopen. Autosave,
the in-progress rack card, rename/duplicate/delete, and the sign-in banner all come later —
this plan proves the architecture holds first.

Purpose: every later plan in this phase widens something this one already runs end to end. If
the seams here are wrong (Clerk's auth context in Server Actions, ownership scoping, the
snapshot surviving a round trip), we find out after one commit instead of after six.

Output: a working account + save + reopen path, the Neon `models` table live, and the pure
snapshot/derivation modules the rest of the phase builds on.
</objective>
