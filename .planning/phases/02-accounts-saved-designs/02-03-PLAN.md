<objective>
The board rack, complete: every board a shaper has — saved or still in their hands — in one
place, in an order that does not move around, showing the numbers that let them recognise the
board without opening it.

Purpose: MODL-03 is "view a list of your saved models", but D-06 and D-07 make it more than a
list. Home becomes the one place a shaper looks: their own boards on top, the preset cards for
starting a new one below, and the board they are part-way through sitting first with an honest
tag saying it is not saved yet.

Output: the complete rack section with the in-progress card, a pure tested ordering rule, and
the failure behaviours that keep a bad row from taking the page down with it.
</objective>
