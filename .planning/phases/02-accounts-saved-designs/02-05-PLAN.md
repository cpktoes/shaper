<objective>
The quiet offer, and the end of the stale promises: a one-time banner on the design screens that
sells the payoff rather than the account, and a sweep that removes every line of copy still
telling a shaper that saving is yet to come.

Purpose: D-02 asks for a nudge that is easy to ignore and impossible to trip over. D-01 says the
tool stays open — so the nudge has to stay a nudge. And several strings and doc-comments in this
codebase were written when there was no saving at all; left alone, they now actively mislead the
shaper reading them and the next person editing the file.

Output: the sign-in banner with a tested visibility rule, and a codebase whose copy matches what
the app actually does.
</objective>
