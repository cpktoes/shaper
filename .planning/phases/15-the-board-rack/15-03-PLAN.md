<objective>
Write the rules for the order the shaper sets, the timing of saving it, and the rack's words, as tested code.

Until a shaper first moves a board the rack keeps today's automatic order; from the first move (or the first
duplicate, D-16) it keeps theirs. A new board goes first (D-01), a copy stands right after its original (D-02, D-16),
a deleted board simply leaves, and the unsaved board always stands first and can't be moved (R9). Two devices can
never corrupt the list. This plan writes those rules (`lib/models/rack-order.ts`), a small saver that sends a burst
of moves as one save and says when one failed, and the rack's one settings-and-words file with the prepared D-11
switch (`components/setup/rack-config.ts`).

Purpose: D-01, D-02, D-03, D-08, D-11, D-12, D-16; R7, R8, R9.
Output: the order rules, the saver and the rack's words, each with tests.
</objective>
