<objective>
On a phone or iPad, hold a board until it lifts, slide it, and let go — and the rack keeps that order on the account.

This is the founder's own pick, tried on their phone (sketch 011 A): hold a board still, it swells and lifts, the
others slide aside, and it drops where the finger lets go; a quick swipe still just moves the rack, and near the
screen's edge the rack scrolls along with the board. The new order shows at once, saves in the background and says
so in a small status pill; a failed save puts the board back and says so. The unsaved board can't be moved and
nothing can go in front of it. This plan also adds the order's shared state and the status pill that the computer's
moves (15-11) reuse, and it is the build the founder tries on their iPad and an Android phone (15-10) to decide D-11.

Purpose: R8, R9; D-03, D-11 (the switch read here), D-16 (order kept across a duplicate); UI-SPEC §5, §9, §10.
Output: `use-rack-order.ts`, `rack-status.tsx`, hold-and-slide in `swipe-rack.tsx`, the carrying line, the phone move spec.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-15-26 | Tampering | a client sending another shaper's ids | high | mitigate | The client sends only its own displayed saved ids, but nothing depends on that: `saveRackOrder` filters to the caller's own rows (15-04) |
| T-15-27 | Repudiation / Tampering | two devices or two tabs saving | low | accept | Last write wins on the whole list (CONTEXT discretion); flushing before Rename/Duplicate/Delete keeps one device's own actions in order |
| T-15-28 | Denial of Service | a stuck carry blocking page scroll | medium | mitigate | The touchmove listener cancels only while a carry is active; pointerup, pointercancel and touchcancel always end the carry and restore snap |
| T-15-SC | Tampering | npm installs | low | accept | Nothing installed |
</threat_model>
