<objective>
Give the rails screen the third tab the prototype always had. A shaper opens INSTRUCTIONS beside
VIEWER and DATA and sees one example rail cross-section with every mark on it named — Apex, Deck 1,
Rail Mk1, Bottom Tuck 3 and the rest — and can flip that example between a Flat rail and a Domed
one and watch it reshape.

Purpose: this is the teaching half of the rails screen. The DATA tab already gives a shaper the
numbers; nothing yet tells them what those numbers *are* on a real rail.

Output: a new INSTRUCTIONS page under `components/rails/`, a shared two-option toggle beside the
other viewer controls, mark-name callouts on the existing rail plot, and a golden-fixture entry
that pins the example rail's numbers to the prototype's own formulas.
</objective>
