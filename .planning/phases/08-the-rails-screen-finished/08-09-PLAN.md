<objective>
The shaper had to print at 97% to avoid blank pages: "without the instructions page, I still get the
blank first and last pages (1 and 4)." On US Letter the order form prints a blank sheet, then the
form, then another blank. The cause is 32 pixels of screen padding above and below the paper that
never gets taken off for print — and Letter, unlike A4, has only about five pixels of room to spare.

Purpose: PRNT-06. A shaper printing an order form should get the order form. Two wasted sheets per
print, and a scaling workaround they have to remember, is a tax on the one thing that leaves the
screen and goes into the shop.

Output: the printed order form is exactly its sheets — two pages, or three with the instructions
sheet — on Letter and on A4, in both systems.
</objective>

## What is already built (do not rebuild)

The whole order-form print path — the per-sheet fit, the `@page` margin, the sheet layout, the third
instructions sheet and the page marks — shipped in Phase 7 and plan 08-05 and is correct. The sheets
themselves already fit their box with nothing clipped, on both papers, in both systems (measured in
the Phase 8 UAT). This plan removes what sits *around* them in the printed flow. Nothing about a
sheet's own content changes.

**A note on PRNT-06.** That requirement asks for printed output unchanged from before the milestone.
The two sheets' own content is unchanged and stays that way; what deliberately changes is the blank
pages either side of them, which the shaper reported during UAT and asked to have removed. This is a
knowing, requested deviation from PRNT-06's letter, recorded here so nobody later reads the page
count as a regression. The three jsPDF outputs are not touched at all.

4. **The arithmetic, measured** (`.planning/debug/order-form-letter-blank-pages.md`): every sheet is
   fitted to 733.4 x 990.5 px — the smaller of Letter and A4 on each axis, less the 8 mm margins,
   times a 0.995 safety shave. Letter's printable height at that margin is 995.6 px, so there are
   about five pixels of slack; A4's is 1061 px. With the banner hidden, sheet 1's top edge sits at
   32 px and the document runs to 2045 px. Take the 32 px off top and bottom and the flow becomes
   0 to 1981.1 px, which lands both sheets inside two Letter pages with nothing left over.
