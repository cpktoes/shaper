<objective>
Give the shaper a Tip Style default — Pin deck or Bottom — in the gear menu's Fit & Tip Defaults, and make
the app's two-way pill properly accessible on keyboards and fingers.

12-03 already stores and reads the Tip Style default (and the list already judges against it); this plan adds
the row a shaper uses to change it (D-04), widens the provider's setter so it can save a Tip Style as well as a
number, and gives `TwoOptionToggle` the three additions the UI contract names (§4): a pressed state screen
readers announce, the accent focus ring, and 44px pills on a touch screen. RAILS's Flat / Domed pills get the
same for free. The gear menu row's detail line learns the new words.

Purpose: R4, R8, R11; D-04.
Output: the Tip Style row, the widened setter, the accessible toggle, the new menu detail, and the e2e that
proves them on both phones and the desktop.
</objective>
