<objective>
Teach the app to cut a board from its blank the way a planer does — a constant deck skin off the top,
the bottom planed down to the centre thickness, the tips thinned last — and prove it on every seeded
blank before anything on screen changes. Then carry every board saved under Phase 11 across, so its five
station numbers do not move.

This is the phase's whole geometry core, and all six named tests (SPEC R7) land here, in wave 1, with no
`components/` file touched:

1. First, before `lib/geometry/blank-fit.ts` changes at all, Phase 11's own foil is pinned: a script runs
   tag v1.3's `boardOnBlank` over the presets, a board shaped like the one version-4 board in the
   development database, a deterministic sweep of the catalogue and a few edge cases, and writes a golden
   fixture. Phase 11's 12" formula survives in `lib/geometry/phase11-foil.ts` only, proven against it.
2. The new cut replaces the scaled foil inside `boardOnBlank` (RESEARCH Pattern 1): deck = blank deck −
   skin; bottom = blank bottom + centre gap; thickness = blank thickness − (blank thickness under the
   board's centre − target); the rocker levelled on the un-thinned bottom (so it is the blank's own rocker
   away from the tips); one signed thinning per tip, eased with Phase 11's `smoothstep`, taken off the
   bottom (Pin deck) or the deck (Bottom); a 12" fine-tune on the surface the board chose (D-13).
3. Saved boards move to snapshot version 5 with the cut on the blank; a blank without a Tip Style is a
   Phase 11 blank and is carried over exactly as D-07/D-14 say.

Purpose: R1–R7, R9, R10, R11 and decisions D-02, D-03, D-05, D-06, D-07, D-09, D-13, D-14, D-16, D-17.
Output: the golden fixture and its script, `phase11-foil.ts`, the new derivation in `blank-fit.ts` and
`board-profile.ts`, snapshot version 5, and every test named above.
</objective>
