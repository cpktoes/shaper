<objective>
Planer Max Depth replaces Extra Center Thickness. From the gear menu to the blank list, the rule for "is
this blank thick enough" becomes the founder's: room at the centre for one deck pass (the Deck Skin) and at
least one bottom pass (one Planer Max Depth) — D-10.

The account preference grows to seven settings — Extra Length, Planer Max Depth, Width Margin, Deck Skin,
the two tips and Tip Style — read from the three columns 12-02 added, with Extra Center Thickness no longer
read (D-19). The list's floor, its intro line, the F4 flag and the E2/E3 empty states quote the deck skin and
the bottom pass the verdicts actually use: the board's own skin with a blank picked, the live account default
without one. The dialog's number rows change to match (Planer Max Depth in Extra Center Thickness's old
place, Deck Skin first under NEW BOARDS START WITH). The dialog's Tip Style row is 12-04's.

This is the first plan that touches `components/`; all six named geometry tests are already committed by
12-01 (SPEC R7). The one deliberate desktop picture change of the phase — the ROCKER screen's list intro —
is re-recorded here, once.

Purpose: R1, R2, R8, R11; D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04, D-09, D-10, D-11, D-19.
Output: the seven-setting preference and its allow-list, the new floor and sentences, the list/flag/picker and
dialog wired to them, the updated fit-defaults e2e, and the re-recorded ROCKER baseline.
</objective>
