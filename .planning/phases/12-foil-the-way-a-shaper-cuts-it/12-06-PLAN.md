<objective>
Older boards open with the shaper's own Tip Style, and every saved board in the development database is
checked to open with its five station numbers kept.

12-01 taught `parseSnapshot` to carry a Phase 11 board across, by its shape, with whatever Tip Style it is
handed. This plan hands it the right one — the shaper's account default, as D-14 says — on the two server paths
that read saved boards: the rack on the home page, and the save / rename / duplicate actions (the last one is
how a tab left open across the deploy gets carried over by shape). Both paths fail soft: before production has
the new column, the style falls back to the cookie's or Pin deck and nothing breaks.

Then a read-only script walks every saved board in the development database — six version-1 boards and one
Phase 11 board with a blank, as research found — and proves each opens and the Phase 11 board keeps its five
station thicknesses. The founder can run the same script against production in 12-10.

Purpose: R9, R11; D-07, D-14.
Output: `resolveCarryOverTipStyle`, the rack and action wiring, `scripts/check-saved-boards.ts` and its
development-database output.
</objective>
