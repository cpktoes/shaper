<objective>
Make the three ways a shaper meets their account on a phone big enough to tap.

There are exactly three, and all three were measured rather than guessed:

1. **"Sign in" in the phone menu is 20 pixels tall.** It is a bare button with text styling and no
   height at all — the one row in that menu under the guideline, while the Units and Theme rows above
   it are already 46.
2. **Clerk's avatar is 28 x 28 with no padding**, measured in the founder's own signed-in session on
   the live site. So it does not clear 44, and the hit area has to grow — through Clerk's own theming
   prop, on the button Clerk actually renders, not a box drawn around it.
3. **The little X that dismisses the "Sign in and your boards are saved" banner is 16 x 16.** Phase 9
   asked for this fix and it never shipped.

The banner has a catch worth stating up front: it is 36 pixels tall, and its one line of copy sits
inside 8 pixels of padding a side. Drop a 44-pixel square into it and the banner becomes 60 pixels,
stealing 24 from the drawing on every design screen. So the tappable square overflows the row rather
than stretching it, and the test measures both things.

Purpose: D-05 and D-06 — signing in on a phone stops being a game of precision tapping.
Output: three touch fixes in two files, a Vitest contract that pins the Clerk prop and the classes
(the only automated proof possible for a control Playwright cannot render signed out), and a new
browser spec measuring the two controls it can reach.
</objective>
