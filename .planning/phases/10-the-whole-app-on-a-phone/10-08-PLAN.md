<objective>
**Put the app back in a shaper's hands, and this time walk the whole thing.**

The first sweep found three real defects no emulator could have caught, and that was worth every
minute of it. But it stopped after the four questions it was pointed at. By its own admission the
ten-step table was never filled in — the account menu, renaming and duplicating and deleting a board
from the rack, working through all five design screens by tapping the bar, saving, and coming back to
the home screen sideways were all never walked at all.

Three of the fixes those failures produced have now landed: a phone held sideways stays a phone, a
board card is about three-quarters of whatever screen it is on, and the account avatar claims a
thumb's worth of space in a way the sign-in service's own styling cannot quietly override. So three
of the answers on this sheet are expected to be different from last time, and the sheet says exactly
which three, so nobody is comparing against a memory.

One question is re-asked differently on purpose. Last time the avatar's tap size came back "unsure,
looks the same to me", and that was correctly counted as a failure — a thumb-sized target is not
something anyone can judge by eye. This time it is a thing to do, not a thing to look at: tap the very
edge of the round picture, five times, and count how many open the menu.

Purpose: closes verification gap 3 in full, and the human half of gaps 1 and 2. This walk is the only
evidence PHON-10 can have.
Output: a second sweep sheet, handed over against a green build, and then filled in — every row, both
phones, no blanks.
</objective>
