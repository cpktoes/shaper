<decisions_made>
- **Clerk never settles under the suite's fake keys.** Measured 2026-09-11 on all three
  projects: `useUser().isLoaded` stays false indefinitely (polled 12s), so `nav-auth-control.tsx`
  holds its loading placeholder forever and no "Sign in" control renders anywhere. Every dialog
  and screen behind a sign-in is therefore unreachable in a browser test. Do not try to automate
  the signed-in half — it belongs to the sweep. 10-01's plan assumed the sign-in dialog was
  reachable; it is not.
- **The dialogs spec now measures the RAILS "View Full Sized" dialog instead** — the one real
  `Dialog` a signed-out run can open. Its close-X measures 44x44 on both phones and 28x28 on
  desktop, which is the shared `DialogContent` rule every other dialog inherits. Its own
  ~10px edge clearance is that dialog's own full-bleed width, NOT a weakened version of D-03's
  16px margin; the replace-board confirm proves the 16px rule (x=35 on an iPhone).
- **Dialogs must be measured after their open animation settles.** A 44px button reads
  43.1-43.4px mid-animation. The spec waits on `el.getAnimations({subtree:true})` rather than
  sleeping.
- **Requirement IDs stay unmarked until the sweep passes.** PHON-07/08/09/10 are all about
  signing in and walking the trip on a real phone. Marking them from an emulator would be a lie.
