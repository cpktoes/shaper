## Addendum — the first walk happened, and it stopped early (2026-09-11)

**Status change: this checkpoint is no longer "waiting for a sweep." One phone was walked, it
found three things, and the walk was deliberately stopped.** Plan 10-08 stays INCOMPLETE — no
`10-08-SUMMARY.md` is to be written until both phones are walked on a fixed build.

What came back, and the two rulings taken from it, are recorded in full at the end of
`10-SWEEP-2.md` ("First walk — what came back"). In short:

- Steps 1–5 passed. Step 9 (Summary) passed in both orientations.
- Step 6: the rail-band controls sit under all three RAILS tabs, INSTRUCTIONS included. Confirmed
  in `components/rails/rail-band-editor.tsx` — its own comment already says INSTRUCTIONS has no
  control targeting it.
- Step 7: sideways on a design screen is unusable — the drawing takes half the height and the
  controls have nothing left. The shaper's ruling (**D-10**): revert plan 10-05 and go back to a
  width-only layout switch, so a phone on its side is treated as a normal browser again.
- Step 10: the board pictures on the home screen collapse to zero height sideways. Confirmed as a
  fault in plan 10-06 — a fixed 205px of card text is subtracted from three-quarters of the screen,
  with no floor, so on a screen 270px tall or shorter nothing is left for the board.
- Steps 8 and the avatar five-tap count were never reached. The second phone was never walked
  (**D-11**: fix first, then re-sweep both phones clean).
- One open question blocks filing the sheet's per-phone columns: **which phone was this walk on?**
  The Hide Toolbar tip is iOS-only by design (`supports-[-webkit-touch-callout:none]:`), so "I did
  not see it" is correct behaviour on Android and a failure on an iPhone.
