<objective>
**Stop the rail-band controls turning up under a page that has nothing for them to do.**

The RAILS screen has three tabs. VIEWER draws the rail cross-sections; DATA lists the numbers;
INSTRUCTIONS is a page of reference reading about what rail marks mean and how to turn them into
bands. The first two have controls a shaper actually uses on them. The third does not — the screen's
own source says as much, in its own words, and then puts the controls under it anyway.

On a phone that is worse than untidy. The controls sit below the content there, so a shaper reads
to the end of the reference page and finds a whole column of sliders underneath that change nothing
they can see. The shaper found exactly that on 2026-09-11 and wrote down six words: *"rails keeps
the controls under all 3 tabs."*

So on a phone, INSTRUCTIONS stops carrying them. Nothing is taken away — every rail-band control is
exactly where it always was, one tap away on VIEWER or DATA, where it moves something you can see.
And a desktop mouse sees exactly what it always saw, on all three tabs, because on a desktop the
controls sit beside the reading rather than after it and nobody has asked for that to change.

Purpose: closes fix 3 of the four the 2026-09-11 walk produced — the one finding that is not a
regression from this phase but a long-standing oddity a real phone finally caught.
Output: one change in the RAILS screen itself, and a browser test that proves a shaper cannot see
what they should not see.
</objective>
