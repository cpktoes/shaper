<objective>
Make the steady taper real: every board in a blank now runs its thickness down steadily into each tip.

Today each tip is eased over the last 12" with an S-shaped blend, and some boards come out thinner a few inches
from a tip than at the tip. From this plan on, each tip's thickness runs down along one smooth curve from its start
point to the tip setting — the start being 12" unless the board can't run down steadily from there, in which case
Automatic moves it in. On the test boards that means no board thinner than its own tip, none under the 1/4" floor,
none newly poking out of its blank, and — with a start at 12" — the same five station numbers as before. Today's
blend stays available by name for the reports. Nothing on screen gains a control yet; the numbers move.

Purpose: R1, R6, R7; D-01 to D-03, D-07, D-14, D-20, D-23, D-25; acceptance 3 and 4.
Output: the taper inside `boardOnBlank`, the per-tip views, the rewritten tests, the stress-set tests, the
re-recorded preset figures and the pending removal note.
