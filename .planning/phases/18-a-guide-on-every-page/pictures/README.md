# The GUIDE on every page — pictures for your look

Every picture is the Shortboard preset, opened from the home page and not saved, so where the strip has room for
the board's name it reads "Untitled". "Computer" is a 1280-dot-wide window; "phone" is an iPhone held upright;
"sideways" is an iPhone on its side (844 × 390). Imperial unless the name says metric.

Three rulings were made for you while you were away, and these pictures are where you can overrule them:

- **D-21 — the board's name steps aside on a crowded strip.** With GUIDE added, ROCKER's and RAILS' tabs leave too
  little room for the name on a narrow phone, so the name is simply not drawn there: on ROCKER below about 412 dots
  wide and RAILS below about 400, on ROCKER held sideways, and on both in an 820-dot computer window. Every tab word stays whole. Your
  other choice is shorter phone tab words, which would bring the name back.
- **D-22 — a Metric shaper reads no inch or foot figure on any card.** Every number on a card follows the system you
  picked, and the rules of thumb in the sentences are said the metric way ("every 10 cm or so", not "every few
  inches"). The only inch figures left are a blank's own name and a maker's rocker order code, as everywhere else.
- **D-25 — a Metric push is a round figure.** The dashed "what if" on each card moves by a round metric amount
  (+15 cm of length, +2.5 cm of width, +13 mm of nose thickness), not an exact conversion of the inch push.
