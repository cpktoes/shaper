# Phase 16: 3D Board and CNC Export — Specification

**Created:** 2026-11-03, from the founder's words of the same evening and the discussion that followed
(`16-CONTEXT.md`, D-01 to D-20) and the approved screen contract (`16-UI-SPEC.md`).
**Requirements:** 12 from the founder's words and rulings, plus 8 carried constraints.
**Source:** `16-CONTEXT.md` (the founder's quoted ask, every ruling), `16-UI-SPEC.md` (every word, state and size on
screen), `pictures/3d-board-mockup.png` (the look he called right, with its scripts in `pictures/3d-mockup-source.tar.gz`).

## Goal

A shaper sees the board they designed as a solid, realistic surface they can spin and move in every direction, lit the
way a shaping room is lit so every contour shows, on a seventh design screen called **3D**; and takes that surface away
as files a CNC shaping program (Shape3D, AkuShaper) reads as a 3D guide. The surface is built from the same
cross-sections the litres count, so what the shaper sees, what they export and what VOLUME counts are one board.

## Requirements

The founder's words are quoted; the sentence after each quote is the requirement as this phase reads it. The decisions
each one rests on are in brackets.

1. **R1 — A 3D screen of its own.** *"maybe a new tab/page titled 3D?"* A seventh design screen, **3D**, last in the nav
   after SUMMARY, with its own route, nav link, phone tile (seven tiles: four by two upright, one row sideways) and
   Back/Next place; every place that counts six screens counts seven. [D-01, D-02, D-03]
2. **R2 — The board as one surface with the litres.** The 3D surface is lofted from the finished-rail cross-section at
   every station along the rocker, with the foam actually there at the tail: a swallow is two pieces from the notch wall
   to the rail, a diamond's V is foam. The mesh's enclosed volume agrees with VOLUME's litres (a relation test). [D-05]
3. **R3 — Realistic foam under shaping-room lights.** *"I'd like the board to look realistic … shaping room lighting for
   seeing contours."* Raw matte foam, white in every theme, a stringer line down the deck and bottom, lit by two lights at
   rail height along the walls and a soft overhead, fixed in the room so the board turns under them. [D-07, D-10, D-11]
4. **R4 — Spin, zoom and slide.** *"move the board on the screen and spin it in all directions."* Drag to spin in every
   direction, wheel or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to slide; preset views Deck, Bottom, Side, Nose, Tail
   and Reset View; keyboard equivalents; no glide under reduced motion. [D-13]
5. **R5 — Fins and the blank as switches.** Simple fins standing at their FINS placement (a Fins switch, on by default);
   the picked blank as a see-through ghost (a Blank switch, off by default, dimmed with its reason when no blank is
   picked). [D-06, D-08]
6. **R6 — Contour stripes.** A Contour Stripes switch, off by default: bands of light that bend where the surface bends.
   [D-12]
7. **R7 — Export for CNC.** *"we'll want to be able to export the data file … create a .dxf file from a surface or
   wireframe model."* One button on the 3D panel downloads one zip holding a DXF wireframe (outline, stringer profile,
   slices), the XYZ text set (outline, profile, one file per slice) and an STL of the drawn surface — the bare board only,
   in millimetres, named from the board's name — with the note that Shape3D and AkuShaper read it as a guide. It works
   signed out and on a locked board. [D-14, D-15, D-16]
8. **R8 — The files are what Shape3D takes.** The DXF, STL and text files open in Shape3D's import/scan option as a guide
   (DXF, STL or text, per its own page) in the axes and units it expects; research fixes the text files' columns and the
   slice stations before the writers are built. [D-14, Claude's discretion]
9. **R9 — Hard Edge Ends At.** *"a hard edge in the tail that should fade into the center tuck just in front of the fins
   (~16-20" from tail)."* A slider on the tail's Advanced menu on RAILS, 14" to 24", default 18", shown only while Hard
   Edge is on, saved with the board (absent reads 18"); the edge runs full to the 12" station and fades smoothly into the
   tucked rail by the set distance; the litres take the same rule, and the presets' recorded figures are regenerated by
   their script. [D-20]
10. **R10 — What the screen says and shows.** Every word, state, size and colour is the approved contract's: the panel
    (Show, View, Export), the canvas messages (no WebGL, can't be built, didn't load), the loading picture, the four
    themes, the phone layout under the three switches. [`16-UI-SPEC.md`]
11. **R11 — Nothing stored, nothing announced.** No database change; the switches and the view are screen state; no
    notice of the new screen. [D-09, D-17, D-18]
12. **R12 — Shown before it ships.** Pictures before every merge, the founder's look between plans, the go-live count his
    call at plan approval, nothing pushed without his go. [D-19]

## Carried constraints

- **C1** Geometry pure and tested in `lib/geometry/` with relation tests and script-generated goldens; never a hand-typed
  expected number (CLAUDE.md Rule 1).
- **C2** Every number through `lib/geometry/units.ts`; the files are millimetres as stored (Rule 2).
- **C3** Width picks the layout, pointer the control size, height the short-screen rules; no fourth switch (CLAUDE.md
  Layout).
- **C4** Sign-in is a nudge, never a gate: the 3D screen and the export work signed out.
- **C5** The renderer is the one new drawing dependency, loaded only on the 3D route; research names it and measures it.
- **C6** Browser specs on three devices; the five desktop reference pictures re-recorded only for the nav row, with the
  diff eyeballed; the practice rack for signed-out tests.
- **C7** Plain English in every word a shaper reads, every commit message and every summary.
- **C8** Nothing pushed, deployed or migrated without the founder's go; work on branch `board-3d`.

## Out of scope

Bottom contours (concave, vee, channels); reading `.s3d`/`.s3dx`/`.brd` files; a glassed look; lights that follow the
view; station lines; an idle auto-turn; fins in the STL; an export button on SUMMARY; IGES or OBJ files.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The nav, the phone sheet and Back/Next show seven screens; SUMMARY's Next reads `3D →`.
2. The Fish, the Shortboard, the Mid-length and the Longboard each draw as a lit solid; the Fish's tail is two pieces.
3. For every preset, the mesh's enclosed volume and VOLUME's litres agree within the tolerance research sets.
4. Each switch, view button and Reset View does what the contract says; a locked board behaves the same.
5. The zip holds the DXF, the STL and the text set with the contract's names; a slice file's points lie on the drawn
   surface; the STL is closed (watertight) for every preset.
6. Hard Edge Ends At: the slider's range, default, visibility and storage per D-20; the litres of a hard-edged tail move
   as the fade says; a saved board without the field reads 18".
7. The three canvas messages appear in their cases; the export still works with no WebGL.
8. The whole unit suite, type check, lint, the named browser specs on three devices and a production build pass; the
   five desktop pictures move in the nav row only.
