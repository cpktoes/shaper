<objective>
Let the shaper turn the board any way they like — by dragging, by the five preset views and Reset View, and from the
keyboard — with the camera and the lights fixed in the room, so the highlights move across the foam as it turns.

The turn is plain numbers in `lib/geometry/view-3d.ts` (a quaternion, research Pattern 6): a drag premultiplies a turn
about the screen's axes, a glide decays over about 600 ms, a view is a target turn reached by the shortest arc in
400 ms, and every view is proven against TEMPLATE's and ROCKER's own drawings by relation tests. The scene only applies
the turn to the board's group. The panel gains its VIEW section. Zoom and slide are 16-09.

Purpose: R4 (spin, preset views, Reset, keys, no glide under reduced motion), R10 (the contract's words and states),
R12. Decisions D-04, D-10, D-13, D-17.
Output: the turning maths and its tests, the screen's pointer and key handling, the VIEW section, the live region, the
spec, pictures of the five views.
</objective>
