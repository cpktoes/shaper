## Known limitations (left alone on purpose)

- A saved twin board keeps the forward base it was saved with: FINS' dots on a saved board never move on their own. So an
  older keel saved at 4.5" with the base not overridden stays 4.5" until the shaper presses Reset or changes its twin
  template, and its label still reads `(standard)`, because that word reads the overridden flag alone. Making it compare
  against the new default, or moving such boards, is a different change; the orchestrator was told.
- The shared corner touch box (`COARSE_TOUCH_BOX`) measures 42 × 42 by its own computed `::after`, because the `::after` is
  placed inside the button's 1-px border, and 44 by the house's `touchSize` rule. The three switches wear it as every corner
  icon does. Making it a true 44 (a 6-dot inset) moves every viewer's Back to 1x and the Customize Rocker icons, so it is a
  to-do for the orchestrator, not this plan.
- FINS' own bases for quad rears, side bites and the trailer (4.0–4.5") are longer than real fins of their depth (research
  pitfall 5), so those fins draw a little more raked than real ones. That is true to FINS (D-25); changing FINS' defaults is a
  FINS question for another phase.
- The SPEC's generic-fins row says the fins never copy a maker's template. D-35 asks for shapes taken from published
  templates: the side fin, the twin and the single are joint fits across makers, while the keel and the small fin are each
  one published fin rounded to two decimals (research pitfall 8). No name appears anywhere and no foil is drawn; the founder
  hears this at his look (Task 3, step 5).
