<objective>

three ways — a slider, a dragged point on the drawing, or a typed value copied straight off a foam
blank's spec sheet — plus the toolbar that rotates the board and hides the outline reference.

their own board next to it. That is the whole reason the five-station model was chosen (D-05), and
this plan is where the screen finally reads as a datasheet rather than a demo.

toolbar and drag wiring in the editor and viewer.
