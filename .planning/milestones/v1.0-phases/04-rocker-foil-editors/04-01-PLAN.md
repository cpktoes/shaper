<objective>
Stand up the ROCKER screen as a working, end-to-end part of the design: a new tab in the nav,
a real side view of the board, and rocker and foil values that live in the shared board state
and survive a save.

This plan is the phase's tracer. It wires one thin path through every layer the phase touches —
pure geometry, the shared store, the nav, a new route, a new drawing, and the save format — and
proves that path works before any of the later plans widen it. Nothing here is a throwaway: the
curve maths, the drawing and the save format are the real ones the rest of the phase builds on.

Phase goal (ROADMAP): "Users can shape a rocker curve and a foil profile as first-class,
interactive parts of the design, with rail band and volume recalculating live as they adjust
either — completing the 'shaper features' milestone's editor work."

Purpose: the last two design surfaces stop being assumptions baked into other screens and become
things the shaper actually draws. Every later plan in this phase reads the specs, the sampler and
the drawing created here.

Output: `lib/geometry/monotone-spline.ts`, `lib/geometry/rocker.ts`, `lib/geometry/foil.ts` and
their test suites; rocker and foil in `BoardSpec`, the design store and the save snapshot; the
`/design/rocker` route with its editor shell and side-profile viewer.
</objective>
