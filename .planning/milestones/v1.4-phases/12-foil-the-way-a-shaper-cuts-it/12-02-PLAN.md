Get the database ready for the three new account settings, and teach the app to count planer passes the
way a shaper would from the numbers on the screen.

Two independent pieces, both additive, neither touching a screen (SPEC R7 keeps wave 1 off `components/`):

1. `user_preferences` gains three nullable columns — the shaper's Planer Max Depth, Deck Skin and Tip Style

   off the printed depth and the printed pass depth, so a shaper dividing the two numbers on the screen by
   hand always gets the count the screen shows (UI contract, Copywriting).
