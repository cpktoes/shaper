<objective>
Finish the fit rules the founder settled at planning: one bottom pass must survive under the board's centre
wherever the board sits (D-15), and a board that would run under 1/8" thick anywhere does not fit, saying why
(D-18).

The list already hides blanks too thin at their printed centre (12-03). This plan adds the placement half of
"one on deck and one on bottom as a minimum": the per-move fit check now also needs a full pass of foam under
the board's centre where it sits, reported through Phase 11's existing F2 ("Doesn't fit at this placement").
And for the rare nonsense board — a very thin centre in a very thick blank, only reachable at centres of
1 1/2" or less — the verdict and the reason line say the blank is too thick for that centre, not too thin.

Purpose: R2, R8, R11; D-09, D-15, D-18.
Output: `fitAt`'s two new checks, the `runsOut` shortfall and its sentence, and their tests.
</objective>
