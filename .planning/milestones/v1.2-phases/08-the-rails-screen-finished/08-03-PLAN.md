<objective>
Show a shaper where the marks actually go. Beneath the example rail, a plan and side view of a board
carries the deck-mark, rail-mark and tuck lines the rail cross-section defines, with the nose, centre
and tail stations marked — and nine tick-boxes to show or hide each family of line so a shaper can
isolate one at a time.

Purpose: RAIL-05. The cross-section says what a mark is; this figure says where it runs along a board.

Output: the prototype's own teaching illustration, ported faithfully, plus the three-step instructional
copy and closing note that go with it.
</objective>
