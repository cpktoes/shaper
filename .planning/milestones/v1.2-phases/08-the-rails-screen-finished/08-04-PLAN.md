<objective>
Let a shaper hold a piece of foam or a ruler against the screen. A "View Full Sized" button on the
rails VIEWER tab opens the rail cross-section drawn at actual size, with Nose, Center and Tail tabs
inside so they can flip between rails, one plain line saying it assumes a standard screen at 100%
zoom, a two-inch bar beside it to check that against, and a Print button that puts the same thing on
paper.

Purpose: RAIL-04. A shaper works with their hands. A drawing they can lay against foam is worth more
than a number, provided it is honestly the size it claims to be.

Output: the actual-size dialog, its check bar and its print path — no calibration step, because the
bar answers that question by itself.
</objective>
