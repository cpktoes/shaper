<objective>

Instructions in Print" — from the rails sidebar or from beside the Print Order Form button — and a
third page appears at the back of the order form, showing the example rail with every mark named and
the plan and side figure with every line drawn. Untick it and the order form is exactly the two-page
form they print today, down to the page marks.

glasser or a helper can send the explanation with it — but only if they ask for it.

Output: the mirror tick-box, the third sheet, and the page marks that count it.
</objective>
