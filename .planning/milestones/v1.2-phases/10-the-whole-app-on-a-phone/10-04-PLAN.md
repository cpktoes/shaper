<objective>
Put the app in a shaper's hands, on two real phones, and walk the whole trip.

Everything before this plan was measured by a machine, at a machine's viewport, signed out, with
fake keys. That catches sizes. It does not catch the iOS long-press menu appearing over a drag, a
hover state that sticks after a tap, Safari's toolbar sliding away mid-scroll, the keyboard covering
the box you are typing a board's name into, or whether a fish still reads as a fish at arm's length
in daylight.

So: ten ordered steps, on an iPhone and on an Android, recorded as a plain table of pass, fail or
not-applicable. Three questions that only a real phone can answer are called out by name — the size
of the account avatar in a genuinely signed-in session, the keyboard over a rename dialog, and
whether two boards side by side is right when the phone is turned. Phase 9 also left the sideways
tab placement to be revisited here, and it is on the list rather than left to chance.

Before any of that, the same trip is walked once by a test, so nobody hands over a phone to discover
a route is broken.

Purpose: PHON-10, and the in-the-hand confirmation of PHON-07, PHON-08 and PHON-09.
Output: an automated trip spec, a sweep sheet ready to fill, and a completed table returned as this
plan's evidence.
</objective>
