<objective>
Make the rack's dialogs work under a thumb.

Four of the five dialogs a shaper meets on the setup screen are already finger-sized without anyone
doing anything — their buttons come from the shared `Button`, which grew to 44px on a touch screen
back in Phase 9. Two rules were missed, and both of them bite:

1. **The little X that closes a dialog is 28 pixels.** Measured on both phone projects on
   2026-09-10. It is on every `Dialog` in the app — including "Name this board", "Rename board" and
   the sign-in dialog.
2. **The box you type a board's name into is 32 pixels tall.** Every typed field on the five design
   screens is 44 with 16px text, and this project's own test file says so out loud. The two name
   fields never got it, so on a phone they are the odd ones out.

Both are one class each, on a shared component, gated on a touch pointer — so a mouse sees exactly
what it sees today.

Purpose: D-03 and D-04 — a shaper can rename, name and delete a board with a finger, and the name
field does not make iOS zoom the page.
Output: two one-line component fixes, a Vitest proof that Tailwind really emits those rules, and a
new browser spec that opens the two dialogs a signed-out test run can actually reach and measures
them on a real phone profile.
</objective>
