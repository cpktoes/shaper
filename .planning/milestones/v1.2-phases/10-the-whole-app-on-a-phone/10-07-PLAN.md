<objective>
**Make the account avatar actually finger-sized, and put a test behind the nav row that already
broke once.**

Three small things, all of them proof gaps rather than new features.

**The avatar.** Signed in, the little round account picture in the top bar is the way to Units,
Theme and signing out. It measures 28 dots across with nothing around it — under the 44 dots a thumb
needs. The phase already added padding for touch screens, but Clerk paints that button with its own
stylesheet loaded at run time, and on a real phone the shaper could not tell any difference. That was
anticipated when the decision was made, and the answer was written down in advance: say it again with
the flag that wins over an ordinary rule. This applies it.

**The nav row.** Halfway through this phase the row across the top of a wide screen — wordmark, six
screen names, settings, Save, account — was running 51 dots off the side of the screen at around 820
dots wide, and growing the account control pushed it 7 dots further. It was fixed. Nothing in the
test suite checks it, so the next person to add a word to that row could reopen the same bug with
nothing to catch it. This adds the check.

**A test that promised more than it did.** One test is named for proving that exactly three files use
the shared text-field component, and a fourth would sail past it. Either it does what its name says or
the name changes.

Purpose: closes verification gap 1's code half and gap 6, plus code-review findings WR-01 and WR-02.
Output: one class change, one compiled-CSS proof, one new browser spec, one honest test.
</objective>
