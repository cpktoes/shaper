<objective>
Make every control on the design screens big enough to hit with a finger, and stop the page jumping
when a shaper taps a number.

Sliders, buttons, tabs and typed fields all grow to at least 44 pixels across on a touch screen —
the size a fingertip actually covers. The slider's visible thumb stays the same small dot it has
always been; what grows is the invisible area around it. Typed number fields grow the same way and
their text goes up to 16 pixels, which is the one thing that stops iOS zooming the whole page in
when a field is tapped. We fix that with type size, not by taking away a shaper's ability to
pinch-zoom.

None of it is keyed to how wide the screen is — it is keyed to whether the pointer is a finger. A
touchscreen laptop gets the big targets; a mouse never does, at any window size. Which is why the
five desktop pictures from 09-01 still match after this.

Purpose: PHON-03, in the four or five central components rather than at seventy call sites.
Output: coarse-pointer size rules on Button, Slider, Input and MeasureField, 44px checkbox rows on
the five design-screen control files, and a spec that measures every one of them.
</objective>
