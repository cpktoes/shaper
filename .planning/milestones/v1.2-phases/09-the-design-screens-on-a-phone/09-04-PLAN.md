<objective>
Put the rails screen and the app's three data sheets on a phone without losing a single column or a
single number.

On a phone the RAILS screen shows one rail cross-section at a time — NOSE, CENTER, TAIL along a tab
strip, the same three-way switch the View Full Sized dialog already uses — so each rail gets the
whole width instead of three rails squeezed to about 120px each. The INSTRUCTIONS tab, which is a
long read with no controls attached to it, simply scrolls as one page.

The View Full Sized dialog on a phone shrinks the rail to fit and says so in one line: shown smaller
than actual size, tap Print for the full-sized rail. The 2-inch check bar and the zoom caveat go,
because on a phone they would tell a shaper something untrue. Printing is unchanged — the paper is
still ruler-true, from a phone as from a desktop.

The rocker DATASHEET, the rails DATA table and the fins data panel keep every column and scroll
sideways inside their own box, with a soft fade on the trailing edge so a shaper can see there is
more. The page itself never slides sideways.

Purpose: PHON-01's "nothing hidden" on the two densest screens in the app.
Output: the rails screen on the shared shell with a one-rail switch, the phone dialog, three
side-scrolling tables, and a spec that proves none of it makes the page scroll sideways.
</objective>
