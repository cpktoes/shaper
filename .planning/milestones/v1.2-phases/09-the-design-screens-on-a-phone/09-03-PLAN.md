<objective>
Move the other four screens onto the shell TEMPLATE already proved, and give each one the phone
layout its own content asks for.

ROCKER gets the same tall pinned area as TEMPLATE: the side profile stands nose-up with the phone
and lies flat when the phone is turned, its four curve handles already showing, its six repeating
sliders folded into Fine adjust. The founder chose to keep the board standing with the phone even
though that leaves the rocker drawing narrower than the screen — measured, that is about 266px wide
on a small iPhone and 336px on a big one, and that is accepted (D-18).

the controls, one scroll. FINS pins its diagram to a little over half the screen and fits it to the
width.

None of these four screens writes any layout of its own — they pass props to the one shell.

Purpose: this is where the roadmap's main risk (fixing the layout five times) is either avoided or
proved real.
Output: four editors on the shared shell, and a spec that proves each one stacks and never scrolls
sideways on an iPhone and an Android.
</objective>
