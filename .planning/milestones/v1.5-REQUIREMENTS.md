# Milestone v1.5 Requirements: Ready for the Shapers

**Status:** No REQUIREMENTS.md was kept for this milestone. Each phase was locked by its own spec, written from the founder's words: [13-SPEC.md](v1.5-phases/13-ready-for-the-shapers/13-SPEC.md), [14-SPEC.md](v1.5-phases/14-realistic-surfboard-flow/14-SPEC.md), [15-SPEC.md](v1.5-phases/15-the-board-rack/15-SPEC.md), [16-SPEC.md](v1.5-phases/16-3d-board-and-cnc-export/16-SPEC.md), [17-SPEC.md](v1.5-phases/17-bottom-contours/17-SPEC.md) and [18-SPEC.md](v1.5-phases/18-a-guide-on-every-page/18-SPEC.md). **Phases:** 13–18 (continuing from v1.4, which ended at Phase 12)

**Goal:** When a room of shapers saw the app on Saturday 2026-10-10 it was safe, tidy and credible on the numbers they know best — and what they and the founder asked for afterwards was built: curves that flow like a real board, the Board Rack, the 3D board and its files, bottom contours, and a GUIDE on every page.
