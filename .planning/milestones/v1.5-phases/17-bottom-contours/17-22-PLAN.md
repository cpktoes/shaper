<objective>
The founder gave two answers about concaves on RAILS on 2026-11-11. At his look at 17-21 he said "Draw the concave": a
tail concave should be drawn under the channels. On this plan he widened it to "Always draw it": RAILS draws every
concave, on the nose, centre and tail sections, whether the board has channels or not. That retires his earlier rule
that a concave changes nothing on RAILS (D-41).

What a shaper sees: on RAILS, the bottom line under each rail section now hollows up into whatever concave is cut
there — a nose concave at the nose section, a single or double concave at the centre, a tail concave at the tail —
instead of running flat. Where the tail carries channels, the channels are cut into that concave last, straight up at
every wall, on the thin bottom line and on the thick Finished Rail line.

What stays the same: a concave hollows the middle and leaves the rail at the planed bottom, so the rail RAILS bands is
not thinner. The rail thickness, the readout and RAILS' DATA figures stay exactly as they are. A band mark that stands
inside the concave now sits on the concave's surface, the way marks already sit on a vee. On the Shortboard and the Fish
no mark is inside a concave, so none moves. On the Mid-length the most is a thousandth of an inch. On the Longboard's
rolled rail the inner bottom tuck mark rises by up to about 1/8" on the tail section with a centre Early Double Concave.
Boards with no concave on a section draw that section exactly as today. No litres figure moves.

From lib/geometry/rail-bottom.ts (367 lines, read-only for logic): `railSectionBottom` samples `across` every 1/8" from
`RAIL_BOTTOM_REACH` inboard of the plot's left edge to the apex, y = `liftIn(inFromRail) - lift` — a concave gives y
above 0 inboard of its edge. Without `channels` it returns `{ lift, across }`. The channels' `hold` keeps each value at
most `deck - across` at its x, so a floor never stands above the deck on a concave. `railPointOnBottom` moves a point by
`fall * (1 - y / thickness)` and returns the point itself where the fall is 0. `railBottomLowest` only ever goes below
0, so a concave never moves the plot's frame (`minY` and the tick labels' baseline in rail-section-plot.tsx read it).
`railBottomLine`, `finishedRailOnBottom` and `channelPieces` are general in the sign of the fall. No logic change is
needed there — only the doc comments, which say a section no vee or belly lifts has a flat `across`.

From lib/geometry/bottom.ts (NOT modified): `sectionsFoamOff(resolved, board, s, y)` is the vees', bellies' and
concaves' foam without the channels; on a flat bottom it is 0 everywhere, so a flat-tail channel section's `across`
stays all 0 and comes out byte-identical.

Band Reference, `order-form.tsx:515`). With a `bottom`, the plot's bottom connector is drawn as `<polyline
data-rail-planed-bottom>` instead of a `<line>` (so the band-line count is one fewer). The RAILS tile
(`railsTileArt`) never reads `bottom`; RAILS' GUIDE reads `railBands`, not these.
