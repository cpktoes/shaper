`lib/geometry/view-3d.ts` after Task 1:
- `ROOM_CAMERA = { azimuthDeg: 12, elevationDeg: 20, fovDeg: 62 } as const`. Azimuth is measured in the room round the vertical
  from +X (the tail end at rest) toward +Z.
- `viewerAxes(azimuthDeg, elevationDeg): { right, up, forward }`: `forward` is minus (cos el cos az, sin el, cos el sin az);
  `right` is (sin az, 0, −cos az), straight overhead too; `up` is `right × forward`. `roomCameraAxes()` is
  `viewerAxes(ROOM_CAMERA.azimuthDeg, ROOM_CAMERA.elevationDeg)`, and at azimuth 90° it equals the old camera's formula.
- `cameraAxes(axes = roomCameraAxes())`, `dragTurn(turn, dx, dy, shortSide, axes?)`, `keyTurn(turn, key, axes?)`: a trailing

## Known limitations (left alone on purpose)

- **Shading, not cast shadows.** A strip 6 m long casts almost no sharp shadow, and neither the stand-in nor `RectAreaLight` can cast one. A surface
  turned from a strip darkens (the shadow down a rail); fins don't cast onto the bottom. The floor's soft dark patch under the board is painted.
- **On the tallest windows one strip leaves the picture:** both show on a computer and an iPhone; on a Pixel 7 or at 820 × 800 the left one may sit at the edge. You see it by walking.
- **Pick Up stops at 60°** in the slider and in a drag: the board never stands on end. Every side of it is still reachable, and D-13's "spin in every
  direction" holds for every face.
- **Nose and Tail at 1x are small** (see Questions card 6). The view no longer moves the board toward the camera, because that would take it off its racks
  and out of its light. This changes 16-07's "each view fits the board at 1x", and the fit tests move with it.
- **The first view changes** from 50° above the rail to the view from the tail, so the 3D tile in the ☰ menu no longer matches it. The tile stays as it is.
- **A drag can swing the Roll or Swing thumb from one end to the other** when it crosses ±180°. The board does not jump, only the thumb.
- **A blank's ghost** (off by default) can pass through a rack's pad: it is see-through and stands wider than the board.
- **The frame costs are from a 2019 laptop chip, not a phone.** The founder's iPhone and iPad are checked at the go-live (16-13).
