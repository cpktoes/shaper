<objective>
Let the shaper zoom in on the 3D board and slide it across the window — wheel, pinch, buttons, right-drag or two
fingers, the keyboard — with the same zoom row every other drawing has, and widen the window with Wide view.

The camera never moves: zoom and slide crop the picture the fixed camera takes (`setViewOffset`, research Pattern 6),
so the shading never changes as the board is zoomed or slid. The crop is plain numbers in a new
`components/viewer/crop-zoom.ts`, beside the 2D viewers' `zoom-math.ts` and in the same half steps, with one difference
the contract asks for: the board may be slid at 1x too, as long as its centre stays in the canvas. The 2D viewers' zoom
row is lifted into a shared presentational `ViewerZoomRow` so the 3D canvas wears exactly the same row.

Purpose: R4 (zoom and slide), R10 (the toolbar's words and rules), R12. Decisions D-04, D-10, D-13.
Output: the crop maths and tests, the shared zoom row, the screen's zoom/slide/Wide view handling, the spec, pictures.
</objective>
