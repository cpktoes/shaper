<objective>
On a phone or an iPad, the rack becomes a strip to swipe along, each board turning as it passes the middle.

A finger swipes; a mouse hovers (D-04). The swipe rack is the phone's own sideways scroller, edge to edge, that
settles one board in the middle; every board turns as it passes the middle, following the thumb, and the middle
board's caption sits under the rack. Tapping a board at the side brings it to the middle; tapping the middle board
opens it. On an upright iPhone 14 page, Shape a New Board stays on the first screen; a phone held sideways gives the
rack most of the short screen (D-06); the board being worked on starts in the middle (D-07).

Purpose: R6, R1, R2; D-04, D-06, D-07; UI-SPEC §2, §3, §4, §7, §9, §11; SPEC constraints 5, 6, 7.
Output: `components/setup/swipe-rack.tsx`, the pointer switch in `board-rack.tsx`, the swipe height properties, the phone spec, the D-04 source contract.
