<objective>
Move boards on a computer — drag them, use the ⋯ menu, or the keyboard — and walk the rack without a mouse.

On a computer a shaper presses a board and drags it (into another row too); the others slide over and a mark shows
the gap. ⋯ → Move left / Move right, and Alt + arrow, do the same without dragging. The keyboard walks the whole
rack and opens a board, and a screen reader hears every move. The unsaved board stays first. On phones and iPads the
⋯ menu stays Rename, Duplicate and Delete (D-12) unless the D-11 switch is on.

Purpose: R5, R8, R9; D-12, D-11 (menu side); UI-SPEC §5, §6, §11.
Output: drag and keyboard moves on the hover rack, Move rows in the ⋯ menu, Alt + arrow on both racks, and the desktop and phone specs for them.
</objective>
