its rocker, with its name and dims running up beside it. As the mouse moves along the rack each board turns by its

through the turn maths to the screen, on the practice rack and on the real home page, before anything is expanded:
phones and iPads get their swiping rack in 15-07, and moving boards comes in 15-09 and 15-11 (until 15-07 every
pointer sees this rack).
