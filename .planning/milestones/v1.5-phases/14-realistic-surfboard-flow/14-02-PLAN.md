<objective>
Write the new curve rule and prove it on every blank before anything in the app uses it.

The research found the rule that redraws real blanks most closely: take the square root of each station's lift
above the lowest station (for the bottom), or of its drop below the thickest station (for thickness and width), run
today's curve through those, and square the result back. This plan writes that rule as its own small module and
proves, on every blank in the catalogue, that it reads every printed station exactly, never strays outside the two
stations either side, keeps each blank's lowest point where the catalogue prints it, and — on the hidden US Blanks
stations recorded in the pin — lands closer to the real foam than today's curve. Nothing on screen changes: no
screen uses the new rule until the next wave.

Purpose: R1, R2, R3, R5 (D-13, D-14), acceptance 1, 2 and 8 at the curve level, written as named tests before any
screen moves (SPEC constraint 2).
Output: `lib/geometry/root-curve.ts` and its tests.
</objective>
