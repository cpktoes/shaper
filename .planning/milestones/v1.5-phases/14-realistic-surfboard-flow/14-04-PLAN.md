<objective>
Switch the first board a visitor sees — and every board without a blank — to the new curves.

A hand-set board is five rocker numbers and five thicknesses the shaper types. After this plan the curve drawn
through them uses the same square-root rule as a blank: the founder's "same 5 point root-squares model". The five
numbers themselves do not move by a single bit; the curve between them does (up to about 1/8"), and so the default
board's litres move (about 30.1 to 30.5 L on VOLUME). Today's curve stays available by name for the reports and
pictures, and the app gains one named way to work out a board's figures under a chosen rule, so the reports never
run a second calculation.

Purpose: R4 (D-13), R3 (the deck stays derived), acceptance 8, D-25.
Output: the switch in `board-profile.ts` and `foil.ts`, `summarizeDesignWith` in `design.ts`, the retargeted tests.
</objective>
