<objective>
Make the Summary order form read in the system the shaper chose.

The order form is the two-page sheet a shaper hands over with a board — the front carrying the
drawings and the seven headline dimensions, the back carrying the rail-band marking data and the fin
placement numbers. About half of it already follows the chooser, because it reuses components Phase 6
converted. The other half — the dimension cells, the identification strip, the rail-band thickness
figure and the fin placement panel — still composes its own inch strings, and that is what this plan
closes.

Purpose: a shaper who designed in millimetres hands over a sheet reading in millimetres, so the
numbers on the paper are the numbers they designed with.

Output: every measurement on both sheets following the chooser; a value that will not fit its cell
shrinking rather than clipping; and the Metric print audit that Phase 6 deferred into this phase
actually carried out.
</objective>
