<objective>
Make the Paper Saver read in the system the shaper chose.

The Paper Saver is the narrow-strip print — the one that saves paper by printing only the band of
each page the curve actually crosses, with a registration line at each page join so a shaper can line
the strips up and tape them. Its numbers are the station it is measuring at and the rail half-width
there, plus the same working-mark labels the Full Sized Template carries, plus its own copy of the
calibration square caption.

Purpose: a shaper working off a metric tape reads `914 mm from tail — rail 273 mm` on one line
instead of shifting a decimal point in their head between a centimetre and a millimetre figure.

Output: the registration lines, the mark labels and the calibration caption all following the
chooser; nothing the Paper Saver draws moved; the frozen pins still green on their original digests.
</objective>
