<objective>
Teach the fin model which of its numbers are lengths along the board and which are small marks, then
make the two fin data surfaces read in the shaper's chosen system.

In plain English: on the Fins DATA tab, a fin's distance up from the tail is the kind of number a
shaper quotes in centimetres, while its toe-in and its distance in from the rail are the kind read
off a rule in millimetres — and today the panel prints all of them with one call, because in inches
the difference never mattered. This adds that distinction where each number is worked out, so the
panel can print each one correctly. The McKee toe-aim tables convert too, headings included.

Purpose: this is the one non-obvious trap in the phase. A plan that only converted the fins sidebar
and drawing would ship a DATA tab with every value in the wrong family for at least half its rows.

Output: `FinSummaryRow` and `FinSummaryGroup` carry a family tag, `toeAimTableFor` is system-aware
and returns display-ready strings, and `fin-data-panel.tsx` and `toe-aim-table-modal.tsx` are
converted.
</objective>
