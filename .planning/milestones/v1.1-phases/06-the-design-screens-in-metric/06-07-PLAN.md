<objective>
Finish the Volume screen, then close the phase: prove every design-screen file goes through the
display boundary, and check what the change means for the Summary order form.

In plain English: on Metric the Volume screen's three headline dimensions read in centimetres the
way a shaper quotes a board's size, while the cross-section thicknesses in the card below read in
whole millimetres the way they are measured — the same thickness, said two correct ways. The card's
area and cubic lines convert too, so nothing on the card is left in inches. Litres never change,
and are the same figure on every screen they appear on.

Purpose: this is the last screen, and the plan that turns the conversion ledger from a running
tally into a completed one.

Output: the VOLUME screen converted, the area and volume conversions added to `units.ts`, and the
ledger's closing assertion that every design-screen display file is converted.
</objective>
