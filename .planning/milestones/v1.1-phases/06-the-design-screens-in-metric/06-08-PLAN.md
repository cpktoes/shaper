<objective>
Make every fin PLACEMENT number read in whole millimetres on Metric, and leave every board-size
number on the Fins screen in centimetres.

In plain English: a shaper reading the Fins screen in Metric wants one unit for the numbers they
mark on a blank — a fin sits `279 mm` up from the tail, `30 mm` in from the rail, with `10 mm` of
toe-in and a `114 mm` base. Today the distance up from the tail reads `27.9 cm` while everything
beside it reads in millimetres, so a shaper has to shift the decimal point in their head between
two numbers in the same group. The board's own size — its length and its tail width — keeps
reading in centimetres, because that is how a shaper quotes a board.

This closes UAT gaps G-06-12 ("all fin placement data should be in mm", DATA tab and toe-aim
tables) and G-06-15 ("all fin dims should be mm", the fin drawing). Both have the same root: phase
decision D-01 classified fin off-tail positions as the cm dims family, and plans 06-05 and 06-06
implemented that faithfully at nine sites. The shaper reversed that classification on 2026-09-05
for fin placement numbers only. This is a decision reversal, not a defect — there is no arithmetic
to fix.

Purpose: the fin-placement calculator is one of the two numbers this whole product exists to get
right (PROJECT.md's core value). A shaper has to be able to read its output without converting
anything in their head.

Output: the three Off-Tail rows re-tagged in the fin model, five `formatDim` calls flipped to
`formatMark` across the sidebar and the drawing, the toe-aim tables' aim cells in whole
millimetres under `(mm)` headings, and the superseding rule written into the planning record.
</objective>
