<objective>
Decide, for every blank in the three catalogues, whether it fits this board — and say why not in a
shaper's words.

Building on 11-01's fit check, this plan adds the judging the list needs: the two floors that hide a
blank (too short, too thin at the centre — both from the shaper's settings), the best-placement search
that decides whether a blank fits anywhere along its length and where the slider should land when it is
picked, the "nearest blank that fits" rule for the offer beside a flag, and the search for the nearest
placement that fits for the "Move to Where It Fits" button.

The second half is the wording, written once in a pure file and tested in Imperial and Metric: "1/8"
too thin 12" from the nose", "1/2" too wide at the widepoint", "under 1/16" too thin at the nose tip",
"1/2" toward nose", "centered", the floor sentences ("It's 1 1/2" too short — you've asked for at least
2" of spare length."), the empty-list messages, the list intro and a blank row's meta line.

Purpose: R2, R6, R12, R15; decisions D-04, D-06, D-07, D-08.
Output: judging functions in `lib/geometry/blank-fit.ts`; `lib/geometry/blank-reasons.ts`; tests.
</objective>
