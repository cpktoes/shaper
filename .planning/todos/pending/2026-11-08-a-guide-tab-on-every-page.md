# A GUIDE tab on every design page

## Problem

Sketch 021 gave the planned BOTTOM page a third tab after VIEWER and DATA: GUIDE — one card per cut, each with a cartoon
drawn by the viewer's own code on the board the shaper has open, a sentence on what the cut is and looks like, a sentence on
what it does, and (added 2026-11-08) a short *How to shape* note in the shop's words. The founder's verdict: *"The Guide
looks awesome. Love everything about it. ... I think every page needs a guide like this. Super useful on what each parameter
does to/for the board."*

Today the other pages explain nothing: a shaper meets Wide Point, Nose Rocker, Apex Center, Tuck, Toe-in and Cant as
sliders with hints and nothing more.

## What is needed

A GUIDE tab, in 021's dress, on each of TEMPLATE, ROCKER, RAILS, VOLUME and FINS — one card per control or per named
choice, each with its cartoon drawn from the open board, two sentences (what it is and looks like; what it does to the
board or the ride) and a *How to shape* note. Roughly:

- **TEMPLATE**: length, nose and tail widths, wide point and its place, the tail shapes (squash, round, pin, swallow,
  diamond), wings, and the outline's curve; how a template is drawn, cut and trued.
- **ROCKER**: nose and tail rocker, where each tip's thinning starts, the blank and a custom rocker, the deck; how rocker
  is checked on the blank and planed.
- **RAILS**: the bands, the apex, tuck, hard and soft edges, the finished rail; how each band is marked and cut.
- **VOLUME**: what litres mean for a rider and a wave, and what moves them; how volume is read off a board.
- **FINS**: the setups, distance off the tail and in from the rail, toe-in, cant, base, the trailing fin; how fins are marked
  and set.

The text should stay as brief as 021's (one or two sentences per part) and the cartoons honest (the real board, the real
maths, the viewer's own dashes). The sentences and shop notes are the founder's to write or correct; the sketch's are first
drafts from shaping lore. Build order: a sketch first (one page's GUIDE in 021's pattern, to settle the shape of a control's
card against a cut's), then the pages together or one phase each, after BOTTOM ships.
