Turning Marks Into Rail Bands
Toggle on/off Markings and Bands using the check boxes.
  1. Use Deck Marks (solid lines) as a guide to blend the Deck Bands into the nose and tail (dashed lines) smoothly. The green Deck Mark 3 Center (full board) line is an example of what the band would look like without tapering. 
  2. Use Rail Marks (solid lines) as a guide to blend the rail side of the Deck Bands toward the nose and tail (dashed line).
  3. The Bottom Tuck (solid line) band should blend into a hard edge around 16-22" from the tail (dashed line).
